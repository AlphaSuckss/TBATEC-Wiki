## Talking

Talk to a mentor (use them) and they answer in chat, a line every few seconds. When there is a choice, it shows as <span class="thing">\[buttons\]</span> in chat: click one. Stay within 12 blocks.

Mentors cannot be hurt. Strike one and they won't speak to you for five minutes. Each keeps to their home, one in every Zestier and the two in Epheotus.
