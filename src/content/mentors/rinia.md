## Readings

In her round hut off Zestier's west street. Every visit she shares a <span class="thing">fragment</span> of what she sees round you, free: a hint fitted to where you stand.

A <span class="thing">true reading</span> costs her lifespan, as it did in the novel: she can read how deep a will goes (its phase count, held or in your hand) or find a hidden place.

## The Price

A hidden place: the nearest Djinn Sanctuary, Hearth, Dire Tombs or Beast Lair within 100 chunks, marked on a map she gives you, with its bearing and distance. Nothing found costs nothing.

**Between readings**: 3 days, +1/8 day for every reading she has ever given (6 days at most)<br>**Her eyes**: cloud after 8 readings, blind after 20
