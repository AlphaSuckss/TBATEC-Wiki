## Where

In his barren <span class="thing">crater</span> on the second summit of [Grandus Hold](/epheotus/grandus), by a throne of rough stone. Anyone who reaches Epheotus may talk to him; he trains those through Kordri's first lesson or with Lord Indrath's leave.

Blunt and rumpled, he cares more for his work than for you.

## His Trials

**The golem army**: three waves of his stone golems (4, 6, then 8), as strong as your core, with two of his golems fighting at your side. Five minutes, in his crater (within 40 blocks), without falling.

The first time he gives you an <span class="item">acclorite</span>: better than making one. After that, again whenever you like, for the practice.

## His Lesson

**Cores worth working**: bring him **three Asura-class beast cores** and he teaches you to set cores yourself, at your own smithing table, without a smith's fee ([Your Own Forge](/arms/core_smithing)). Dwarves know it already.

**From the books**: his golem armies against Arthur, the acclorite he made, Dawn's Ballad. **Invented**: the trial's numbers and the smithing lesson.
