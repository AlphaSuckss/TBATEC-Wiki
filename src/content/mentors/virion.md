## Where

In his manor in [Zestier](/sites/zestier), by the fire. In the novel he trained the young Arthur's will in Zestier: the warning, the body's slow assimilation, the awakening, then real fighting until Arthur could keep up with his panther.

Talk to him while you hold a will and he takes you as his <span class="thing">disciple</span>.

## His Lessons

**Mentor bonus**: a disciple's will assimilates x1.5 within 16 blocks of him, meditating or training (not with Sylvia's bonus on top: the better one counts).

Once your will has awakened he has three lessons, in order. They never open a phase for you: Integrate still needs its 100 Insight and Acquire Mastery II.

**Keep up**: with Acquire up, tag him 4 times in 90 s while he leaps from shadow to shadow round his house: +150 Acquire Mastery<br>**Real battle**: defeat 3 worthy foes (AA class and up, or bosses) with Acquire up, then go back to him: +15 Integrate Insight<br>**The panther**: once Integrate is open: in his panther form he fights you; hold Integrate the whole minute and land 6 blows: +150 Integrate Mastery and his compass

## The Panther

His panther form is the novel's Integrate: black skin, black eyes with glowing yellow irises, black hair. Its blows never take you below half a heart. Lessons start over if you take a different will.
