## Where

On the sparring floor of Lord Indrath's training cave, by the aether orb, deep under the arrival peak (see [The Ascent of Geolus](/epheotus/geolus)). He trains only those with Lord Indrath's leave whom Windsom has spoken for.

In the novel he trained Arthur inside the Indrath clan's aether orb, which draws the soul out of the body into a soul realm where time runs about twelve times faster, and where death only throws you back into your body. At first he struck Arthur down in one blow, again and again.

## The Orb

Take the trial he offers in talk, then use the <span class="item">aether orb</span> on its stand on the pool's island (he must be near it). Your soul is drawn into the orb: a floor of pale stone in violet mist, yours alone, with Kordri waiting.

Use the orb again to retry (twice after a while: the first touch asks).

## Your Body

Your body waits by the orb. You go in bare-handed, without effects, at full health; nothing can be broken, placed, dropped or used inside. However you leave (passed, failed, fallen, <span class="thing">\[Leave the orb\]</span>, a logout, the server stopping) you wake where you touched the orb, everything as it was.

## Time and Falls

**Time bends**: the bar shows the orb's clock and your body's, which runs twelve times slower; on waking your effects have lost only your body's time.

A fall costs nothing: his blows land on the soul through any armour, a third of your health each (the third throws you back), as does a step off the floor.

## His Blows

Each blow is wound up in plain sight: a gesture, a creak of leather, dark motes along its line on the floor. Be out of reach or off the line when it comes. He holds back his mana, not his skill: keep away from him and he bursts in.

## The Three Trials

**The body**: 8 bare-handed blows in 90 s. He slips every blow except in the half second after his own (blue sparks, a chime), and answers the others with a quick counter<br>**The eyes**: he throws 16 blows, ever faster (0.6 s down to 0.35 s); evade 14. By the end only his rhythm, read, will save you<br>**The weight**: 6 blows, fist or spell, from within 5 blocks, in 60 s. He can be struck only while he gathers his King's Force (1 s); crouch as it lands, or it throws you toward the edge

## Lessons

Each trial teaches something for good:

**Trust in Your Body**: bare-handed blows +4<br>**Reading the Blow**: 15% chance to slip a melee blow (every 4 s at most)<br>**Bear the Weight**: knockback taken -35%<br>**Thyestes Step (all three)**: a Burst Step leaves the creatures hunting you without you (not bosses; every 8 s)
