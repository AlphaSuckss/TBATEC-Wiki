## Where

At the [arrival shrine](/epheotus/geolus), beside the pad where his column of light sets you down. The Asura Sigil is still how you get to Epheotus; Windsom helps once you are there.

He tells you how the realm works and who may enter the castle, and can send you home to Dicathen at once.

## Guidance

**Travel**: his column of golden light sets you down at the foot gate, the golden gate (never past the [Indrath ward](/epheotus/indrath)), Everburn, Lady Myre's cottage, or the arrival of any of the seven domains. Once every 2 minutes.

**Kordri**: with Lord Indrath's leave (an Invitation of Indrath in your name, or as a grown dragon) he speaks for you to Kordri, then can take you to the training cave.
