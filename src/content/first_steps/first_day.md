**What to do first**

1. Choose a heritage. If the mana gives you a core, choose where you were raised.<br>2. You wake among your own people. **Use** the people there: they trade, guide and teach.<br>3. Open the Character screen (<kbd title="Character Screen">I</kbd>). **Workshop** shapes spells; cast with <kbd title="Cast Selected Spell">R</kbd>.<br>4. Fight mana beasts and spend mana: both grow your core.<br>5. Follow the tips the game gives you as you go (<code>/tbatectips off</code> stops them).

The first time you join, the <span class="thing">heritage menu</span> opens. Nothing can hurt you until you have chosen, and if you close it, it opens again five seconds later.

Pick **Human**, **Elf** or **Dwarf**, or **Feeling Lucky?** to let the mana choose your race as well.

## The Reveal

Then the mana decides the rest, and a reveal shows you what you got: whether a <span class="thing">mana core</span> awakened, your <span class="thing">class</span>, your <span class="thing">elements</span> and any rare <span class="thing">gift</span>.

What each race is born with is in [Heritage](/heritage/choosing).

## If You Have a Core

With a core, open the Character screen (<kbd title="Character Screen">I</kbd>) and press **Workshop**, or use the mana-core button in your inventory, to open the [Spell Workshop](/spellcraft/workshop) and shape your first spell. Drag it into the loadout bar, then press <kbd title="Cast Selected Spell">R</kbd> to cast it.

Every point of mana you spend [refines](/mana_core/refinement) your core toward its next rank.

## If You Do Not

A human without a core is a hardened warrior instead: +2 hearts and +1 attack damage. Anyone can bond a [companion](/companions/contracts), ride it, trade with the [Quest Master](/peoples/sapin) and travel the gates with a [Teleportation Crest](/items/teleportation_crest).

## Where You Wake

You wake where your people live: an elf in Zestier, a dwarf in Vildorial, a human of Dicathen in a town of Sapin, one raised in Alacrya in Maerin, an asura on its clan's ground. That place is where you wake again.

**Alacrya** is a continent of its own, across the ocean from Dicathen: in the game a separate land, reached by a crossing, never by walking.

## Where to Go

The world's spawn lies in the plains of [Sapin](/lands/sapin). The elven forest of [Elshire](/lands/elshire) is to the north, the dwarven wastes of [Darv](/lands/darv) to the south, and the [Beast Glades](/lands/beast_glades) to the east, where mana beasts grow stronger the deeper you go.

Hunt them for their [cores](/items/beast_cores): the fastest way to grow your own.

## This Book

You are given this codex once, the first time you join. If you lose it, craft another from a book and an amethyst shard.
