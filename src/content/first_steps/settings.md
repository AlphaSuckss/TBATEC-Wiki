Most of the mod's numbers live in the server config <span class="thing">tbatec-mana-progression.toml</span>. Some worth knowing:

**origins**: every race's core chance, element odds, gift odds and Random odds, and the list of wraith players<br>**breakthroughTrials**: the advancements each breakthrough needs

**world**: how much each beast core refines; the core needed to enter Epheotus (Black: anyone); the rebirth crystal's cooldown; Elshire's mist; where the kingdoms' borders and the Beast Glades lie<br>**environment**: whether spells change the world: fire starting fires, glass shattering, strong spells breaking the ground<br>**world.settlementPopulation, settlementCrowd**: how many people the cities hold (1.5: half again what was built), and the crowd near one spot past which no extra people are called (70)<br>**spellCaps, refinement, mastery, combat**: the numbers behind every spell

## Client Settings

Each player's own <span class="thing">tbatec-client.toml</span> sets particle density and limits, screen shake, post-processing effects and light pools, and the wraith's Super Jump.

**combat**: your attack animations by weapon (playerAnimations), your own hand following each cut as you see it through your eyes (firstPersonSwings), the ribbon a blade leaves (weaponTrails).
