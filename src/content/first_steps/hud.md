The <span class="thing">mana orb</span> sits in the bottom-left corner once you have a core. It fills with your mana, takes the colour of your core and glows brighter with each stage. The numbers under it are your current and maximum mana.

To move it, size it or hide it, open the Character screen and press **HUD**: drag the orb, turn the wheel over it.

## Spell Selector

The <span class="thing">spell selector</span> in the bottom-right corner shows the selected spell with two neighbours on each side: icon, name, and a sweep while it cools down. A toggle that is up has a bright outline, and a conjurer's slot fills with pale light while it gathers mana.

Under it is the spell's mana cost, red when you can't afford it. When more than one preset holds spells, its numeral (I, II, III) is shown too.

## Everything Else

Riding a companion shows its two moves and their cooldowns.

While you channel a Crystal of Rebirth or an Asura Sigil a ring fills round the crosshair and light wells in from the corners of the view, and the screen fades as a gate carries you. A wraith has its own bar of powers and timers.
