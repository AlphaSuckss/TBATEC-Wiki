Every key below can be changed under <span class="thing">Options &gt; Controls &gt; Key Binds</span>, in the four TBATEC sections. Keys for powers you do not have are not listed there. This page shows your current bindings.

**<kbd title="Character Screen">I</kbd>**: the Character screen. Its buttons open the Spell Workshop (which has no key of its own unless you bind one) and the HUD layout.<br>**<kbd title="Previous Spell">\[</kbd> / <kbd title="Next Spell">\]</kbd>**: select the previous or next spell

## Casting

**<kbd title="Cast Selected Spell">R</kbd>**: tap to cast. Hold it to channel a Beam, Cone or Circle that has a hold time. Press it on a toggle that is up (Iron Skin, Body Augmentation) to drop it.

**<kbd title="Cycle Loadout Preset">\</kbd>**: switch between the three loadout presets, I, II and III.

**<kbd title="Mana Flight / Hover">O</kbd>**: switch mana flight on or off; in the air, glide or hover (White core). See [Mana Flight](/mana_core/flight).

## Companions

**<kbd title="Mount Ability 1">Z</kbd> / <kbd title="Mount Ability 2">V</kbd>**: while riding a companion, use its first and second moves.<br>**<kbd title="Companion Command / Whistle">H</kbd>**: look at your companion to cycle Follow, Stay and Guard; press it anywhere else to whistle every companion within 48 blocks back to your side.<br>**<kbd title="Sneak">Shift</kbd> + use**: on your companion opens its screen.

## Wraiths

**<kbd title="Spiritual Pressure (Wraith)">N</kbd>**: Spiritual Pressure<br>**<kbd title="Projection (Wraith)">B</kbd>**: Projection<br>**<kbd title="Sonido (Wraith)">Y</kbd>**: Sonido<br>**<kbd title="Super Jump on/off (Wraith)">J</kbd>**: Super Jump on or off
