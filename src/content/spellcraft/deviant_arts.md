A <span class="thing">deviant art</span> is an element pushed past itself. Arts are not born: they **awaken through practice**. Every point of mana you spend on a parent element's spells counts toward its arts (your race's training speed applies), and once you pass the threshold the art is yours for good.

## Awakening an Art

Your race decides which arts can awaken, and how much practice each takes:<br>**Humans**: Ice, Lightning 5,000; Sound 8,000; Metal 12,000; Gravity 15,000<br>**Elves**: Plant 4,000; Sound 7,000<br>**Dwarves**: Metal 1,500; Magma 9,000; Gravity 15,000<br>You also need a parent element. Magma is earth and fire fused, so it needs both; a dwarf can always bend metal.

## Using an Art

Hover your mastery in the workshop to see how far each art has awakened.

An awakened art can be layered onto your ordinary spells of its element (the last chip beside the modifiers): it recolours them and gives their hits its own effect. Its signature spells carry a stronger version.
