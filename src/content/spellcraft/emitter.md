A gift of healing, only ever born: 3% of human mages are Emitters. Its spell has its own tab in the workshop.

**Mending** <span class="dim">Black</span><br>Heals the ally you aim at, or every ally within 4 blocks. From 8 power it also cures them of poison and wither.

An emitter mends others far better than themselves: on your own body Mending only closes surface wounds, a quarter of the healing and two hearts at most a cast, with no cure.
