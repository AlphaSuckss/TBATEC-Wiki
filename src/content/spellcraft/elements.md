Every hit of an elemental spell carries its element's effect, lasting 2 seconds and more with power (up to 8):<br>**Fire**: sets the target alight.<br>**Earth / Water**: Slowness / Weakness, a level stronger per 12 power (to III).<br>**Wind**: knocks back and lifts, more with power.

## Domes

A deviant or asuran art layered onto a spell replaces its element's effect with its own.

**Domes**: fire and water domes hold twice as well against fire, wind domes against missiles (which they turn back rather than stop), and earth domes 1.5 times against explosions and half as much again overall. A fire dome burns whoever strikes you through it; a water dome puts you out; a wind dome takes most of the knockback.

## Walls

**Walls** hold too, and are worn down the same way: earth walls hold half as much again, wind walls a third less. A missile or spell bigger than what a wall has left breaks it and goes on; a beam playing on a wall wears it down pulse by pulse.
