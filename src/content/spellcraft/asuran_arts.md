Each asuran race casts every common spell form through its art, and has signature arts no one else can cast: its clan's techniques from the books, and a few of ours. All need a White core, which every asura is born with.

An art's hits carry its own effect in place of an element's. Through its art an asura's spells hit 50% harder.

## Dragon: Aether

*Hits slow (III) and tire. An aether art strikes a quarter harder; the rest is the dragons' pure mana, pale where aether is violet.*

**Dragon's Breath**: A colossal beam of pure mana that follows your gaze for over a second. You are slowed while you breathe. Every dragon has it.

**Aevum Stasis**: Time stops in a sphere: creatures freeze in place and missiles hang in the air. When it snaps back, everything inside is hurt. Aether of the Aevum branch: only for a dragon it answers there.

**Static Void**: Time stops for everything around you. Creatures freeze mid-step and missiles hang in the air until the void lifts. *Sylvia; Kezess Indrath; Arthur through Sylvia's will, ch. 9 and 57.*

**Realmheart**: Gold runes burn on your arms and your eyes see every particle of mana: hidden foes are revealed, and spells and missiles aimed at you fall apart. *The Indrath clan; Arthur through Sylvia's will, ch. 32.*

**King's Force**: The will of a grown asura laid on everything around you: lesser creatures break and flee, and the rest are slowed and weakened under its weight. It harms nothing by itself. A grown asura who holds the suppression key releases the same force for as long as it is held. *Kezess Indrath and the Indrath dragons, Sylvie, ch. 107 (the pantheons have their own, after Kordri).*

**Spatium Step**: Fold space and step through the fold: you arrive where you looked, even through solid rock. *Not named in the books: ours, after the spatium of the Indrath (Sylvia, Windsom), ch. 118 and 248.*

**Solar Judgement**: A column of pure mana and aether with the heat and light of the sun comes down on the aimed spot. *Kezess Indrath, ch. 520.*

**Crystallization**: Condenses air, time and space round the aimed creature into a pearlescent crystal coffin - then crushes it. *Kezess Indrath, ch. 520.*

**Silver Spear**: Summons a long spear of silver pure mana and hurls it through everything in its path. *Windsom Indrath.*

**Vivum Mending**: Aether of existence knits you and every ally near you whole, closing wounds no emitter could. *Not named in the books: ours, after Lady Myre Indrath, ch. 117.*

**Aevum Acceleration**: Speed up time for your own body: you move and strike faster than anything around you can follow. *Not named in the books: ours, after Sylvie Leywin, ch. 443.*

**Pure Mana Blast**: Shapes raw, unattuned mana into blasts and looses them in a stream. *Sylvie, ch. 221.*

## Basilisk: Decay

*Hits wither and weaken, and you heal 15% of the damage.*

**Soulfire**: A cone of black fire. What burns keeps burning and withering for the whole duration, even in water, and while it burns it cannot cast and gets no mana back. Sneak and cast again to put your fire out.

**Withering Rot**: A pool of rot that withers, weakens and starves those in it, eats their armour, and heals you.

**Void Wind**: Black wind that erodes whatever it touches, loosed as a fan of scythes. *Cadell, Seris, Melzri, Lyra Dreide, ch. 371.*

**Blood Iron**: Spikes of black iron pierce up out of the shadows under your enemies, laced with a poison that stops wounds closing. Cast while sneaking, it is one great spike under the one enemy you aim at, far stronger. *Uto, Nico, Dragoth, Cadell, ch. 175.*

**Bile Water**: A grey, poisonous haze rolls over the aimed area, corroding flesh and driving those who breathe it mad. *Jagrette, Richmal, ch. 155.*

**Black Lightning**: Blackish-purple lightning that leaps from foe to foe. *The Wraiths Perhata and Ulrike, ch. 389.*

**Shadow Meld**: Melt into the shadows and rise from them elsewhere, unseen. *Not named in the books: ours, after Mawar, Valeska, Agrona, ch. 474.*

**Blood Iron Golems**: Builds golems of black blood iron that hunt your enemies. Choose how many rise: they share the spell's strength. They are real and can be broken; their health and blows grow with the power of the spell. Use one to order them all (follow, stay, stand down); sneak and use to dismiss them. *The Wraiths Ifiok and Perhata, ch. 494.*

## Pantheon: Force

*Hits throw targets back, stagger them and break shields.*

**Crushing Force**: After a heartbeat's warning a weight of mana falls on the aimed spot: it slams down everything under it, pins it and breaks shields. Ours; King's Force itself is an aura, below.

**Panoptic Gaze**: Extra eyes open on the sides of your head and lay everything around you bare: it glows, and every enemy you can see takes 35% more damage from all sources.

**World Eater**: Compress mana until it bursts, then loose it as a golden beam: the chain reaction rips along its whole path. It costs a share of all your mana, nearly half at its weakest and most of it at full power. The forbidden art of the Thyestes. *Aldir Thyestes, ch. 321 and 325.*

**Mana Feedback**: A touch sends a pulse of mana into every cell of the aimed creature, overloading its senses: it drops where it stands, asleep for a few seconds with its mana sealed. It does almost no harm. *Aldir Thyestes, ch. 377.*

**Mana Disruption**: A raised hand and a twist of the wrist scrambles the mana around you: enemy spells unravel and casters are sealed. It does no harm of its own; more power only carries it further. *Taci Thyestes, ch. 378.*

**Void Orb**: A sphere of light-swallowing nothingness opens at the aimed spot, dragging everything toward it and swallowing missiles whole. *Taci Thyestes, ch. 380.*

**Third Eye Beam**: Open the third eye and burn a thin ray of purple force through what it looks upon: what it strikes is weakened and marked, seen through walls for a while. *Not named in the books: ours, after Aldir Thyestes.*

**True Release**: The pantheon's true form shows through without your growing into it: a second pair of arms, more eyes, and hardened golden scales that heal the wounds beneath them. *Not named in the books: ours, after Taci Thyestes, ch. 380.*

## Phoenix: Phoenix Fire

*Hits burn for 8 seconds, and you heal 10% of the damage.*

**Sunfire Nova**: A small sun bursts around you, searing foes and healing you and friendly players.

**Phoenix Dive**: Rise on wings of flame, then dive like a falling star onto the aimed ground.

**Domain of Flame**: A great dome of your own bright orange fire mana closes over the space around you: it pushes out the mana of others, burning and sealing your enemies while you and your allies grow stronger inside it. *Mordain Asclepius, ch. 458.*

**Cleansing Flame**: A warm, healing fire washes over you and your allies, burning every affliction away - and searing the enemies around you. *Not named in the books: ours, after Soleil, ch. 500.*

**Flame Bow**: Draw a bow of fire and loose arrow after arrow of flame. *Not named in the books: ours, after Naesia Avignis, ch. 502.*

**Rain of Fire**: Fire rains from the heavens over the aimed area, as the phoenixes rained it on Taegrin Caelum. *Not named in the books: ours, after the phoenixes of Asclepius.*

**Phoenix Rebirth**: While it lasts, a killing blow burns away instead: you rise from your ashes with half your health and a wave of fire. *Mordain Asclepius, ch. 425.*

**Phoenix Mantle**: Wrap yourself in fire hot enough to burn an asura that comes too close. *Not named in the books: ours, after Chul Asclepius, ch. 425.*

## Titan: Titan Earth

*Hits slow and weaken, and lift a little.*

**Titan's Fist**: A stone fist erupts under the aimed spot and hurls everything skyward.

**Stone Golems**: Shape the earth into golems that march on your enemies - simple warriors. Choose how many rise: they share the spell's strength, so one is a towering titan and ten are a small, quick swarm. They are real and can be broken; their health and blows grow with the power of the spell. Use one to order them all (follow, stay, stand down); sneak and use to dismiss them. *Wren Kain IV, ch. 121.*

**Earthen Maze**: Walls of stone heave up in a ring round the aimed spot, shutting whatever is inside in. *Not named in the books: ours, after Wren Kain IV, ch. 473.*

**Titanic Growth**: Grow to a titan's size: you reach further, hit harder and stand against any blow. *Radix Grandus, ch. 486.*

**Forged Arsenal**: Swords of earth form in the blink of an eye and fly at your enemies. *Not named in the books: ours, after Wren Kain IV, ch. 121.*

## Hamadryad: Living Wood

*Hits root and poison.*

**Verdant Grove**: A grove around you heals you and friendly players and cures poison, while it roots and poisons your enemies.

**Ironwood Spears**: A line of ironwood stakes bursts from the ground: impaled, rooted and launched.

**Rootbind**: Living roots surge up and bind the aimed creature and its companions. *Not named in the books: ours, after the Mapellia clan.*

**Sap of Life**: Healing sap wells up from the ground round you, mending you and your allies. *Not named in the books: ours, after Lady Morwenna Mapellia, ch. 505.*

**Living Bark**: Bark grows over your skin like armour; thorns on it bite whatever presses close. *Not named in the books: ours, after the Mapellia clan.*

**Briar Field**: A thicket of briars bursts up across the aimed ground, tearing and snaring whatever walks in it. *Not named in the books: ours, after the Mapellia clan.*

## Leviathan: Leviathan Tide

*Hits steal breath, slow, and douse flames.*

**Tidal Wave**: A towering wave rolls forward and carries everything along.

**Abyssal Prison**: The aimed enemy is held in a sphere of crushing deep water: rooted, drowning and slowed.

**Torrent Gate**: Wrap yourself in a torrent of water and vanish, to step out of it wherever you were looking. *Not named in the books: ours, after Lord Veruhn Eccleiah.*

**Echo of the Tide**: Listen to the echoes on the waves: you see the blows coming before they land, and every creature near you is revealed. *Not named in the books: ours, after Lord Veruhn Eccleiah, ch. 456.*

**Leviathan's Grasp**: A sea serpent of water bursts from you and coils through your foes, dragging them under. *Not named in the books: ours, after the leviathans' true form.*

**Abyssal Pressure**: The crushing weight of the deep sea falls on the aimed spot. *Not named in the books: ours, after the Eccleiah clan.*

**Twin Tide Blades**: Two blades of water fly out on their own, cutting through everything they pass. *Not named in the books: ours, after Zelyna Eccleiah.*

## Sylph: Sylph Gale

*Hits lift and push.*

**Skyward Gale**: Hurl yourself into the sky, falling slowly after, and blast the enemies around you away.

**Cyclone**: A cyclone marches forward, lifting and spinning everything it catches.

**Cloud Step**: Step up onto the wind as if it were a cloud and drift on it. *Not named in the books: ours, after the Aerind clan.*

**Cloud Veil**: A bank of cloud settles over the aimed area: inside it you and your allies cannot be seen, and your enemies cannot see. *Not named in the books: ours, after the Aerind clan.*

**Gale Scythes**: A fan of scything blades of wind. *Not named in the books: ours, after the Aerind clan.*

**Cloud Form**: Your body grows light as cloud: you fall softly, move swiftly and missiles slip past you. *Not named in the books: ours, after the Aerind clan.*

Skyward Gale and Cyclone also grant a [Wind Ward](/items/wind_ward_charm). The asura themselves are in [Heritage](/heritage/asura).
