## From the Books

Every water spell named in the books has its place in the Water school, each with its own shape. A few the books only show are ours, marked so.

Water Bullet is the **Bolt**, Aqua Fist the **Mana-Infused Strike**, Water Barrier the **Dome** and **Wall**, and Absolute Zero is the Ice art's own.

**Aqua Cannon** <span class="dim">Black; augmenters Silver</span><br>Fires a ball of pressurised water that slams its target back.<br>*An examinee at Xyrus, ch. 29.*

**Water Cannon** <span class="dim">Orange; augmenters Silver</span><br>A sphere of pressurised water three metres across, growing as it nears its target and bursting over everything there.<br>*Damien, ch. 34.*

**Water Serpent** <span class="dim">Yellow; augmenters Silver</span><br>A gigantic snake of water surges out from you, winding toward your foes and crashing through all it meets.<br>*Feyrith Ivsaar III, ch. 52.*

**Flood Domain** <span class="dim">Yellow; augmenters Silver</span><br>Floods the ground around you knee-deep with water: enemies wade slow and weak, while you and your allies move freely.<br>*Feyrith Ivsaar III, ch. 52.*

**Siren's Howl** <span class="dim">Orange; augmenters Silver</span><br>A great pillar of water erupts under the aimed spot, strong enough to throw back a cavern worm.<br>*Diane Whitehall and Samantha Tempest, ch. 29 and 34.*

**Aqua Siphon** <span class="dim">Red; augmenters Orange</span><br>Draws the water out of everything around you: living things wither as it is pulled from them, and it mends you.<br>*Samantha Tempest, ch. 34.*

**Sunken Tomb** <span class="dim">Yellow; augmenters Silver</span><br>Seals you inside a thick barrier of dense water. You cannot move, but almost nothing can reach you.<br>*Arthur Leywin, ch. 329.*

**Water Whip** <span class="dim">Red; augmenters Silver</span><br>Lashes out with a whip of water that strikes everything along it and drags the first in toward you.<br>*Roland (Widow's Crypt), ch. 66.*

**Heaven-Piercing Trident** <span class="dim">Orange; augmenters Silver</span><br>Hurls a trident of water that pierces through everything in its path.<br>*Jamiel Glayder (webcomic).*
