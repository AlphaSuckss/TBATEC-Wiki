Every element can be cast in these shapes. Each line gives the core a shape needs, and where a class needs more, that class's colour too (see [Augmenters and Conjurers](/mana_core/classes)).

**Bolt** <span class="dim">Black; augmenters Silver</span><br>A projectile that flies to the point you aim at.

**Arrow** <span class="dim">Black; augmenters Silver</span><br>A swift arrow along your gaze that pierces everything in its path.

**Beam** <span class="dim">Black; augmenters Silver</span><br>Strikes everything along a line twice a second. Hold to channel it, or quick-cast a single pulse.

**Cone** <span class="dim">Red; augmenters Orange</span><br>A spray of your element in front of you, pulsing each second while you hold it.

**Circle** <span class="dim">Red; augmenters Orange</span><br>A ring that spreads out from you to its range, hitting each target once. It can be held, too.

**Eruption** <span class="dim">Red; augmenters Silver</span><br>A line of eruptions tears out of the ground ahead and hurls targets up (wind highest).

**Wall** <span class="dim">Orange; augmenters Silver</span><br>A wall up to 12 wide and 5 tall. It stops enemy missiles and spells, each wearing it down (it holds 6 damage per power) until one bigger than what is left breaks through; enemies who push through it are hurt, at most once a second.

**Trap** <span class="dim">Orange; augmenters Silver</span><br>A rune on top of a block. It arms after a second and a half and strikes the first enemy to step on it.

**Meteor** <span class="dim">Orange; augmenters Silver</span><br>Falls from high above onto the aimed spot: a blast up to 8 blocks wide, strongest at the centre, that throws targets back.

**Dome** <span class="dim">Yellow; augmenters Silver</span><br>A dome of your element around you, 2.5 to 5 blocks across the middle, that moves with you. It stops enemy missiles where they cross it (protecting whoever stands inside) and soaks the blows that reach you, 3 damage per power, until a hit bigger than what is left overpowers it.

**Lance** <span class="dim">Yellow; augmenters Silver</span><br>A heavy lance that punches through its targets.

**Volley** <span class="dim">Silver</span><br>A fan of bolts behind you, loosed one after another over its duration: more bolts with more power, up to 64.

**Vortex** <span class="dim">Silver</span><br>A whirl that drags in everything near it and grinds what it holds. Wind lifts them, too.
