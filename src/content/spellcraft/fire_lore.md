## From the Books

Every fire spell named in the books has its place in the Fire school, each with its own shape. A few the books only show are ours, marked so.

Fireball is the **Bolt**, Fire Fist the **Mana-Infused Strike**, Heatwave Barrier the **Dome** and **Wall**, and Flame Pillar and Soulfire are spells of their own.

**Fire Spark** <span class="dim">Black; augmenters Silver</span><br>Flicks a fast spark of fire from your fingertip that bursts into a small blaze where it lands.<br>*Arthur Leywin, ch. 26.*

**Ember Wisps** <span class="dim">Red; augmenters Silver</span><br>A cloud of small fireballs gathers around you and rains down on what you aim at, one after another.<br>*Lucas Wykes and Professor Geist, ch. 29 and 57.*

**Fire Cyclone** <span class="dim">Orange; augmenters Silver</span><br>A twister of fire swirls up on the aimed spot, drawing in and burning everything inside it for as long as it lasts.<br>*Lucas Wykes, ch. 33.*

**Fire Fall** <span class="dim">Yellow; augmenters Silver</span><br>A shower of fire pours from the sky over the aimed area, setting everything below alight.<br>*Lucas Wykes, ch. 57.*

**Inferno's Cage** <span class="dim">Yellow; augmenters Silver</span><br>A dome of fire closes over the aimed spot. Whatever is caught inside burns, and is thrown back whenever it tries to leave.<br>*Lucas Wykes, ch. 57.*
