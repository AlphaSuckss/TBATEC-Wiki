## The Body's Arts

Raw mana worked through the body, with no element: the augmenter's own school from Black. Conjurers reach it at White; asura may use it too. Mana-Infused Strike and Blade Shroud take on your <span class="thing">affinity</span>, the element you have mastered most.

**Burst Step** <span class="dim">Black</span><br>Driven along your view in a few ticks, far faster than any dash: six blocks, or as far as your core lets you set (10 at Red, 40 at White), then a moment of speed and lift. A wall stops you.

**Mana-Infused Strike** <span class="dim">Black</span><br>For its duration every melee blow deals +50% plus 8% per power, more slowly past 4 (up to +130%), and knocks back, with your affinity: fire burns, water chills, earth staggers, wind flings.

**Body Augmentation** <span class="dim">Black</span> toggle<br>Mana poured into every muscle: attack, speed, armour, attack speed, footing, jump and health. Your core sets full strength (at Black +4 attack, 12% speed, +2 hearts; at White +13 attack, 40% speed, +20 armour, +14 hearts); the Strength slider how much of it you hold.<br>Your mana refills under it: at 60% it holds as long as your mana keeps up (on the move, without Mana Rotation, a slow drain), and more costs steeply more. Its extra hearts fill from mana as it rises and regrow from mana. Tap <kbd title="Body Augmentation (tap) / Iron Skin (hold); with sneak: set their strength; the aether arts once reborn">B</kbd> to raise or drop it, hold it for Iron Skin: your own from any loadout, or a ready-made one. With sneak, the same tap or hold steps the ready-made one's strength instead: 20, 40, 60, 80, 100%.

## Unseen

**Mirage Walk** <span class="dim">Orange</span><br>Kordri's technique: every ripple of your mana stilled, you vanish. Anything hunting you within 32 blocks loses you, and nothing can pick you out beyond a couple of blocks. It hides what you hold and wear, too.

## A Clan's Arts

Some arts belong to a clan of the asura, and someone of another people must be <span class="thing">taught</span> them: **Mirage Walk** (anyone) and **Flash Strike** (other asura) by the pantheons, **Blade Shroud** by the sylphs and **Overdrive** by the phoenixes (other asura). A clan's own need no teacher.

To be taught, sneak and use an asura of that clan while holding a beast core of S grade or better: they keep the core and show you the clan's arts. [Kordri's](/mentors/kordri) graduates have Mirage Walk already. <code>\[who teaches what follows the books; the price is invented\]</code>

**Iron Skin** <span class="dim">Red</span> toggle<br>A faint white layer that cuts physical, missile and blast damage, and three fifths as much of a spell. Its slider sets how much: up to 50% at Red, 85% at White.

Nearer your core's limit it costs steeply more. Iron Skin and Body Augmentation stack; with both at their ready-made strengths a full pool lasts two to three minutes on the move (six at White). A greater hardening an art lays on you (True Release) does not stack with Iron Skin: the stronger of the two counts.

**Blade Shroud** <span class="dim">Yellow</span><br>Edges of your affinity along your arms and blade: every swing, landing or not, sweeps them through a wide arc in front of you (3 to 6 blocks) and cuts every enemy in it. It never cuts on its own.

**Shockwave Slam** <span class="dim">Yellow</span><br>Leap and drive down. Everything around the landing is hit hard and hurled back; you take no fall damage.

**Flash Strike** <span class="dim">Silver</span><br>You are behind the enemy in your sights, with a blow of a fifth again your attack, plus the spell's power, already landing.

**Overdrive** <span class="dim">Silver</span>, asura only<br>The Asuran Body State, which no lesser body can enter. Ailments cleansed; for 15 seconds or more, far faster, quicker to strike, much harder hitting, tougher, and nothing harmful or any knockback can touch you. When it ends, the body pays: slowed, weakened and tired.

**Burst Strike** <span class="dim">Orange; conjurers White</span><br>Burst Step's principle in the arm: your muscles fire all at once and the blow lands before it can be seen.<br>*Arthur Leywin, ch. 465.*
