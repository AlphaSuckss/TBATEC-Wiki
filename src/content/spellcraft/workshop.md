Open the Character screen (<kbd title="Character Screen">I</kbd>) and press **Workshop**, or use the mana-core button in your inventory, to open the <span class="thing">Spell Workshop</span>, where you design your own spells. It needs a mana core.

Name the spell (up to 40 letters, or leave it blank for a fitting name), then pick a <span class="thing">school</span> and a <span class="thing">shape</span>.

## Schools

Schools: **Pure Mana** and each element you hold. Once a deviant art awakens, a **Deviant Arts** tab holds the spells of every art you have. An [Emitter](/spellcraft/emitter) or [Diviner](/spellcraft/diviner) also gets a tab for the gift. Schools you can't use are greyed out. An asura sees Pure Mana and its own art.

Beside the school is your [mastery](/spellcraft/mastery) of it; hover it to see how close your deviant arts are to awakening.

## Shape and Sliders

The shape list shows every shape of the school. One your core can't hold yet is badged with the colour it needs; hover any shape to read what it does and when each class can learn it.

Then set the sliders: <span class="thing">power</span>, <span class="thing">range</span>, <span class="thing">speed</span>, <span class="thing">duration</span> and <span class="thing">cooldown</span>. For Beam, Cone and Circle the duration is the longest you can hold it; 0 makes it a quick cast.

A slider that opens at your next rank shows locked, with the rank's name. Hover a slider to read what it does for that shape. Summoning spells add <span class="thing">amount</span>: how many rise, sharing the spell's strength.

**Tint**: a six-digit hex colour recolours the whole spell; leave it empty for your element's colours.

**Modifiers**: the chips under the sliders add [Homing, Burst or Split](/spellcraft/modifiers), and the last chip layers one of your [deviant arts](/spellcraft/deviant_arts) onto the spell.

## Preview

The preview shows exactly how the spell will look. Drag to turn it; Replay plays it again and the dummy button sets up a target.

Under it is the <span class="thing">mana cost</span> (with upkeep per second for spells you hold or keep up), already lowered by your mastery, and your core's refinement bar.

## Icon and Library

Click the icon slot to pick a preset icon, right-click it for an automatic one, or drag any item from your inventory onto it.

Save, and the spell joins your <span class="thing">library</span> on the right (up to 64 spells). Click a spell to edit it; right-click it to duplicate or delete it; the filter chip shows one school at a time.

## Loadout

Drag a spell, or shift-click it, into the <span class="thing">loadout</span> bar at the bottom (nine slots). Drag to reorder; right-click to remove.

Buttons I, II and III hold three separate loadouts. Switching preset, here or with <kbd title="Cycle Loadout Preset">\</kbd>, puts the new preset's spells on at least a second's cooldown.
