## Ambient Mana

Spells drawn from the mana around you, a conjurer's from Red.

**Flame Spear / Ice Dart** <span class="dim">Red; augmenters Silver</span> fire or water<br>A fast, piercing shard. The spear sets what it passes through burning; the dart freezes and slows it.

**Wind Gust** <span class="dim">Red; augmenters Orange</span> wind<br>The half circle in front of you fills with gale. Every creature in it is thrown back (as far as the range), and missiles are turned around; arrows become yours.

**Earth Bastion** <span class="dim">Orange; augmenters Silver</span> earth<br>A 3x3 stone wall rises just ahead. It pushes creatures out, stops missiles and blows through it, and holds 5 damage per power before it crumbles.

**Flame Pillar / Blizzard Field** <span class="dim">Yellow; augmenters Silver</span> fire or water<br>A 4x4 patch of ground burns or freezes for 6 seconds (longer from Yellow), hurting and slowing all who stand in it.
