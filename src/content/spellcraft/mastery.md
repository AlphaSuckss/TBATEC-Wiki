Each element you cast grows your <span class="thing">mastery</span> of it, from 0 to 100, shown beside the school selector. Mastery makes that element's spells stronger and cheaper: at full mastery, a quarter more power for a quarter less mana.

Full mastery takes 25,000 practice: about 20,000 mana for a human, less for a dwarf in fire or earth, still less for an asura.

Mastery you neglect fades a little whenever you train another element.

Your most-mastered element is your <span class="thing">affinity</span>: the colour of your flight trail and the edge of your Pure Mana arts.
