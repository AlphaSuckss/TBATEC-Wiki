Bolt, Arrow and Lance take all three modifiers; Volley takes Homing and Burst.

**Homing**: (+25% mana) locks on to the enemy most squarely in front of you and leads it as it moves.

**Burst**: (+35%) explodes where it lands, 2 to 4.5 blocks wide, for half its damage.

**Split**: (+30%) three projectiles in a fan, each at 60% power.
