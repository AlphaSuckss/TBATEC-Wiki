Techniques beyond the common spell forms. Most take on the colour and the hit of the element you cast them with (a fire Mana Tether burns, a water one weakens) but some belong to one element alone. All are ranged spells, so augmenters reach them at Silver.

**Elemental Prison** <span class="dim">Red</span><br>Seals the aimed enemy in a cage of your element: it can't move, and takes damage every second.

**Elemental Orbs** <span class="dim">Orange</span><br>Two to six orbs circle you. Every half second one fires a homing bolt at the nearest enemy in sight.

**Mana Tether** <span class="dim">Black</span><br>Yanks the aimed enemy to you and slows it. Aim at a block, or at a beast far larger than you, and you are reeled to it instead.

**Mana Brand** <span class="dim">Orange</span> <code>fire only</code><br>A burning sigil on the target. It detonates when its fuse runs out or the bearer dies, harder the longer it burned, and splashes those nearby.

**Rending Fissure** <span class="dim">Red</span> earth or magma<br>The ground splits in a racing line, hurling up everything it passes under.

Chain Arc and Thunderstorm are [Lightning](/spellcraft/lightning) arts, and Mirror Ward a [Sound](/spellcraft/sound) art.
