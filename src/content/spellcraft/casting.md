&#8203;<kbd title="Previous Spell">\[</kbd> and <kbd title="Next Spell">\]</kbd> move through your loadout, and <kbd title="Cast Selected Spell">R</kbd> casts the selected spell.

**Tap** to cast.<br>**Hold** to channel a Beam, Cone or Circle that has a hold time; let go to stop.<br>**Press** it on a toggle that is up to drop it.

## When It Fails

A cast fails during its cooldown, when you lack the mana, or while a screen is open. If a spell finds nothing to work on (no surface to stand a trap on, no enemy within reach) nothing is spent.

Spells never touch your own companions or players you can't harm, can't pass through walls, and never touch a wraith.

## Cost

Every slider moves the cost. Power weighs most, then duration, range and speed. Pushing a slider beyond the shape's ordinary limit costs steeply more. Homing adds 25%, Burst 35%, Split 30%.

A longer cooldown divides the cost: two and a half times the shortest rest costs about half the mana, five times about a third (never less than a quarter). Toggles and held channels pay as they go, so theirs doesn't.

## Upkeep

**Toggles** (Iron Skin and Body Augmentation) cost five seconds of their drain to raise, then drain every second, by your core rank and their strength (see [Pure Mana](/spellcraft/pure_mana)). They stay up when you switch loadouts. Dropping one starts its cooldown. Only one of each can be up at a time.

**Channels** cost their price to start and drain each second you hold them. Let go early and the cooldown is shortened to match, never below a quarter.

Your mana keeps refilling while a toggle is up, but not while you channel, gather or fly.

A Volley's cooldown is never shorter than the time it takes to loose.

Each element's hit has its own effect: see [The Elements](/spellcraft/elements).
