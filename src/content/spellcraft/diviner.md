A gift of foresight, only ever born: 4% of elves are Diviners, and every Diviner has danger sense.

**Foresight** <span class="dim">Orange</span><br>You see the next one to four attacks coming and sidestep them. Hostiles within 24 blocks glow.

**Remote Viewing** <span class="dim">Yellow</span><br>Your sight leaves your body: fly it with the movement keys (jump and sneak to rise and sink), 16 blocks from your eyes plus one a point of power, 96 at most. It sees no further through walls than your eyes would.

While your sight is away your body stands still and can't act. A blow, your mana running out, your body being moved or the cast key pressed again brings it home. It costs mana every second it is out, and its cooldown starts when it returns.
