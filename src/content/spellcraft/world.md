Magic changes what it touches:

**Water and ice**: quench lava to obsidian and put out fire.<br>**Fire**: melts snow and ice, lights campfires, candles and TNT, and can start fires.<br>**Plant**: grows crops, and unbinds a [Plant-Binding Rune Seal](/items/warded_blocks).

**Lightning**: scrapes the tarnish off copper.<br>**Earth**: cracks stone bricks; earth and sound shatter glass.<br>**Wind**: blows out flames.

Server owners can turn these off, or stop fire spreading and glass breaking, in the <span class="thing">environment</span> settings. Rune seals always answer plant magic.

## Power

Power is more than damage. A stronger spell is bigger, lands harder (what it strikes, and what stands near, is thrown back), shakes the ground and sounds heavier.

Past power 12 (a Red core's best), a missile, blast, strike, slam or beam breaks the ground: a crater that grows with power, shaped by its element.

## The Ground

**Earth**: craters and throws up soil and stone.<br>**Fire**: scorches, burns wood, can set fires.<br>**Water, ice**: wash away plants, sand and gravel and muddy the soil; ice freezes water and lays snow.<br>**Lightning**: splinters wood, fuses sand.<br>**Wind**: strips leaves and loose sand.<br>**Gravity, aether**: crush the ground flat.

Only a share of what breaks drops. Containers, wards, gates and protected ground are never touched; mobGriefing and the <span class="thing">spellsBreakTerrain</span> setting turn it off.

## Places Spared

TBATEC's own places stand whole. Inside a city, village, keep, camp, lair or dungeon, spells and hard landings break, scorch, crack and shatter nothing, and no fire is lit near one; cast from inside one, a spell breaks no ground at all.

Only what harms nothing answers there: candles and campfires, stray fire, a skin of ice on water, crops. The one exception is the godrune of Destruction, which burns through anything. The <span class="thing">spellsSpareStructures</span> setting turns this off.
