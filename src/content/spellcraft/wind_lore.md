## From the Books

Every wind spell named in the books has its place in the Wind school, each with its own shape. A few the books only show are ours, marked so.

Wind Bullet is the **Bolt**, Whirlwind Fist the **Mana-Infused Strike**, Wind Barrier the **Dome** and **Wall**, and Burst Step and Mirage Walk are [Pure Mana](/spellcraft/pure_mana).
