**How to get it**: Found in djinn relic chests (the clans of Epheotus and deeper); asuran treasuries and dragon hoards (Castle Indrath).

</div></div>

Aldir's weapon, silver and malleable, leaving silvery arcs where it cuts. It has been a moonlit rapier, a curved kopis and an ornate longspear. After Aldir's death it turned to light, passed over the council and chose Eleanor Leywin, becoming a bright, unstrung bow whose string appears only when she wills it.

Every arrow adds 35% of the hit again as pure mana, which armour does not stop. It draws arrows of mana: no ammunition needed. Sneak and use to change its form.
