The arms, armour and relics of the story, found where the story keeps them: lords' weapons in the cities' armouries and royal rooms, Alacryan steel in the strongholds and the basilisks' laboratories, asuran arms in Epheotus and Castle Indrath. Little turns up in watchtowers and hamlets; the deeper and more dangerous the place, the richer its chests.

## How They Work

**Launchers**: Gideon's Rune Cannon and Stannard's Mana Launcher fire their own shells: right click, no mana needed, then they rest<br>**Passives**: some weapons add something to every blow: fire, frost, lightning, aether, mana drain...<br>**Your core**: weapons and armour grow with your mana core: from their own numbers at Black to x2 (mana steel) up to x4 (asuran) at White<br>**Unbreakable**: no weapon or armour of the armoury wears out; relics that are used up still are<br>**Foci**: staves, wands and some relics strengthen your own spells and cheapen them while held; the magic is yours, a staff holds no spell<br>**Sets**: wear every piece of a set for its bonus<br>**Relics**: each has one power; some work just by being carried

## Wearing Relics

Rings, necklaces, bracers, gauntlets, the healing glove, Bairon's cape and many small relics can be worn in the accessory slots (the Curios screen, from the button in your inventory). Worn, a relic works as if carried: its effects, its focus, a phoenix pendant's ward. A worn gauntlet arms your bare fist. <kbd title="Use Worn Relic (sneak: choose)">U</kbd> uses the power of a relic you wear; sneak and press it to choose which. Pendants, bracers, gauntlets, the glove and the cape show on you while worn.

## Growing With You

Every weapon and armour piece grows with its holder's mana core, along the same curve as their spells: blows hit harder (x1.27 for an Asuran blade at a Red core, x2.41 at Yellow, x4 at White; x1.13, x1.55 and x2 for mana steel), armour gains toughness so it keeps turning the blows of foes of your level, and a relic's spell or a launcher's shell is cast at the power a mage of your core could give it. The tooltip shows each at your own core.

**Aether** is not mana: armour, Protection and shields do nothing against it. Aether weapons add a share of every blow again as aether and break raised shields; an aether relic's spell strikes through armour.

## Making Them

A few things can be made: Gideon's fire-salt inventions, Ellie's bows, a dimension ring, the exoform, runed leather, and a handful of beast-crafted arms. The rest must be found.
