## Beast-core Smithing

Dwarven arms and armour are held the finest on Dicathen, and Darv's master smiths the best there are. Two will work for you: the <span class="thing">master smith</span> in a smithy at the foot of <span class="thing">Vildorial</span>, a short walk from the great plaza, and the <span class="thing">forge-master</span> at the hearth of the Great Forge, on the central island of the <span class="thing">Magma Forge</span> of Darv.

Right click him. Put a weapon or armour piece of the Armoury in the first box and a beast core in the second, click the heart or a slot, and read what will change; the result shows the piece as it will come back, its price beside it. Take it to pay.

## The Heart

A beast core keeps the attribute of the beast it came from, and a core set in metal answers to it. Each piece takes **one heart**: its class sets how strong it is (E weakest, then D, C, B, A, AA, S, SS, Asura), its attribute what it does.

**Weapons**: each hit carries 4% (E) to 16% (S), 18% (SS) or 20% (Asura) more as the heart's element: fire burns, water weakens, wind pushes, earth slows; a bow's arrows carry it too<br>**Staves and wands**: +2% (E) to +10% (Asura) damage to spells of its element<br>**Armour**: each piece wards off 3.5% (E) to 15.5% (Asura) of its element: fire and lightning, water, frost and drowning, wind and falls, or earth and blasts; a full set stops at 50%

## Raw and Attuned

**Raw cores**: a core with no attribute is raw mana: in a weapon three quarters as strong, as pure mana that armour does not stop; in a staff half as strong, for every spell; in armour half, against every spell (at most 25%)<br>**Attuned**: a heart of an element you have an affinity for (or of its deviant art) counts a quarter more<br>**Aether**: no ward stops aether

## Slots

Besides its heart a piece has **slots** for lesser cores: one in mana steel, two in Artifact and Masterwork pieces, three in Relics and Asuran arms. The metal bears cores only so strong: up to B-class in mana steel, A in Artifacts, AA in Masterworks, SS in Relics; only asuran arms hold an Asura core.

Cores of one kind in one piece stack: the strongest counts whole, the next half, the third a quarter, up to a limit.

## What Slots Do

**Weapons**: fire +1.5% to +5.5% damage; wind as much attack speed; earth +0.1 to +0.5 knockback; water heals you 0.6% to 2.2% of each hit; raw restores a little mana each hit<br>**Bows**: fire, wind and earth harden the arrows (+1.5% to +5.5%); water and raw as for weapons<br>**Staves**: spells of the core's element cost 0.5% to 4.5% less (raw: every spell, half as much)<br>**Armour**: earth +0.2 to +1.8 toughness; wind +0.5% to +4.5% speed; water +0.2 to +1.8 health; fire +0.4% to +3.6% melee damage; raw spells 0.3% to 2.7% cheaper

## Which Core Is Which

Slain by you, a beast drops its core with its attribute. Bought, looted or paid as a bounty, a core is raw.

**Fire**: Batrunner, Black-fanged Wolf, Charwood Glider, Flare Hawk, Hades Serpent, Phoenix Wyrm, Wyvern; Lightning: Thunder Hawk, Thunder Wolf; Magma: Emperor Scourge, Lava Salamander<br>**Water**: Flutter Drake, Glitterfish, Ten-thousand-legged Crab, Venator; Ice: Frost Snail, Frost-claw Bear, Snarler, Widow Queen; Plant: Bladegrass Cat, Elderwood Guardian, Thorned Growler, Tree Golem, Wood Wight<br>**Wind**: Bladewing, Griffon, Mountain Wing, Raptor Squirrel, Sky Swimmer, Skyray; Sound: Scarlet Harpy, Sonic Hawk, World Lion<br>**Earth**: Chimera, Ebon Scourge, Giant Red Worm, Guardian Bear, Mountain Golem, Skitter, Titan Bear, Tunnelworm; Metal: Iron Hyrax, Iron-bite Bear, Ironhide Mammoth, Midnight Grizzly, Steelbeak

The rest, from bears and oxen to the creatures of the Relictombs, carry raw mana.

## Fees

**A heart**: 4 emeralds and 4 more per class step (8 for an E core, 32 for an S), times 1 for mana steel, 1.25 Artifact, 1.5 Masterwork, 2 Relic, 2.5 Asuran: an S heart in a Relic is 64, an Asura heart in asuran arms 100<br>**A slot**: half a heart<br>**Drawing out**: half of what setting it cost<br>**Kin**: a dwarf pays four fifths

Creative play is free.

## Drawing Out

Only a smith takes a core out again: click a set core with the core box empty (or with a new core in it, to swap). Some of its mana stays in the steel, so it comes back **a class weaker**, keeping its attribute; an E-class core crumbles.

The cores stay with the piece through anvils, renames, grindstones and form changes. Combining two pieces at an anvil keeps the first one's cores. Arthur's aether blade (condensed aether) and Regis (alive) take none.
