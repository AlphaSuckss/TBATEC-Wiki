**How to get it**: Only in the Relictombs, at the maze's shrine. It answers only to someone who holds aether.

</div></div>

Found as heavy, far-too-long robes embroidered with runes in a Relictombs shrine that opens only to a blood sacrifice, made by a djinn woman who led a resistance against Clan Indrath.

**Full set**: Answers only to aether: it will not stay on anyone else. Speed I and Resistance I; a quarter of every blow against you is turned back on the striker. It runs on your aether: each piece gains armour and toughness, and the whole suit health and a share of every blow, for each layer of your core.

Unbreakable. 4 toughness a piece, growing with your core to +18 more at White.
