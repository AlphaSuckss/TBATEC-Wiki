TBATEC has no single quest line. Your mana core is the thread that runs through it: it grows as you use it, and each new colour asks you to prove yourself somewhere new. This page walks that road in order and links the chapters that tell each part in full.

## 1. Who you are

The first time you join, the heritage menu opens. You choose **Human**, **Elf** or **Dwarf** (or let the mana choose), and the mana decides the rest: whether a core awakens, your class, your elements and any rare gift. See [Choosing a Heritage](/heritage/choosing).

- **No core?** A human without one is a hardened warrior instead, and everything without magic is still open to you: companions, the kingdoms, the gates, the Armoury.
- **A rare few** are born asura, of one of the eight clans of Epheotus, or are wraiths. They play very differently: see [Heritage](/heritage).

A human mage then chooses an upbringing, for life (see [Two Upbringings](/alacrya/upbringing)):

| Raised in Dicathen | Raised in Alacrya |
| --- | --- |
| You shape your own spells in the [Spell Workshop](/spellcraft/workshop). | Your spells are runes bestowed on your spine at a [dais](/alacrya/bestowal). |
| You are an augmenter or a conjurer, with the mana arts. | You have no class, but a [role](/alacrya/roles) and a [blood](/alacrya/bloods). |
| You wake in the kingdoms of Dicathen. | You wake in [Maerin](/alacrya/maerin), across the sea. |

## 2. Growing your core

A core goes **Black, Red, Orange, Yellow, Silver, White** (see [Core Ranks](/mana_core/ranks)). Within a colour it grows freely: every point of mana you spend [refines](/mana_core/refinement) it, [meditation](/mana_core/meditation) refines it while you rest, and absorbing a [beast core](/items/beast_cores) is the fastest way of all.

Moving to the next colour needs its **[breakthrough trials](/mana_core/trials)**: two deeds each. When your core waits at its limit, the game lists what is missing and what each trial asks of you.

| To | Raised in Dicathen | Raised in Alacrya |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red | Cast a spell; slay a mana beast | Bear a mark; cast through it |
| Orange | Shape a spell in the Workshop; claim a beast core | Have a rune deepened; register as an ascender |
| Yellow | Slay an A-class beast; bind a beast by contract | Earn your ascender's badge; be called Allied by a blood |
| Silver | Slay an AA-class beast; enter the Dire Tombs | Bear a crest; beat one of Vritra blood |
| White | Slay an S-class beast; defeat the Elderwood Guardian | Bear an emblem; beat the Retainer Echeron |

Along the way, [Sylvia](/mana_core/rotation) teaches Mana Rotation, a [backlash](/mana_core/backlash) punishes running yourself dry, and [mentors](/mentors) teach what no book can.

## 3. Dicathen

The overworld is the continent of Dicathen: the human kingdom of Sapin, the elven forest of Elshire, the dwarven kingdom of Darv, and between them the **Beast Glades**, where the strongest [mana beasts](/beasts) live.

- [Lands of Dicathen](/lands) and its [sites](/sites): Xyrus, Zestier, Vildorial, Etistin, the Wall, the Dire Tombs and more.
- Teleport gates join the cities; a [Teleportation Crest](/items/teleportation_crest) takes you through them.
- Bind beasts as [companions](/companions), ride them, and take their [wills](/wills) into your core.
- Earn [standing](/standing) with the kingdoms, take requests from their guards, and perhaps become a Lance.

## 4. Alacrya

Across the sea lies [Alacrya](/alacrya), the Vritra's continent, a land of its own reached by a crossing. Its towns, its five Dominions, the bloods, the battlegroups, the Victoriad, the Scythes and their Retainers, and Taegrin Caelum at the heart of it all. A Map of Alacrya is yours on first arrival.

## 5. The Relictombs

Beyond Aramoor's portal lie the djinn's ruins. [Become an ascender](/relictombs/becoming) (register, pass the assessment, survive a preliminary ascent), then climb: the [First and Second Levels](/relictombs/ways), zones of [fights](/relictombs/zones_fights), [hazards](/relictombs/zones_hazards), [ruins](/relictombs/zones_ruins) and [trials](/relictombs/zones_trials), and their [rewards](/relictombs/rewards).

## 6. Epheotus

The hidden realm of the asura floats over a sea of clouds. An [Asura Sigil](/items/asura_sigil) takes you there and back. See [Epheotus](/epheotus): Castle Indrath, the eight clans' seats, and Lord Indrath himself.

## 7. Past White

A White core is not the end:

- **[Integration](/mana_core/integration)**: mana that runs through the whole body, for the very few who reach it.
- **[The aether path](/relictombs/rebirth)**: a White core with a living asura bond, killed by its own mana, may be reborn into an asuran body that runs on aether. Only one player in a world can take it. Its godrunes are the strongest power in the game.
