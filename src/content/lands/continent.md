Dicathen's kingdoms lie in bands across the overworld. Spawn is in [Sapin](/lands/sapin), the human kingdom.

**North**: past about Z -700, the elven forest of [Elshire](/lands/elshire); its canopy heart some 400 blocks further in<br>**South**: past about Z 900, the dwarven [Kingdom of Darv](/lands/darv)

**East**: past about X 700, at every latitude, the [Beast Glades](/lands/beast_glades), which swallow the kingdoms; their Depths begin some 1,200 blocks further in

The borders wander a few hundred blocks either way, and server owners can move them.

Oceans, rivers, beaches, peaks, snow, jungles and caves stay as they are; the kingdoms claim the land between.

## Beyond

Hidden from all of it, reached only by an [Asura Sigil](/items/asura_sigil), is [Epheotus](/epheotus/realm). The cities, castles and dungeons of each land are under [Sites of Dicathen](/sites/xyrus).
