Alacryan battlegroups raid Dicathen's villages and towns. A bell rings and the raiders come from the Glades' side: Etril's marked patrols against weak mages, a dominion's emblem-led group against Yellow and Silver cores, the Central Dominion's against White.

Raiders in the village wear its hold down; beat them all and the village is safe. Villagers are never harmed.

## The Aftermath

If no one stops them, they take two or three villagers and withdraw. Come to a raided village later and smoke still rises over it; two <span class="thing">survivors</span> stand in its middle.

Talk to one: they tell you how many were taken and which way. Follow the trail of ash to the raiders' camp, 70-110 blocks out, within 12 minutes, and beat them to bring the captives home.

## Notes

Defending a village, or bringing its captives home, earns standing with its kingdom and the others, war merit, emeralds and the title Raid-breaker.

**From the books**: in the war Alacryan mages raided Dicathen's roads and convoys, and the Alacryan army forced captured Dicathians into its ranks.

**Invented**: the raids, the aftermath, the trail and the rewards.
