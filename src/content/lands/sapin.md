The human kingdom at the heart of the continent: rolling plains and farmland between the elven forest to the north and the dwarven wastes to the south.

Oak and birch groves, bee-filled oaks, tall grass and drifts of wild golden wheat. Its rivers are the <span class="thing">Sapin River</span>.
