The dwarven kingdom in the south: hot, dry wastes of red sand, coarse earth and banded terracotta, cactus and dead bush, over the mountain halls where the dwarves live and forge. It never rains.

[Dwarven villagers and guards](/peoples/dwarves) are found in and around it, with skitters and flare hawks.

## Places

[Dwarven desert holds](/sites/darv_hold) stand on the surface. Below lies the [abyss](/lands/darv_abyss), and in it the capital, [Vildorial](/sites/vildorial).
