A wild expanse east of the kingdoms, teeming with mana beasts: jungle trees, bamboo, melons and dark <span class="thing">glade titans</span> 22 to 38 blocks tall, under a green-grey sky.

Where swamps would be there is <span class="thing">Marsh</span> (mangroves, mud and blight hobs); where hills and meadows would be, <span class="thing">Highlands</span> (hawks, bears and lions).

## The Depths

Past about 1,200 blocks in, the <span class="thing">Depths</span> begin: darker forest, mushrooms and cobwebs, and the deadliest beasts, the S-class among them.

Everything gets stronger the deeper you go: ordinary creatures [awaken](/beasts/awakened) a class higher every 500 blocks, and a quarter of the beasts in the deep are [Elders](/beasts/variants).

Hostile beasts hunt here by day as well as by night. See [Where Beasts Roam](/beasts/roaming).
