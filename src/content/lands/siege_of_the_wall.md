In the war the Alacryans drove hordes of <span class="thing">corrupted beasts</span> out of the Beast Glades against the [Wall](/sites/the_wall), with their mages riding in the horde. One of them carried a <span class="thing">black stone</span> that held the beasts; when Arthur crushed it, they went wild and tore at each other.

Stand near the Wall with a Silver core (or as a grown asura, a wraith or one of the reborn) and the Alacryans may come again.

## The Waves

**Horns**: for 60 seconds horns sound and the <span class="thing">Bulwark</span>, Sapin's knights, forms up before the gate.

**The horde**: corrupted thunder wolves, hounds, black-fanged wolves, a two-headed serpent and flocks of steelbeaks, led by a horde caller, come over the river gorge. Their <span class="thing">Beast Handler</span> walks at the back: bring him down and the horde turns on itself.

**The battlegroups**: the siege camp's emblem-led groups march on the gate with their corrupted wolves.

**The Retainer**: last comes a Retainer of the Vritra (Uto, by default), with two corrupted thunder wolves, and the three-storey metal mammoth if a White mage defends.

The masons shore the gate up for 30 seconds between the waves.

## Holding It

Foes at the gate wear its <span class="thing">hold</span> down (the bar shows it). Beat every wave and the Wall holds. If the hold runs out, the gate gives way and the siege pours into <span class="thing">Greengate</span>: clear it within 120 seconds to retake the town. If not, its stalls stay shut for 24 in-game hours and the next siege comes sooner.

Holding the Wall earns standing with Sapin and the other kingdoms, war merit, emeralds and the title Defender of the Wall (Warden after three).

## Notes

**From the books**: the hordes of corrupted beasts at the Wall, the mages in the horde and the black stone, the beasts going wild when it broke, the thunder-wolf pack, the steel-beaked birds, the metal mammoth, the Bulwark forming up at the Glades entrance, archers and conjurers at the Wall's slits.
