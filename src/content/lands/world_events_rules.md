**Where**: Dicathen only (never Alacrya, Epheotus, the Relictombs); never within 96 blocks of the world spawn, in a capital or haven (Xyrus, Etistin, Zestier, Vildorial, the djinn sanctuary...), on peaceful, or into a chunk that is not loaded.

**When**: a chance round each player every 5 minutes; one event a region every 2 in-game days, a siege every 1.

**Who**: the siege and raids are for the Dicathen-raised to fight; anyone can turn a stampede.

## Safety

An event with no one near pauses; after a few minutes it ends (raiders get away with captives). Its foes break no block and leave with it. Those who stayed for a quarter of the fight, or struck a foe down, share the reward.
