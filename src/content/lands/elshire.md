A vast, ancient forest in the north and the home of the elves of Elenoir: moss and podzol, flowers, and <span class="thing">great birches</span> 18 to 30 blocks tall, under a pale haze of drifting white motes.

[Elven villagers and wardens](/peoples/elves) live beneath its trees, with moon oxen and forest hounds.

## The Mist

Elshire's <span class="thing">mist</span> confounds anyone who isn't an elf: it closes in to about 14 blocks, and neither light nor a Wind Ward lifts it. It keeps to the open forest: inside a town's walls, a hall or under a roof it lifts. Elves see clearly, and in Elshire nothing can blind them.

Server owners can turn the mist off.

## Places

The elven capital of [Zestier](/sites/zestier) stands in the canopy; [Elven Tree Halls](/sites/tree_hall) and [Elshire Shrines](/sites/elshire_shrine) stand throughout the forest. Where the wild strip meets the Glades lies [Sylvia's Cave](/sites/sylvias_cave).
