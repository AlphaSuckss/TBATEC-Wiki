Under Darv, below Y -10, lies the <span class="thing">abyss</span>: vast caverns 25 to 60 blocks across, banded with tuff, deepslate and basalt, a third of them holding underground seas, others crossed by <span class="thing">magma rivers</span>, their roofs hung with fangs of glowing <span class="item">abyss crystal</span>.

## Its Beasts

Cave rats swarm it. Giant red worms and titanic bloodworms swim its stone, hades serpents coil in it, lava salamanders lurk in its magma and shadow prowlers hunt its walls.

## Places

[Vildorial](/sites/vildorial), the dwarven capital, fills one great cavern; the [Magma Forge](/sites/darv_forge) and the hidden [Djinn Sanctuary](/sites/djinn_sanctuary) lie deeper still.
