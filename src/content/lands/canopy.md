Deep in Elenoir the forest closes over into a twilight <span class="thing">canopy</span>: giant dark trunks 26 to 40 blocks tall whose crowns touch, curtains of hanging leaves and glow berries, moss, and a haze of spore-blossom pollen.

## Spore Haze

The pollen blinds anyone who isn't an elf: the world shrinks to about 11 blocks. Hold something that glows (a torch, a lantern, a Glow-Lotus) or carry a [Wind Ward](/items/wind_ward_charm) and it parts. Inside Zestier, a hall or a building there is none.
