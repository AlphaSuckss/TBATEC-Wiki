## Teleport Gates

<div class="spotlight"><img class="icon big" src="/img/item/tbatec/gate_anchor.png" alt="Gate Anchor" title="Gate Anchor" loading="lazy"><div>

A gate is an arch over a floor of <span class="item">teleport runes</span>, with a <span class="item">gate anchor</span> in its pier. Neither can be broken.

</div></div>

## Discovery

Come within about ten blocks of a gate, or use its anchor, and you <span class="thing">discover</span> it, along with every gate of the same site within 400 blocks: all of Xyrus's at once, but one keep's gate never reveals another's.

## Travel

Use the anchor to list every gate you have discovered in this dimension, with the cost and distance of each (your own site first). Pick one and a portal opens in the arch for fifteen seconds: walk through it and you step out of the other gate, keeping your stride. It closes soon after someone goes through.

A warp costs <span class="thing">20 mana</span> plus one for every 40 blocks, at most 400.

## Gate Sickness

Gate travel leaves you reeling: a first trip brings <span class="thing">Nausea III</span> for about eight seconds. Each trip you get more used to it, down to a second or two of Nausea I for a seasoned traveller, and the habit fades slowly (by half in three days) when you stop travelling.
