Now and then the Glades' beasts break out toward a <span class="thing">border town</span> or village: from 800 blocks outside the Glades border to 900 blocks inside it. The ground trembles, a roar comes from the Glades' side, and the stampede follows.

The beasts come from deeper in the Glades than the town stands (about 600 blocks), so their class follows that depth, as the Glades rank every beast.

## The Herd

Three waves: the runners (a class lower), the herd, and the beast that drives it (a class higher; at A class, a horde caller) with the last of the herd.

Beasts in the town wear its hold down. Beat every wave and the stampede turns back; let the hold run out and it tramples through, and the town's stalls shut for a while.

These are wild beasts: not corrupted, no one's. Turning one earns standing with the town's kingdom, war merit, emeralds and the title Stampede-breaker.

## Notes

**From the books**: the Beast Glades teem with mana beasts ranked by how deep they live; Sapin built the Wall against them.
