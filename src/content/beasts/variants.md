**Elder**: a beast below S-class that spawns in the Glades Depths is an Elder one time in four: a class higher, half again the health, a third harder hitting, larger, worth more experience. A companion that [ascends](/companions/raising) becomes one too.

Names say so: <span class="thing">Elder Wyvern \[AA\]</span>.

**Corrupted**: 8% of two-headed serpents, 5% of grizzly wolves, 25% of tunnelworms and 6% of hades serpents. A class higher, nearly twice the health and far harder hitting, but their cores are spoiled: a corrupted beast never drops one.

## Devourers and Leaders

**Devourer**: a Widow Queen who kills a rival queen becomes AA-class, with more than twice the health and twice the bite.

**Pack leaders**: the first of a pack to arrive leads it (a <span class="thing">Pack Leader</span> gnoll and an <span class="thing">Alpha</span> thunder wolf): a class higher, larger, with more health and bite. Its howl sets the pack on.
