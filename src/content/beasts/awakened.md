The mana of the Glades seeps into everything born there. Ordinary creatures that spawn in the Glades are <span class="thing">awakened</span>: they take a class from how deep they are, one class for every 500 blocks past the border, E up to A, with a chance of one higher or lower. In the Depths some reach AA or even S.

An awakened creature has up to five and a half times the health and over three times the damage, more armour and speed, and a name like <span class="thing">Zombie \[Class C\]</span>. From C-class even neutral creatures hunt you.

Slain by a player, it may drop a core of its class: E 5%, D 10%, C 20%, B 35%, A 55%, AA 80%, S always.
