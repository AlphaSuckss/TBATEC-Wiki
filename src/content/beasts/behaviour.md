**Hostile**: hunts players on sight. Night hunters (snarlers, batrunners and nightmare foxes) hunt only by night, except in the Glades.<br>**Neutral**: fights only when struck.<br>**Passive**: never fights; flees when hurt.

Many beasts also hunt smaller creatures of their own accord.

## Pack Sense

Strike one wild beast and every wild mana beast nearby that has nothing else to do comes too, whatever its kind.

**Blind** beasts (ebon scourges, giant red worms, tunnelworms, glades ravagers, emperor scourges and aether millipedes) ignore anyone sneaking more than 3 blocks away.

## Sleep

Wild beasts doze in their resting hours (night, or day for night hunters) when no one is within 10 blocks, 5 if sneaking. A sleeper wakes if you come within 4.5 blocks (1.5 sneaking) or hurt it, and ignores anything further than 3 blocks away.

Bosses never sleep.

## Bosses

Every beast of class <span class="thing">AA</span> or above, asuran beasts too, is a boss: a red bar shows its health to all within 48 blocks while it fights.

Beasts take less from a fall; fliers and burrowers none.

**Asuran** beasts take 40% less from spells and half from aether.

## Fliers

A beast that cannot fly does not stand under a flier to be killed. Hang above its reach for two seconds and a beast of class <span class="thing">B</span> or better **leaps** at you: 8 blocks up at B, 12 at A, 15 at AA, 19 at S, 24 above that. If it reaches you it strikes, drags you down, and you cannot take to the air again for 4 seconds.

A weaker beast, or any beast when you fly higher than it can leap, **breaks off** after five seconds: it bolts for cover, will not be drawn back while you stay in the air, and mends 4% of its health a second. <code>\[invented\]</code>
