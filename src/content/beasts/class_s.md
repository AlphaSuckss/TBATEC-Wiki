## Elderwood Guardian

The Dire Tombs' guardian: a twenty-metre vine centaur with a drill-lance.

600 health, hits for 22, 14 armour. Hostile, alone. A three-phase boss: roots, spears, a stamp; then mist and tree golems; then an emerald aura of vines. Fire burns it.<br>**Found**: only its lair below the Dire Tombs (the Guild sells a map).<br>**Bond**: by force only.<br>**Drops**: Elderwood Heartwood, Elderwood Guardian's Will; oak log, moss block, emerald, golden apple (25%).

<span id="phoenix_wyrm"></span>

## Phoenix Wyrm

A pearly pink wyrm, high S-class yet no fighter: it cocoons and vanishes.

300 health, bites for 10, 6 armour, flies, fireproof. Neutral, alone. **Cocoon**: below a third of its health it shells up, then flees far, healed. **Fire Breath**.<br>**Found**: cherry groves, mountains, the Glades Depths.<br>**Bond**: a core. **Ride**: Fire Breath, Surge.<br>**Drops**: Phoenix Wyrm Feather; feather, blaze powder, totem of undying (15%).

<span id="glades_ravager"></span>

## Glades Ravager

Not in the novel: a fifty-foot centipede of ours that hunts great beasts.

420 health, stings for 24, 16 armour, burrows, blind. Hostile, alone. Hunts S-class beasts, even bound ones. **Burrow**: up to 40 blocks. Its sting badly poisons.<br>**Found**: deep caves, the Glades Depths.<br>**Bond**: by force only.<br>**Drops**: Ravager Stinger; bone, yellow dye, slimeball.

<span id="iron_hydrax"></span>

## Iron Hyrax

A massive hyrax in riveted iron plate; ramming into one is like hitting a wall.

450 health, bites for 20, 20 armour. Hostile, alone. **Charge**: a ram that breaks shields. **Iron Spikes**: it stamps them up in a line. Missiles do a quarter.<br>**Found**: stony peaks, gravelly hills, the Glades Depths.<br>**Bond**: by force only.<br>**Drops**: Hyrax Plate; iron ingot, block of iron (35%).<br>*Only named in the novel.*

<span id="midnight_grizzly"></span>

## Midnight Grizzly

Six-limbed bears furred in metal from the ores they eat; B to AA in the novel.

380 health, mauls for 26, 14 armour. Hostile, alone. **Night Hunter**: stronger in the dark, and brings darkness. **Roar**: weakens.<br>**Found**: mountains, taiga, the Glades Depths by night; the Beast Lair.<br>**Bond**: by force only.<br>**Drops**: Midnight Grizzly Pelt; leather, amethyst shard.

<span id="emperor_scourge"></span>

## Emperor Scourge

Lord of Hollow's Edge: an ebon scourge many times the size, horns glowing like coals.

480 health, hits for 26, 14 armour, blind. Hostile, alone. Calls ebon scourges. **Roar**: slows. **Charge**: breaks shields.<br>**Found**: under the Glades Depths.<br>**Bond**: by force only.<br>**Drops**: Emperor Scourge Horn; bone, leather, diamond (40%).

## Black-horned Mutant

A lion-headed S-class beast the Alacryans turned mutant, black horns and all.

520 health, bites for 28, 14 armour. Hostile, alone. Commands and summons orcs and gnolls. **Slam**, **Pounce**, **Roar**.<br>**Found**: caves under mountains, the Glades Depths.<br>**Bond**: never.<br>**Drops**: Black Mutant Horn; bone, leather.<br>*(The novel never names it; the name is ours.)*

<span id="venator"></span>

## Venator

A twenty-foot, six-eyed alligator on its hind legs: the deep Glades' ambusher.

460 health, claws for 26, 16 armour. Hostile, alone. **Fade** in the dark, **Stalk**. Its tail sweeps all round; its claws rake. Missiles do 60%.<br>**Found**: the Glades Depths.<br>**Bond**: by force only.<br>**Drops**: Venator Osteoderm, Venator Fang (50%); leather, bone.

## Ironhide Mammoth

A three-storey mammoth in metal hide with a mace-orb trunk, from the Wall.

540 health, hits for 24, 22 armour, fireproof. Neutral, alone. Trunk smash, **Slam**, **Charge** (breaks shields). Missiles do half.<br>**Found**: the Glades Depths.<br>**Bond**: by force only.<br>**Drops**: Ironhide Plate; iron ingot, block of iron (30%).<br>*(The novel never names it; the name is ours.)*

## Striped Fiend

A striped cat-man twice a man's height, fast as an asura, open after it strikes.

400 health, claws for 24, 8 armour, fast. Hostile, alone. **Shadow Step**: behind you in a blink. **Stalk**, **Pounce**, a raking swipe.<br>**Found**: the Glades Depths.<br>**Bond**: by force only.<br>**Drops**: Striped Fiend Pelt (70%), Wind-infused Claw (40%); leather.<br>*(The novel never names it; the name is ours.)*

<span id="shadow_golem"></span>

## Shadow Golem

The black platform's faceless guardian: thirty feet of shadow plate, horned helm.

620 health, pounds for 22, 14 armour. Hostile, alone. **Shadow Plate**: non-aether harm x0.2. **Shadow Slam**: its seams open (x2.5, 2 s). **Grasp**, **Sink and Rise**; sentinels at half.<br>**Found**: the Relictombs only. Black platform.<br>**Bond**: never.<br>**Drops**: Shadow Golem Shard, Aether Mote; obsidian, black dye.<br>*The seams and smoke are ours.*

<span id="aether_millipede"></span>

## Aether Millipede

Towering, ridged, hundreds of legs and a translucent purple shell.

480 health, snaps for 18, 12 armour, blind. Hostile, alone. **Head Crater**: head x2. **Charge**, **Mandible Grab**, **Leg Stab**, **Crystal Spew**. Hunts by sound.<br>**Found**: the Relictombs only. Crystal den.<br>**Bond**: never.<br>**Drops**: Aether crystal, Aether Millipede Chitin; slimeball.

## Three-headed Serpent

A black-platform serpent with three heads, big enough to stand on.

420 health, bites for 20, 12 armour. Hostile, alone. **Three Breaths**: fire, frost, poison in turn. Venom. **Roar**: slows.<br>**Found**: the Relictombs only. Black platforms.<br>**Bond**: never.<br>**Drops**: Aether Mote; bone, blaze powder, snowball, spider eye.<br>*Its breaths and look are ours.*
