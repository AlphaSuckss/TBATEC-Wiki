Mana beasts are ranked by the core they carry, from <span class="thing">E</span> through D, C, B, A and AA to <span class="thing">S</span>, then the <span class="thing">asuran</span> beasts of Epheotus. <span class="thing">SS</span> is a class no wild beast holds; only a companion can rise to it.

A beast's class shows in its name, coloured by class.

## Core Drops

A beast slain by a player may drop the core of its class:

**E**: 25%<br>**D**: 35%<br>**C**: 50%<br>**B**: 65%<br>**A**: 80%<br>**AA and S**: always<br>**Asura**: 60%

A companion never drops one, nor does a corrupted beast.

Absorb a core to refine your own, feed it to a companion, or use it to bind a beast: see [Beast Cores](/items/beast_cores) and [Contracts](/companions/contracts).

The deeper you go into the [Beast Glades](/lands/beast_glades), the higher the class of what hunts there.
