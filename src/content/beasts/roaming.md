Most beasts live in the [Beast Glades](/lands/beast_glades). Every few seconds, near each player east of the Glades border, a pack may appear 26 to 56 blocks away, unless 14 beasts are already within 64 blocks.

The Depths have their own, deadlier list, and only one S-class beast walks within 160 blocks of another.

In the Glades and all over Epheotus, hostile beasts come out by day as well. Elsewhere they wait for the dark, like monsters.

Cave-dwellers spawn only underground, below sea level; canopy hunters only under open sky.

Each beast's page says where to find it; the realm's are also under [its biomes](/epheotus/biomes).
