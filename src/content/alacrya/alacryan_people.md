## Townsfolk

Etril's farm folk and townspeople: they trade for gold.

## Town Guard

The watch of Alacrya's towns: Shields and Strikers in grey.

## Ascender

Relic hunters of the Relictombs; some hide their runes.

## Trade

&#8203;<span class="thing">Gold</span> is Alacrya's money: townsfolk sell bread, blood ox steak and wogart fleece for nuggets and buy rocavid hides; the ship hands of <span class="thing">Etril Landing</span> sell the ships' stores. Soldiers carry a few nuggets; officers more, and beast cores.

Sapin's <span class="thing">Quest Master</span> sells a map to the nearest Alacryan beachhead and its war-gate (20 emeralds and a compass).
