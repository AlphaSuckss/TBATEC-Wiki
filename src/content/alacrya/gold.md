## The Runecard

<div class="spotlight"><img class="icon big" src="/img/item/tbatec/runecard.png" alt="Runecard" title="Runecard" loading="lazy"><div>

Alacrya pays in gold, and Alacryans carry it on a <span class="item">Runecard</span>, a card tied to the Alacrya Bank. Use it to see your balance and every blood's favour.

</div></div>

## The Card

Your gold is kept with you, not on the card: a lost card loses nothing, and a teller reissues it for 5 gold. The Alacryan-born start with a card and 10 gold.

Contracts pay onto the card; the officiant takes offerings from it; Granbehl sells its gear for it. Dicathen keeps its emeralds, and there is no exchange.

## Teller

Tellers of the Alacrya Bank, in Maerin and Aramoor.
