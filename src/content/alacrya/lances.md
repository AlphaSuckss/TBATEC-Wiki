## Allies and Duels

**Raised in Dicathen**: they are allies who guard the castle. Talk to one (use them).

**Duel**: with a Silver core or better. It ends when the Lance is down to 15% or after three minutes, and never kills you. Your first win against each earns their <span class="thing">Lance's Seal</span>: use it to call them to your side for 90 seconds, once a day, in Dicathen only.

**Raised in Alacrya**: they are your enemies.

## Lessons

**Lessons**: with a Yellow core or better, ask a Lance to teach you, once a day each. The lesson refines your core (a twentieth of the stage you are in) and, if you hold the element their art grows from, raises your mastery of it by 2: Varay water, Bairon fire, Mica earth, Aya wind. <code>\[invented, after Varay's training of Arthur\]</code>

## The Unsealing

Beat **Varay** and **Mica** in duels and ask either about the artifact: Virion has new artifacts from the asura, made without the limiter. Go to him in his manor; he gives you two <span class="thing">Unsealed Artifacts</span>. Carry one to each.

Each passes white into integration (half again the health and art power) and gives you their weapon. Your own worn artifact's limiter is gone.

## Lance Artifacts

&#8203;<span class="thing">Alea's Artifact</span> and <span class="thing">Olfred's Artifact</span> are engraved coins, carried in a charm slot. A Lance's artifact is <span class="thing">bonded</span> with two bloods, a crown's and its bearer's: the bearer's Silver core becomes a White core at once, and the artifact's hidden limiter marks it. From then on refinement comes at half the rate and nothing takes them past White, artifact worn or not, until the Unsealing.

## The Bonding

**The Council**: the artifact Virion gives a Lance he names is bonded there.

**Between players**: whoever holds an unbonded artifact stands for the crown. Sneak and use it on another player who kneels (sneaks) before you and has a Silver core. Each gives a little blood, the artifact passes to them, and their <span class="thing">oath</span> is to you: nothing they do can harm you.

Worn unbonded, an artifact still lends a Silver core White's mana and Mana Flight (O) while it is worn. <code>\[the blood and the breakthrough are the books'; who may stand for a crown, and the oath's form, are invented\]</code>

## Olfred Warend

He waits in the hangar sealed under Vildorial (the stair inside the plaza's monument, behind the vault door). Magma Gauntlet blows, molten ground, Magma Knights; at half health his <span class="thing">Hell's Armour</span> closes and stays. Fire cannot hurt him.

1,600 health. He drops <span class="thing">Olfred's Artifact</span> and a piece of Olfred's Magma Plate. Raised in Alacrya, he lets you pass.

## Never Fallen

A Lance brought below a tenth of their health outside a duel flies home to recover; they cannot be killed at the castle. A seal's Lance is a copy at your side, gone when its time is up.
