Vechor's tournament, in its arena west of Taegrin Caelum. Raised in Alacrya, with a silver core or better: ask <span class="thing">Echeron</span> and the bouts run one after another, no rest between:

**First**: two corrupted beasts loosed on the sand<br>**Second**: Echeron's trial<br>**Third**: Dragoth's trial, if the Titan is in his seat

## Bouts

No bout is lethal: the beasts' blows stop at a fifth of your health, and that loses the run, as does leaving the sand (40 blocks) or losing a trial.

Win them all for favour with <span class="thing">Blood Frost</span>, who back the Victoriad's fighters, the Retainer's deepening, Dragoth's writ, and the advancement. Raised in Dicathen, the arena's masters simply fight you.
