Nobody chooses their blood. Raised in Alacrya, your birth is drawn: most are born **unblooded**, carrying only their own name (15 in 100 are born to a lesser Named Blood, 2 in 100 to a Highblood <code>\[invented\]</code>). An unblooded name begins **plain** and can be raised by deeds.

**Named**: 2,000 favour with the bloods in all and a Retainer beaten (or out-duelled). Register at the Alacrya Bank in Aramoor and choose one of eight crests: your blood's banner is yours, and you stand as one of the Named (+1 mark slot).<br>**Highblood**: Named, Allied with two Highbloods, and holding Melzri's writ: the Sovereign raises your blood. Highblood standing (+1 crest slot) and an Obsidian Vault writ.

## Standing

**Standing** also comes from others: Allied with Granbehl you stand as Named, Allied with Milview or Frost as a Highblood, whatever your own blood is.

The Dicathen-raised can earn the bloods' favour too, as Grey did, but have no blood of their own: their rewards are the gear, writs and vouches.
