Each Scythe has a <span class="thing">retainer</span>, Vritra-blooded and terribly strong: a hard fight for a Silver core, with a boss bar and a second phase at half health.

**Raised in Dicathen**: they fight to the death; the kill is a deed, and Agrona sends his Wraiths after you<br>**Raised in Alacrya**: a <span class="thing">trial</span>: strike them to begin; it ends when they are down to a tenth. The spoils without the horn, and a deepening for a rune

## Uto

In the sealed level under the [Widow's Crypt](/sites/widows_crypt), where he killed the Lance Alea; her best strike only chipped his horn. He conjures black-iron weapons and shields in an instant:

**Blood Iron**: cracks open under you, then a ring of black spikes<br>**Spears**: black-iron spears loosed from over his shoulders<br>**Aegis**: come close and a black shield turns your blows<br>**Chains**: iron binds you where you stand while he watches

## Uto

**Half health**: laughing, he calls up the crypt's corrupted beasts (the Hades serpent). The fight is sealed: no one walks out of it.

1,500 health. He leaves <span class="thing">Uto's Mask</span> and <span class="thing">Uto's Horn</span>, and half the time a Vritra Horn. Only Widow's Crypts made since 1.27 have the sealed level.
