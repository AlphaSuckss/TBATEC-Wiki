## Oludari Vritra

&#8203;<span class="thing">Oludari Vritra</span>, Sovereign of Truacia: reclusive, quick to panic, no fighter. In the books he fled Agrona's wrath, claimed to be the last Sovereign left, begged for his life, and was taken alive by Agrona's Wraiths, then from them for what he knew.

## The Hunt

He hides at the foot of <span class="thing">Viessa's seat</span> in Truacia, by the road. Come near (Silver or better) or strike him and he <span class="thing">bolts</span> across his dominion, leg by leg. Follow the violet motes he sheds.

He wails out blasts that throw back everything near, friend or foe; leaves veils of black smoke; blinks ahead when cornered; stops to beg.

## The Wraiths

**Raised in Dicathen** (or a rebel): Agrona's Wraiths hold posts along his way, and fight you for him.<br>**A loyal Alacryan**: it is the High Sovereign's errand; the Wraiths take him from you once he is bound.

Lose him (no hunter near for half a minute) or take too long, and he is gone, back in hiding a day later.

## Subdue, Then Bind

&#8203;<span class="thing">Take him alive</span>. A blow that would take him below 15% of his health stops there (even a godrune's): he falls to his knees, <span class="thing">subdued</span>, for twenty seconds. Use <span class="thing">three chains</span> on him then to bind him.

Strike him while he kneels and he dies: the hunt is failed. Leave him and he tears free and runs again.
