## The Rite

<div class="spotlight"><img class="icon big" src="/img/item/tbatec/bestowal_dais.png" alt="Bestowal Dais" title="Bestowal Dais" loading="lazy"><div>

Runes are given, not learned. In Maerin's plaza and at Stormcove Academy in Aramoor stands a raised platform with a dais stone; its officiant, a poker-faced man in black with an obsidian staff tipped with a gem, grants them.

</div></div>

## Kneeling

Kneel on the dais. The officiant chants in a tongue you do not know and holds the staff's tip just above your tailbone; it draws blood from your back and its gem glows, the brighter the better the rune. The rune burns into your spine and the officiant announces it to everyone near: your name, your blood, your town, and what you are now: a Caster mark, a Striker crest, a Sentry emblem.

Two hooded assistants in grey stand beside him. A new Striker or Sentry is taken aside for the academies.

## The Gem

**The gem**: dark (nothing took), dim (a mark), bright (a crest), blinding (an emblem), a flash of four colours (a regalia).

**Unadorned**: sometimes nothing takes. Half of what you offered comes back, and you may kneel again another day; Alacryan children try year after year.

Once each in-game day. Your first bestowal is free and begins the moment you reach Maerin; it never leaves you unadorned.

## Offerings

Talk to the officiant (or use the dais stone) to open the rite. Pay in gold from your Runecard or with a beast core:<br>**Humble**: 12 gold or a C core: unadorned 8%, mark 80%, crest 11%, emblem 1%<br>**Generous**: 40 gold or an A core: 4 / 50 / 38 / 8%<br>**Lavish**: 120 gold, an S core or two AA: 2 / 25 / 48 / 24%, regalia 1%

## Odds

Inside its tier the rune is drawn by weight: three times as likely in your own elements and deviant arts, half again for unaspected runes, never one you already bear. The blood that keeps a dais lowers its price: Fairweather at Maerin, Vale at Stormcove, 10% when they trust you and 25% when allied.

## Sacrifice

**Sacrifice**: before you kneel you may give up a rune. It burns away whatever comes, cannot come back in that draw, and spares you a quarter of the price.

**No room**: if the rune revealed has no free slot of its tier, give up one of that tier for it, or decline it and 60% of the offering comes back.

## Deepening

A **deepening** raises a rune one tier along its line: a mark becomes a crest, a crest an emblem. Kneel at any dais with one to spend; it is free and does not wait for the day.

Deepenings come from the bloods (Allied with Granbehl one, with a Highblood two), from beating a Retainer, and from each dais's trial: Maerin's officiant sends you to clear a corrupted den in Etril's fields; Stormcove's to win three bouts in Maerin's colosseum.

## Bouts

The rite is for the Alacryan-born. Dicathen's mages shape their own spells, and the officiant turns them away politely.

**Bouts**: stand on the sand of Maerin's colosseum with a bout to fight and an Etril Striker walks out (its rank follows your core). Neither of you dies: whoever is brought too low yields. Three minutes, or leaving the sand, ends it with no winner.
