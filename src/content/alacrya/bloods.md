## Bloods

In Alacrya your <span class="thing">blood</span> (your family) matters more than your name. Plain bloods are simply "Blood" so-and-so; a Named Blood descends from famous ascenders; a Highblood is true nobility, often reaching back to a Vritra. The Sovereigns can raise a blood: Milview became a Highblood after Circe Milview fell.

## Favour

Six bloods keep envoys you can deal with. Their favour climbs from **Unknown** through **Known** (100) and **Trusted** (400) to **Allied** (1,000). Earn it with contracts posted by their nobles, by bringing a fallen soldier's effects home to their blood, and in the fights to come. Strike or kill their people unprovoked and you lose it; strike a noble and you lose a great deal.

## Fairweather (plain, Maerin)

Fairweather is a plain blood of Maerin, at the southern tip of Etril: farmers, rocavid hunters and the folk who keep the town's bestowal platform swept and hung with banners.

Rocavid hunts and Maerin's fields. Trusted: 10% off at Maerin's dais; Allied: 25% and its banner.

## Vale (plain, military, Aramoor)

Vale is one of Etril's few military bloods. Its sons and daughters lead the battlegroups that patrol the dominion and its manor stands inside Aramoor's walls.

Patrols and the war camps' night watch; camp officers' escorts and defences pay it too. Trusted: 10% off at Stormcove and its battlegroups to hire; Allied: 25%, its officers' dye, its banner and a vouch for your assessment as an ascender.

## Granbehl (Named, Vechor)

Granbehl is a Named Blood of Vechor, descended from famed ascenders. Its crest is a crown trimmed in gold, and its heirs climb the Relictombs in teams of their own.

Hunts and Maerin bears (ascents with the Relictombs). Trusted: Kalon's and Ezra's gear for gold; Allied: a deepening and Named standing.

## Milview (Highblood, Truacia)

Milview of Truacia was a lesser line until the Sovereigns raised it to Highblood after Circe Milview, the Sentry who mapped the Elshire for the war, fell. Its people are watchers and finders.

Night watches and a Sentry's trial. Trusted: Blood Hearing and Far Survey twice as likely; Allied: two deepenings and Highblood standing.

## Denoir (Highblood, Central Dominion)

Denoir is a Highblood of the Central Dominion, close to the Sovereigns. It fosters a Vritra-blooded daughter, and knows better than any blood how to hide horns that should not show.

Corrupted beasts; the first big errand pays with a pendant like Caera's. Trusted: the Vritra lines twice as likely; Allied: two deepenings and an Obsidian Vault writ.

## Frost (Highblood, Sehz-Clar border)

Frost is a Highblood whose estates lie on the border of Sehz-Clar. It breeds Strikers and sends them to the Victoriad, and it judges a mage by how they fight.

Bouts in Maerin's colosseum. Trusted: Striker runes twice as likely; Allied: two deepenings and Highblood standing.

## Effects

**Effects**: a fallen Alacryan soldier may leave a bundle of effects. Hand it to a noble of their blood (Etril's soldiers and the war in Dicathen are Vale's, Vechor's Granbehl's, Truacia's Milview's, the Central Dominion's Denoir's) for 25 favour and 3 gold. Sehz-Clar leaves its dead as they fell. A noble will not take effects you took from someone you killed.

Other bloods (Favager, Bancroft, Bloodworth, Gladwyn, Seabrook, Ramseyer) appear only on banners.
