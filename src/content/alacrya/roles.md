Every rune names a role. In the books the bestowed rune decides it; in the mod your role is whatever most of your runes are, a tie going to the role holding the higher tier, then to the newest. One rune: exactly the books.

Each role has a passive; it grows a little with each further rune of the role.

## Striker and Caster

**Striker**: close combat, with strength, speed and quick healing. +12% melee damage (+2% a rune, to +20%), +8% speed, and 0.5 health a second once unhurt for 5 s.

**Caster**: long-range spells from behind the Shield. Rune cooldowns -15% (-3% a rune, to -27%), rune range +10%.

## Shield and Sentry

**Shield**: protects the Striker. Shield runes 30% stronger and longer; 8% less damage taken within 6 blocks of an ally.

**Sentry**: detection and sensing. Hidden, invisible, suppressed and veiled creatures within 16 blocks (+2 a rune, to 24) are outlined; strong mana nearby is felt as the danger sense feels it; runes are read from 12 blocks.

## Instiller

**Instiller**: enhances objects with mana. Instill runes last half again as long; weapon arts and set bonuses +20% and beast-core sockets +25% (when the Armoury reads them).

An Alacryan battlegroup is built from these roles: Strikers charge under the Shield's cover, Casters strike from behind, a Sentry watches and an Instiller arms them.
