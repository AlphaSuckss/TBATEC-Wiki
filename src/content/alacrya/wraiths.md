## Agrona's Hunters

Agrona's private army, that most Alacryans think a myth: half-blood basilisks, born to basilisks and Vritra-blooded Alacryans, with red eyes, long canines and horns. They hunt in <span class="thing">squads of five</span>, and one squad can kill an accomplished asuran warrior. A tempus warp is their mark.

The playable Wraith race is the mod's own and has nothing to do with them.

## The Squad

**Perhata**: leads (900 health): volleys of black spikes, Black Lightning, Blood Iron Golems<br>**Ulrike**: Black Lightning, and one huge bolt from the sky<br>**Ifiok**: Blood Iron Golems<br>**Richmal**: Bile Water; his black Wraith Plate mends itself<br>**Valeska**: Shadow Meld: gone, and behind you

650 health each; armour 18, blows of 18.

## The Hunt

**Agrona's hunt**: raised in Dicathen, kill a Retainer or a Scythe and a squad finds you at night one to three days later: once for every kill, one squad at a time, never in Epheotus.

They fight as one: they share their quarry and whoever strikes any of them; the casters keep their distance. The last of a squad, below a fifth of its health, escapes by tempus warp with nothing left behind: a squad beaten that far does not come for you again, and the next is a day off at least. One that only lost sight of you does come again.

## Spoils

**Perhata**: always her Tempus Warp<br>**Each**: a Wraith Plate piece 40%, their weapon 30%, a Vritra Horn 50%

Their magic cannot touch someone of the Wraith race: against one they fight with their weapons and their conjured iron. Raised in Alacrya, they leave you be. A squad also keeps Taegrin Caelum: that one never warps out.

Black Lightning and Void Wind are the Vritra runes' spells: the Vritra-blooded cast them just as a bearer of the rune would, without the rune.
