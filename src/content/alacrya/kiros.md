## The Challenge

**Raised in Dicathen** (or a rebel): talk to him, or strike him in his box, and he gives you a show.<br>**Raised in Alacrya**: only the Victoriad's champion may go down to the sand against him: his trial, which ends at a tenth of his health.

Silver core or better (or an asura, a wraith, one of the reborn).

## Three Phases

First <span class="thing">the spectacle</span>: he stays in his shielded box and looses two waves of corrupted beasts through the gates while decay rains on the sand. Kill them and he comes down himself.

**The Host**: Void Wind; decay falling in columns; the weight of a Sovereign (leave his reach or jump)<br>**Bread and Circuses**: (two thirds) beasts and two champions on the sand; Black Lightning<br>**The Craven**: (a third) he flees to his box; a tide of void wind with one gap

## The Crowd

&#8203;<span class="thing">The crowd</span> has its own bar. Clean blows (while he has not hurt you) win it; his hits lose it.

**With you**: he is rattled (takes more), and the crowd throws you a healing flask now and then<br>**With him**: he basks: he heals and his arts come quicker

In his box half of every blow reaches him; four blows, or twelve seconds of the crowd's jeers, drive him down.

## Spoils

4,800 health. Brought down, he flees the sand and is back in his box three days later.

**Spoils**: a horn fragment; sometimes a piece of Granbehl Silver, Kalon's Spear or a dimension ring. The kingdoms' regard (or, for his trial, the bloods'), and the title <span class="thing">Humbler of Kiros</span>.
