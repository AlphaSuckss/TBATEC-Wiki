## Cecilia

&#8203;<span class="thing">Cecilia</span>, the Legacy, brought back by Agrona in <span class="thing">Tessia Eralith</span>'s body: Tessia's face and hair with runes of warm light over the skin. She commands every element, flies, pulls mana out of the air and out of a mage's core and sets it hard. She keeps a chamber in the spire of Taegrin Caelum.

## Cecilia

Tessia's body in emerald battle robes, runes glowing on her arms.

## Mana Answers to Her

**Your spells feed her**: a spell bolt that comes near her is taken apart; spell damage that lands is about a third of itself. Both fill her <span class="thing">store</span>, which she throws back at whoever fed her most.

**What works**: blows of the body (blades, fists, arrows) land in full. The aether is no mana: it lands harder.

## Her Arts

**Volley**: fire, water, wind and earth in turn (and decay from two thirds)<br>**Cage**: warm motes close on where you stood: move, or be held<br>**Draw**: she drinks mana from the air: strike her body now, half again as hard; hit her hard enough and it breaks<br>**Drain**: (two thirds) a cord to your core: break line of sight

## Saturation

From two thirds she <span class="thing">flies</span>.

At one third comes the <span class="thing">great draw</span>: five seconds of the whole chamber's mana pulled into her. Deal enough blows of the body or the aether and it breaks, and something in Tessia holds her still for five seconds, open to everything. Otherwise it bursts on everyone she can see: hide behind a pylon.

## Spoils

3,600 health. She is not slain: beaten, she withdraws, and returns three days later.

**Spoils**: a piece of the <span class="thing">Emerald Battle Robes</span>; the first time, the Battle Robe itself.

Raised in Alacrya, she lets you be unless you have turned rebel.
