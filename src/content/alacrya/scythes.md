## The Scythes

The six <span class="thing">Scythes</span> command Alacrya's dominions under the High Sovereign: all Vritra-blooded but Nico. Each fights in three phases, at two thirds and one third, and seals the fight round its seat.

**Raised in Dicathen**: a fight to the death<br>**Raised in Alacrya**: a trial that ends at a tenth of her health

Above them all, [the High Sovereign](/alacrya/high_sovereign).

## Melzri

Scythe of Etril: white scale, white hair, black horns, two swords.

## Melzri

Bone-pale armour of small arrow-shaped scales, bright white hair, two pairs of black horns, and two long curved swords, one wrapped in black wind and one in dark fire.

**Black Wind**: fans of Void Wind; a cone of dark fire close in<br>**Void Step**: (two thirds) she steps through her black wind to your side; a gale throws you back<br>**Both Blades Burning**: (one third) her cuts burn, she is faster, and she dashes through you

## Melzri

2,800 health; her arena holds 22 blocks round her seat.

**Spoils**: <span class="thing">Melzri's Fang</span>, a piece of <span class="thing">Melzri's Bonescale</span>, one or two Vritra Horns. A trial won gives the same without the horns, and her <span class="thing">writ</span>: what a Named blood needs to rise to Highblood.

## Seats and Writs

Each Scythe's retainer stands between you and the Scythe: Lyra before Cadell, Echeron before Dragoth, Jagrette before Viessa (raised in Alacrya: win the retainer's trial first). Every Scythe's trial gives its <span class="thing">writ</span>: the Obsidian Vault asks for one.

## Cadell

Scythe of the Central Dominion: ash-grey, red eyes, black armour.

## Cadell

The strongest of them, in Taegrin Caelum: black skin and plate, a serpentine face, serrated horns, two swords.

**Soulfire**: crescents of black fire; Void Wind; Blood Iron<br>**Wings**: (two thirds) wings of soulfire: he fights from the air and dives on you<br>**Black Rain**: (one third) soulfire falls round you

3,200 health; Cadell's Blade, a Cadell's Plate piece, Vritra Horns.

## Dragoth

Scythe of Vechor: over seven feet, bald, bearded, bull horns.

## Dragoth

The Titan of Vechor, at the Victoriad. His warhammer, wrapped in void wind, <span class="thing">breaks shields and wards</span>; his black shield turns blows from the front; he leaps through the air onto you, and late in the fight the ground breaks round him.

After each hammer blow he is <span class="thing">staggered</span>: strike his head then (from above his shoulders, or from the air) and a <span class="thing">horn snaps</span>, his blows a quarter weaker. Mica did the same.

## Dragoth

3,000 health; Dragoth's Hammer, Vritra Horns.

## Viessa

Scythe of Truacia: very pale, long purple hair, robes hung with bones.

## Viessa

Illusions and the dead. Her <span class="thing">doubles</span> look and move as she does, but cast nothing, their blows land on no one, and one blow undoes one: only the real Viessa <span class="thing">throws a shadow</span>. She raises the dead round you as puppets; later her visions blind and sicken, and a tether of decay feeds her.

2,400 health; a Viessa's Robes piece, Vritra Horns.

## Seris

Scythe of Sehz-Clar: slender, pearl-white hair, long spiral horns.

## Seris

"The Unblooded", who rebelled against the High Sovereign. She is no one's foe: she fights only in a duel she agrees to (Void Wind, a shell of it round her, a step through it).

**Raised in Dicathen**: ask her for a duel (a silver core or better); win it for a Battledress piece<br>**Raised in Alacrya**: hear her out: win Cylrit's trial, then bring her a Tempus Warp taken from Agrona's own Wraiths; a Battledress piece and her writ

## Nico Sever

Scythe of the Central Dominion: black clothes, no horns.

## Nico Sever

The only Scythe without Vritra blood (no horn to take), in Taegrin Caelum with his Charwood staff. Volleys of stone missiles, lines of earth spikes bursting toward you, and from two thirds metal spikes and two sentinels he makes on the spot.

2,400 health; Nico's Staff.
