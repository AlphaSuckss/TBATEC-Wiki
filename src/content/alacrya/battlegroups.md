## Five Roles

Alacrya's mages fight in teams, each soldier in the [role](/alacrya/roles) its runes name: Strikers close in, Casters fight at range, Shields cover the Striker, Sentries sense and scout, Instillers work mana into weapons. A team is a <span class="thing">battlegroup</span>.

Etril's soldiers wear a black quilted gambeson cut open down the spine, so the black runes on their backs show, under a red tabard.

## Ranks

A soldier's <span class="thing">rank</span> is the tier of the runes it carries, drawn from the rune table's soldiers' runes ([\*](/alacrya/rune_index)) by its role:

**Marked**: 36 health, 6 a blow, armour 10; one or two marks<br>**Crested**: 70, 9, 14; a crest and a mark<br>**Emblem**: 160, 12, 16; emblem, crest, mark: the officers<br>**Regalia**: 420, 16, 18: rare Central commanders

Their runes strike at three quarters of a rune's power.

## Reading the Fight

A rune glows on the back as its spell is cast: watch the spine. The rune that fires flashes in its school's colour a moment before the spell comes. Sneak and look at a bare back to read its runes.

When an officer falls each of the rest may break and run; a Caster or Sentry left with no front withdraws.

## Patrols

**Etril**: 2 Strikers, a Caster, a Shield and a Sentry, all Marked; sometimes 1-2 corrupted beasts<br>**Truacia, Vechor, Sehz-Clar**: an Emblem officer, Crested Striker and Shield, Marked Caster, Sentry and Instiller<br>**Central**: an Emblem officer, five Crested and a corrupted class A beast; one in ten led by a Regalia

Patrols walk Alacrya's wilds, never within 96 blocks of a city, at most two near you; they move on when you leave.

## Friend or Foe

**Raised in Dicathen**: soldiers at beachheads, the Wall's siege camps and war camps attack on sight. In the wilds a patrol lets you be until its <span class="thing">Sentry</span> feels your core: half again as far as a mage would, or, suppressed (J), within 8 blocks. Its <span class="thing">Reveal</span> strips invisibility, Mirage Walk, a will's Fade and suppression for 6 s, 24 blocks round.

## Friend or Foe

**Raised in Alacrya**: the army is your ally. But cast a rune within 24 blocks of Dicathen's guards or knights, or let one within 8 blocks see the runes on your bare back, and you are <span class="thing">exposed</span>: they hunt you for 60 s.

**In towns** the guards and ascenders let everyone be, until you attack anyone there or, raised in Dicathen, cast a spell. Dicathen's guards and Alacrya's soldiers always fight.

## Stray Blows

**A stray blow**: raised in Alacrya, hit an allied soldier once (a wide spell catching them, say) and they shrug it off with a warning. Hit them again within 10 s and they and their squad turn on you.

## War-Camp Work

Raised in Alacrya, talk to a war camp's <span class="thing">Camp Officer</span> (any soldier there points you to them) for work. **Escort**: walk a supply courier and its pack donkey down the road toward the nearest city to a relay post (green sparkles), up to 140 blocks. The courier waits while you are over 20 blocks away; part way an ambush comes and the courier takes cover until you clear it.

**Defence**: hold the camp against three waves with its soldiers.

## War-Camp Work

Waves and ambushes are corrupted beasts or, as you were raised in Alacrya, a Dicathen raiding party of adventurers and Sapin knights. A boss bar shows how it stands.

**Pay**: favour with the camp's blood (escort 60, defence 90) and 5 or 8 gold per tier on your runecard. It fails if the courier falls, or you die, leave for 30 s or run out of time. One at a time; 10 minutes before the next (2 after a failure).

## Escorts

The work is yours alone: its foes never come within 24 blocks of other players, never turn on them and cannot hurt them.

**Vale's battlegroups**: <span class="thing">Trusted</span> with Blood Vale, hire a Shield, a Striker and a Caster (Crested once Allied) from a Vale noble or an Etril camp officer: 20 gold for 10 minutes, once every 20. They follow you in Alacrya, fight what you fight and what hunts you, and cannot be hurt by you. Talk to one to send them back.
