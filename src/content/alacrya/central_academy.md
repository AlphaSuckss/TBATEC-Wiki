## The Academy

Alacrya's great school, in <span class="thing">Cargidan</span> in the Central Dominion, a walled campus north of Taegrin Caelum (about -140, -510). Its colours are azure and black.

Talk to <span class="thing">the Dean</span> in the Great Hall. He takes only the **Alacryan-born**: 60 gold off your runecard, or nothing for a Named or High blood's child. He gives you the academy's azure cloak.

## Lessons

A student takes **one lesson a day**:

**Theory**: your core's refinement deepens (a fifth of a shade)<br>**Practice**: your core's capacity stretches (+0.5%)

After seven lessons you <span class="thing">graduate</span>: the horned half-mask, a title, an advancement.

## The Reliquary

East of the quad, the <span class="thing">reliquary</span>: dead relics of the ancient mages in glass cases. Its <span class="thing">keeper</span> lets out one duplicate a day, its depth by chance:

**Depth 1**: 40 gold<br>**Depth 2**: 80<br>**Depth 3**: 150<br>**Depth 4**: 260<br>Students pay half. Aroa's Requiem can wake a dead relic.
