## Purgatory

Once Khaernos is unmasked, the next time you walk in Taegrin Caelum Ji-ae drops you into a <span class="thing">fold</span> of space under it: a black room whose four ways run on and on and bring you back in at the other side.

## The Seams

The fold's skin has six <span class="thing">seams</span>. One glows at a time, for four seconds, somewhere on the walls, then hides and another shows. **Use** each while it glows. Unpick all six inside four minutes and the fold opens: you have learnt to fold space.

Three lost rounds or a death and she lets you go; she takes you again ten minutes later. Nothing can be broken or built there.

## Your Fold

The <span class="item">Fold Key</span> is yours when you leave. **Use** it to step into your own fold: a room **10 by 10 by 10**, yours alone, to build in and keep chests in. Use it inside to step back out where you left.

**A mage pays**: 15% of their whole mana<br>**The reborn pay**: 30 aether<br>Not in the Relictombs, the aether orb or a boss fight.

## The Trap

**Sneak and use** it aimed at a creature within 12 blocks to fold it into a pocket of its own for **15 s**: a shell of motes holds it there. It takes 1.25x damage and deals 0.65x. Once every 2 minutes.

**A mage pays**: 40% of their whole mana<br>**The reborn pay**: 100 aether

## Lords

&#8203;<span class="thing">Lord Indrath</span> and <span class="thing">Agrona</span> in their true forms are weakened worse, cut off from the world they draw on: 1.5x damage, and they cannot heal while folded.

**From the books**: Agrona twisting Taegrin Caelum is laced with trap pockets of folded space, and Arthur fought Kezess and Agrona inside one. **Invented**: purgatory, the seams, the key, the pocket and the trap.
