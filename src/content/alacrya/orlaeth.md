## Orlaeth Vritra

&#8203;<span class="thing">Orlaeth Vritra</span>, Sovereign of Sehz-Clar, has two heads, each under a mop of dark hair with two horns on its outer side; the left is the elder, with two stubs on its inner side. He runs two minds at once, feels what others feel, reads their minds and bends their will through the fear they bear him, and he flies. Seris took him to power her barrier.

## The Vault

He waits in the <span class="thing">barrier vault</span>, hollowed in the rock under Seris's seat: a tunnel leads in from the foot of the rock's west face.

**Two heads, two bars**: aim at a head to hurt it (strike or shoot toward that side of him). Bring one to nothing and it hangs slack and its arts stop.

## His Minds

**The elder (left)**: dread when you meet its eyes (look away); a compulsion that walks you toward him (crouch, or break his sight); a sidestep when it reads your blow coming<br>**The younger (right)**: Void Wind, a withering breath, Black Lightning<br>**Both**: from a third they cast together, and he takes to the air

## The Pylons

&#8203;<span class="thing">Barrier pylons</span> rise from their sockets as the fight opens (four) and at each new phase (two more). Each takes a share off every blow that reaches him and feeds him back a little; cords of light run to him, and from two thirds they can burn along their length: stand off the lines.

Break them all and the backlash stuns him.

## Spoils

5,600 health, half in each head. Taken, he is dragged off; three days later he holds the vault again.

**Spoils**: horn fragments; sometimes a piece of Seris's Battledress, Caera's pendant or a black stone ring; the title <span class="thing">Barrier-breaker</span>.
