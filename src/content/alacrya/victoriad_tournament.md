Vechor's great tournament, held in its coliseum before tens of thousands. The bloods, the academies and the Ascenders send their fighters; pairings are drawn; it opens with beasts loosed on the field, and to interfere is death.

## The Herald

He keeps the roll by the champions' gate.

## Registering

A tournament opens every two days at noon when someone is near the arena, or when you ask the <span class="thing">herald</span> on the champions' plaza. Registration runs a minute: at the herald, or with the buttons sent to everyone in Alacrya.

Register <span class="thing">open to player bouts</span> to be drawn against other players who chose the same (never your own party; only where the server allows player fights).

## Bouts

Eight places; the bloods' and Ascenders' champions take the rest. Each round is drawn as it opens; champions' bouts among themselves are told by the herald.

Called, come down to the sand. A bout ends at a fifth of a fighter's health (no one dies), after three minutes, or when one leaves the sand. Blows from anyone else are stopped.

## Champions

A champion of the bloods.

## Prizes

**Champion**: 150 gold, the bloods' regard, a prize (Kalon's Spear or crest, the Ascender Suit, a ring), the title<br>**Second**: 70 gold and a prize<br>**Semi-final**: 35 gold<br>**First round**: 10 gold<br>**The crowd's favourite**: 40 gold, a title

Then Kiros calls the champion down to the sand.
