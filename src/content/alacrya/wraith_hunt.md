## Hunted

Agrona's Wraiths kill asura. Grow strong enough and he sends a squad after you: a <span class="thing">captain</span> and four Wraiths.

**Who**: a White core or past it, a grown asura, the Wraith race or the reborn<br>**Renown**: 4 or more: Retainers 1, Scythes 2, the Legacy 3, Agrona 4, guild rank A+

## Wraith

A hunting squad's Wraith: horns, red eyes, black plate.

## Warning

Rare: a chance every twenty minutes of play, never within four days of the last. First you are <span class="thing">watched</span>, for thirty seconds. Then black light tears the air near you.

Never in Epheotus, the Relictombs, near the world spawn, inside a settlement or site, asleep, or on peaceful. Raised in Alacrya and loyal, never at all.

## The Captain

The captain carries Agrona's eye. Below a quarter of his health the runes in him <span class="thing">ignite</span>: three seconds of burning and a count, then he blows apart (34 at the heart, 10 at eight blocks), leaving nothing.

Kill him first: he falls with his <span class="thing">Tempus Warp</span>, his gear and a deed.
