## The Mask

The first time you climb to the throne hall, the one waiting there is not the High Sovereign: it is <span class="thing">Khaernos Vritra</span>, the Central Dominion's unseen Sovereign, made into Agrona's double and worn by him. He looks the part and fights with his arts, but weaker: **5,000 health**, an asura warrior's strength, the arts at six tenths.

## Unmasked

Bring Khaernos down and the mask slips: the face goes slack, and Agrona laughs from somewhere under the fortress, where his own body waited all along. The deed <span class="thing">unmasked</span> is yours and its advancement; spoils as for Agrona but no dagger and a third of the experience.

From then on, the High Sovereign meets you himself ([\*](/alacrya/high_sovereign)).

## Lore

**From the books**: Khaernos, the Invisible Sovereign, as the body double Agrona wore through most of the story while his own body lay hidden beneath Taegrin Caelum.

**Invented**: that he stands in for your first fight, and his numbers.
