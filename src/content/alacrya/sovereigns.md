Each of Alacrya's dominions has a <span class="thing">Sovereign</span>: a full-blooded basilisk of the Vritra clan who rules it for Agrona, above its Scythe. Vechor has [Kiros](/alacrya/kiros), Sehz-Clar [Orlaeth](/alacrya/orlaeth), Truacia [Oludari](/alacrya/oludari).

They are stronger than any Scythe, and far below the High Sovereign.

## Where

**Kiros**: in the Sovereigns' box of the Victoriad, west in Vechor: a challenge, or the Victoriad's crown<br>**Orlaeth**: in the barrier vault under Seris's seat, south in Sehz-Clar: a tunnel in the rock's west face<br>**Oludari**: hiding at the foot of Viessa's seat, north in Truacia: a hunt, not a duel

## Who They Fight

Raised in Dicathen, or an Alacryan who carried Seris's rebellion through: they are your foes. A loyal Alacryan is let be (Kiros offers his champion a trial; Oludari's hunt is the High Sovereign's own errand).

They are <span class="thing">not slain</span>: beaten, they withdraw, and hold their seats again three days later. Everyone who dealt a real share of the fight takes the spoils. One blow never carries them past a phase, but a godrune's can.
