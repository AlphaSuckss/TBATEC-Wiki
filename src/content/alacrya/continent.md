## The Continent

Across the sea from Dicathen lies <span class="thing">Alacrya</span>, the continent the Vritra clan rules: basilisk asura, exiled there long ago, under the High Sovereign <span class="thing">Agrona</span> in his spire, Taegrin Caelum.

In the game it is a land of its own (a dimension, like Epheotus): a continent some 5,500 blocks from north to south and 4,700 across, with two great gulfs and a ragged west, ringed by deep ocean. Its shores, hills and borders follow the world's seed; its cities always stand in the same places.

## Dominions

Five dominions, each under a Vritra <span class="thing">Sovereign</span>:

**Central**: the middle, round Taegrin Caelum at (0, 0); Khaernos, the unseen<br>**Truacia**: the mountainous north; Oludari<br>**Etril**: the east: farmland and old roads; Exeges<br>**Sehz-Clar**: the south, the "soft south"; Orlaeth<br>**Vechor**: the west; Kiros. The Victoriad is fought there

## The Map and the Roads

You are handed a <span class="item">Map of Alacrya</span> the first time you stand there (the outfitters sell it too). Use it: the dominions, the mountains, the roads, every place that is built, and in grey the cities the books name that this book does not build (Cargidan, Aedelgard, Nirmala and the rest), so nobody rides out for them.

&#8203;<span class="thing">Roads</span> join every built place; <span class="thing">guideposts</span> along them give both ends with distance and bearing. Roads appear in land made from 1.29 on. The roads are the mod's own.

## The Lands

**Central Dominion**: grey-black ground where tuff and basalt break through thin grass, sparse dark oaks, ash on the wind.<br>**Truacia Highlands**: cold spruce country, colder and higher than the rest; snow lies above about Y 140.<br>**Etril Fields** and **Downs**: hedged fields of wheat and roots, pasture and hay, copses; and rolling oak and birch woods.

**Sehz-Clar Coast**: warm, lush land of olive groves and blossoming orchards, white limestone on its slopes.<br>**Vechor Steppe**: open russet grass to the horizon, horses, a lone acacia here and there; its rivers run red.

The seas, the shores, the high peaks and the caves are as they are anywhere.

## Life

Etril's fields and downs are home to <span class="thing">wogarts</span> and <span class="thing">blood oxen</span>, which its farms raise, to <span class="thing">rocavids</span>, which Maerin's hunters track, and in the woods to the <span class="thing">Maerin maw-bear</span>.

Beds work in Alacrya; respawn anchors don't. Monsters come out in the dark as they do at home.

## Getting There

**Raised in Alacrya**: you wake in [Maerin](/alacrya/maerin) when you choose the path, and Maerin stays where you wake again until you sleep elsewhere (and if that bed is lost, Maerin takes you back).

**Raised in Dicathen**: Alacrya's armies hold <span class="thing">beachheads</span> on Dicathen's beaches, war camps round an Alacryan <span class="thing">war-gate</span>. A Quest Master sells a map to the nearest.

## The War-Gate

The war-gate's veil is a dark, restless sheet. Walk into it: there is no fare. The gates are unstable: you come out anywhere from 6 to 32 blocks from the gate at [Etril Landing](/alacrya/etril_landing), on dry ground, and gate sickness follows as with any gate.

Etril Landing's gate takes you back to the beachhead you came through (or, if you never came that way, the one nearest the world's spawn).

The camp round a beachhead is Alacrya's army: it fights anyone raised in Dicathen. Fight your way to the gate, or suppress your core and slip through.

Inside Alacrya, gates join its cities as they do Dicathen's: tempus warps in Maerin, at Etril Landing, in Aramoor's Ascenders Association and at Stormcove Academy. They lead only to gates in Alacrya.
