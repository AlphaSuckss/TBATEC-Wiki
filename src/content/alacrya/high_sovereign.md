## Taegrin Caelum

The way in: up the spire's terraces, past the Wraiths, through the open door into the [Legacy's chamber](/alacrya/legacy). A stair goes down through its floor into the <span class="thing">throne hall</span>. He will not come down to you while the Legacy stands.

## The Fortress

In a new world the fortress stands on a <span class="thing">mountain</span>, ringed by dark peaks; the way up is a pass south of the gate. Under the spire the plinth holds two floors: the <span class="thing">Obsidian Vault</span>, the Hall of Horns, officers' rooms, kitchens and an armoury below; apartments, a library and the council chamber above; <span class="thing">cells</span> in the north side. Something lies past the last cell. There is no way up to the throne from there.

The rooms and the mountain's shape are the mod's own.

## The Serpent

**Breath**: soulfire gathers in its jaws, then pours out in a cone that turns slowly after you.<br>**Tail**: the floor cracks all round it, then the sweep.<br>**Charge**: a line is scored toward you. Step off it. If it hits a wall it is stunned and takes more.

## His Will in Your Blood

He reaches for everyone near him with his blood: a <span class="thing">Vritra</span> half-blood, an Alacryan whose Vritra blood is woken or latent, the [Wraith race](/heritage/wraiths).

Your heart pounds and red motes rise off you: you have **two seconds**. He sees through your eyes, so <span class="thing">look away</span> or put a wall between you.

Held, your eyes are turned on him, you are dragged toward him, and your hands do nothing. Each press of <span class="thing">sneak</span> struggles some of it off.

**Latent blood**: 1.5 s<br>**Wraith race**: 2.5 s<br>**Woken Vritra blood**: 3 s<br>**Half-blood**: 3.5 s<br>**His true form**: +1 s<br>Then fifteen seconds before he can reach again.

## A Long Fight

11,000 health: second only to Kezess, a long fight. <span class="thing">His marks hold</span>: no single blow carries him past three quarters, half or a quarter, and at each he gathers himself for a few seconds. Breathe, drink, move.

## Spoils

He is <span class="thing">not slain</span>: brought down, he withdraws into his spire and holds court again three days later.

Everyone who dealt a real share of the damage gets <span class="thing">Agrona's Dagger</span> the first time (a quarter of the time after), sometimes a piece of <span class="thing">Agrona's Dragonscale</span>.
