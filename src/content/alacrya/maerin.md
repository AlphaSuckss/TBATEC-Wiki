**Where**: (1,696, 992), at the southern tip of Etril in what Alacryans call the Eastern Domain.

A walled town round a plaza. On its raised platform the town's children kneel for their first runes at the <span class="thing">bestowal</span>; beside it stands the <span class="thing">Descension Chamber</span>, a small pillared hall where those coming out of the Relictombs are checked by an official.

A colosseum, the rocavid hunters' lodge, an inn, a bank, a smithy and the house of Blood Fairweather.

Alacryan-born players wake on the plaza paving before the bestowal platform. A tempus warp joins Maerin to Alacrya's other gates.

Aramoor, the nearest city, lies some 1,200 blocks north.
