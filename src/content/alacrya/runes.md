## Runes

Every Alacryan mage's magic is written on their back. A rune burns into the skin of the spine and grants one spell; Alacryans know fewer spells than Dicathen's mages, but each is drilled until it is stronger and quicker to cast.

## Tiers

Four tiers, weakest first: **Mark**, **Crest**, **Emblem**, **Regalia**. The tier can be told at a glance: a mark is a single stroke, a crest has a crown-like top, an emblem is enclosed, a regalia is ornate.

Ordinary runes are black and glow when their spell is cast. Anyone who has studied runes can read a bare back: sneak and look at an Alacryan from behind or the side, within 6 blocks (a Sentry reads from 12). Some ascenders cover theirs to hide their strength; on the Spine you choose to bare or cover yours (covered at first). A covered back, or one under armour, shows only a band of light along the spine as a rune fires, and cannot be read.

## The Spine

The first rune sits just above the tailbone and the rest climb the spine: marks lowest, then crests, emblems, and the regalia between the shoulder blades.

**Every spine**: 4 marks, 2 crests, 1 emblem, 1 regalia<br>**Orange core**: +1 mark<br>**Silver core**: +1 crest<br>**White core**: +1 emblem<br>**Named standing**: +1 mark<br>**Highblood standing**: +1 crest<br>At most 6 / 4 / 2 / 1: thirteen runes.

## Standing

Standing comes from the bloods: your own blood Named (or Allied with a Named Blood) is Named standing; a Highblood likewise.

Slots are only room: runes come from bestowals. Open the Spine (the Workshop key) to see your runes, pick one and **Put on bar**; right-click a bar slot to clear it. Rune spells cannot be edited, copied or deleted.

## Numbers

A rune's numbers are drilled at its tier's core: a mark at Red's, a crest at Yellow's, an emblem at Silver's, a regalia at White's, with power x1.15, the shortest cooldown x1.25 and the Workshop's price x0.8. A rune too dear for its tier is drilled leaner (Marks stay under 38 mana).

Your core adds +8% power for every colour above the rune's, up to +32%. A rune costing more than your whole pool waits until your core can feed it.

## Casting and Deepening

Runes are quick and cast with little backlash: a rune's mana counts only half toward overfilling your core. Running dry still brings Mana Backlash.

A rune can rise one tier along its **line** (Fire Spark, then Crescent Ember, Sun Flare and Hell's Rain): a **deepening**, earned from the bloods, a dais trial or a Retainer beaten, moves it into a free slot of the next tier at any bestowal dais.
