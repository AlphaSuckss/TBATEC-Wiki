## The Taint

The beasts the Vritra have tainted are the <span class="thing">corrupted</span> beasts Dicathen already knows. In the dungeons under the Widow's Crypt the beasts grew stronger, and the Hades Serpent there wore black flames, a black horn and black scales, and had no core. In the war the Alacryans drove corrupted packs against the Wall.

## What Changes

**Look**: decay wisps of smoke and black-violet dust; a darker body and a violet glow on beasts with no corrupted coat of their own

**Strength**: a class higher, x1.8 health, x1.6 blows, no core

**Bite**: its blows wither (Wither I, 3 s)

## Where and Whose

**Where**: one in ten hostile class C-A beasts born in Alacrya, and every one an Alacryan camp keeps; never an S-class beast.

**Handled**: the camps' and patrols' beasts never turn on Alacryans or the Alacryan-raised; they keep with their group, and its Sentry sets them on its prey.

The <span class="thing">Black-horned Mutant</span> roams the Central Dominion's wilds, one at a time.
