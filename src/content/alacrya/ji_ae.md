## Ji-ae

What is left of a <span class="thing">djinn</span> mind, once the Relictombs' index, kept in a crystal in Taegrin Caelum by the cells. Bound to the fortress, she feels all that moves through it and far past it.

Talk to her with **Revered** standing with the Vritra (600 and up) and she will <span class="thing">seek</span> for you: any of the mod's places (towns, sites, ruins, gates), or any named person of the story, wherever they stand. She gives the bearing and the distance.

## Her Shard
