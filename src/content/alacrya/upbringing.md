## Two Upbringings

When your core awakens on the reveal screen, a human mage chooses how they were raised: in <span class="thing">Dicathen</span>, taught by the mages of the three kingdoms, or in <span class="thing">Alacrya</span>, across the sea under the Vritra. The choice is for life.

Coreless people, elves, dwarves, the asura and wraiths are raised in Dicathen (server owners can open the choice to elves and dwarves).

## What Changes

**Raised in Dicathen**: you shape your own spells in the Spell Workshop, your core awakens as an augmenter or a conjurer, and you learn the mana arts (Body Augmentation, Iron Skin, Burst Step).

**Raised in Alacrya**: your magic is bestowed. Runes on your spine each grant one spell; you have no class but a [role](/alacrya/roles), and a blood whose name outweighs your own. You begin in Maerin, at the southern tip of Etril, and the first bestowal is free.

## Blood

Name your blood when you choose Alacrya: one word, or your own name if you leave it blank. People will call you "of Blood ..." before your given name.

&#8203;<span class="thing">Vritra blood</span>: a few Alacryans carry Vritra blood without knowing it. If yours manifests (it can only once your core reaches Silver), small black horns grow through your hair, which Caera's Pendant hides, and runes of the Vritra lines can be bestowed on you.

## Older Worlds

Worlds from before 1.27: a mage whose core had already awakened stays raised in Dicathen. If you have never cast a spell, a button in chat offers the Alacryan upbringing once.
