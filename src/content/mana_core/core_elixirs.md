## Brewing

A beast core brewed under a water bottle in a <span class="item">brewing stand</span> makes a <span class="item">Core Elixir</span>: lesser from E-C cores, common from B-AA, greater from S, SS and asuran.

Drink one every five minutes. Each adds <span class="thing">potency</span>, up to 100, for small boosts that last.

## Potency

At 100 potency: +4 max health, +5% mana, +5% recovery (in proportion below).

Each class carries potency only so far:<br>**E, D, C**: +1, 1.5, 2: up to 10, 18, 26<br>**B, A, AA**: +3, 4, 6: up to 36, 48, 62<br>**S, SS**: +8, 10: up to 78, 90<br>**Asuran**: +14: up to 100

## Grades

A beast you kill drops its core <span class="thing">graded</span> by purity: Clouded (4 in 10), Clear, Bright or, 1 in 20, Flawless. The grade scales an elixir's potency (x0.5 to x2.5) and how much a core [purifies](/mana_core/purification) a peak White core. Cores from traders and chests are ungraded: Clear.

*The elixirs and grades are invented.*
