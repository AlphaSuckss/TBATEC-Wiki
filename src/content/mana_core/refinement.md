Your core grows by use. Every point of mana you spend on a spell that goes off, in survival, <span class="thing">refines</span> it by two. The bar under the workshop's preview shows how far along the shade you are; when it fills, your core moves up a shade and the rest carries over.

## What Counts

Upkeep on a toggle counts a quarter. Mana spent on flight and mana paid to a gate don't count at all. [Meditation](/mana_core/meditation) refines a core too, without spending anything.

Refinement needed for each next step, Black to Silver:<br>**Black**: 2,500 / 3,375 / 4,556<br>**Red**: 6,151 / 8,304 / 11,210<br>**Orange**: 15,134 / 20,430 / 27,581<br>**Yellow**: 37,234 / 50,266 / 67,860<br>**Silver**: 91,610 / 123,674 / 166,960

## Beast Cores

All told, about 637,000 refinement (some 318,000 mana spent) lies between dark Black and White: the trials, not the grind, are what hold you back.

The fastest road is the cores of mana beasts: hold use with a [beast core](/items/beast_cores) to absorb it, from 4% of a shade (E-class) to twice a shade (asuran).

Refinement stops at the end of each colour until you pass its [trial](/mana_core/trials).

A White core refines no further. At its peak, only S-class cores and stronger can still be absorbed: they [purify](/mana_core/purification) it toward integration.
