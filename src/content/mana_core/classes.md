## Two Ways of Mana

The moment a core forms it settles how your body handles mana, an even chance either way.

**Augmenters** draw mana inward through their veins: stronger, faster and tougher than other mages, they fight with body and blade.

**Conjurers** gather ambient mana and shape it outward into ranged spells, barriers and fields.

## Disciplines

Every spell belongs to a discipline, and each class opens it at a colour. A spell needs the later of that and its own core:

**Pure Mana arts**: Augmenter Black, Conjurer White<br>**Infusions**: Augmenter Orange, Conjurer White. Thunderclap Impulse

**Waves**: Augmenter Orange, Conjurer Black. Cone, Circle, Wind Gust, Frost Nova, Absolute Zero, Sonic Boom, Steel Spires, Thorn Lash<br>**Barriers**: Augmenter Silver, Conjurer Orange. Dome, Wall, Mirror Ward<br>**Ranged**: Augmenter Silver, Conjurer Black. Everything else<br>**Gifts**: both Black

## Augmenter

Below Silver, an augmenter's elemental spells reach no more than 8 blocks; at Silver, 16; at White, any distance. The Pure Mana arts and gifts aren't held back.

A Black or Red augmenter therefore fights with the Pure Mana arts (and any gift), and works its element into body and blade from Orange.

## Conjurer

Before each instant spell a conjurer gathers mana, slowed while it does (and while it channels):

**Black, Red**: 0.6 s, at 40% speed<br>**Orange, Yellow**: 0.3 s, at 70% speed<br>**Silver**: instant for spells of Orange or lower; 0.2 s at 85% speed for the rest<br>**White**: never

## A Refined Body

From **Silver**, augmenter and conjurer alike are stronger even at rest: +3 attack, +2 armour, 6% faster, 8% quicker blows, a little steadier and lighter on their feet; an augmenter half again as much, and a little from **Yellow**. At **White** both grow to +6 attack, +4 armour, 10% faster and 15% quicker blows.

At **White** the divide disappears: every art of both paths is open to you, every spell casts instantly, and you can [fly](/mana_core/flight).

In the workshop, hover a shape to see when each class can learn it.
