Press <kbd title="Meditate (sit / rise)">M</kbd> to sit down cross-legged and meditate, drawing the mana around you into your core. Moving, jumping, sneaking, attacking, casting or taking a hit breaks it; press the key again to rise.

While you meditate your mana refills three times as fast, your core refines, and its capacity grows. A mana backlash settles twice as fast.

## Refining

The purer the core, the slower it refines. Minutes of meditation alone to fill each shade:

**Black**: 7 / 8 / 10<br>**Red**: 11 / 13 / 15<br>**Orange**: 18 / 21 / 25<br>**Yellow**: 29 / 34 / 39<br>**Silver**: 46 / 54 / 63

A breakthrough trial still holds the core at the end of each colour.

## Capacity

Magic used develops a core. Every full pool of mana you spend grows its capacity by 0.1% of the pool; meditation grows it too, 1.2% a minute at dark Black, slowing to 0.13% at high Silver. Growth carries over as the core climbs, up to a cap that rises with it:

**Black**: +10% to +13%<br>**Red**: +15% to +18%<br>**Orange**: +20% to +23%<br>**Yellow**: +25% to +28%<br>**Silver**: +30% to +33%<br>**White**: +35%

Press <kbd title="Character Screen">I</kbd> for your character screen: race, class and affinities, your core's colour with its shades (the one you are on lit), refinement to the next, your mana and how fast it refills, capacity grown, meditation and Mana Rotation.
