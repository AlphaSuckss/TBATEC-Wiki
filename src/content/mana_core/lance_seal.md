## Virion

The asura sent Virion new artifacts made without the limiter \[lore\]. Besides his manor in Zestier, Virion now keeps to the refugees' town hall in the [Djinn Sanctuary](/sites/djinn_sanctuary) under Darv \[invented: his place there\]. Speak to him there and he gives you one.

## The Mending

Take off the old artifact, hold the new one in your off hand, and meditate: five minutes mend the scar, and the artifact is spent. Lost it? He has another.
