## Purity

A White core at its peak can grow no larger, only purer. <span class="thing">Purity</span> (0-100) shows on the character screen.

**Meditation**: with Mana Rotation: 100 in three hours of it alone<br>**Beast cores**: absorb an S-class core: +4, SS +7, asuran +12, times its grade<br>**Casting**: from a pool at least 90% full: +2 for each full pool spent

A [core's grade](/mana_core/core_elixirs) scales what it gives: Clouded x0.5, Clear x1, Bright x1.5, Flawless x2.5. Lesser cores give a White core nothing.

Half-bloods gain everything half again. A sealed Lance artifact worn halves it, as it halves refinement. A sealed core stops at 40.

## The Rite

At 100, with everything else in place, meditate with a pool at least 95% full. A five-second threshold: rise to refuse. Then **six waves**, ten seconds apart, each pushing 12% of your pool out into your body.

Each wave may <span class="thing">fracture</span> the core: 6% each, so about 69 in 100 come through all six.

## Fracture

Anything that breaks the meditation once the waves begin fractures it too: moving, casting, a blow. Do it somewhere safe.

**A fracture** is a backlash: 8 to 18 damage through armour (more at later waves), the pool emptied, [Mana Backlash](/mana_core/backlash), weakness and slowness, 25 purity lost, and ten minutes before the core can try again.
