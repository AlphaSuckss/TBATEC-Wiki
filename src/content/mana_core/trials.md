A shade-up within a colour comes freely. Breaking through to a new <span class="thing">colour</span> does not: your core strains against its limit, and your refinement waits at the threshold, until you have proven yourself in the world. A reminder over your hotbar names what's missing.

## The Trials

**To Red**: First Incantation (cast a spell) and Glades Hunter (slay a mana beast)

**To Orange**: Spellwright (shape a spell in the workshop) and Beast Core (claim a beast core)

**To Yellow**: A-Class Threat (slay an A-class beast) and Bound by Contract (bind a beast)

**To Silver**: Beyond the Guild's Rank (slay an AA-class beast) and Into the Dire Tombs

**To White**: Worth Ten Adventurers (slay an S-class beast) and Heart of the Elderwood (defeat the Elderwood Guardian)

These are TBATEC advancements of the same names (see the advancements screen). The Adventurers' Guild in Sapin sells a map to the Dire Tombs. Server owners can change the trials in the <span class="thing">breakthroughTrials</span> settings.

## Raised in Alacrya

A mage <span class="thing">raised in Alacrya</span> is proven Alacrya's way: by the rune on their spine, the Relictombs, the bloods and the Victoriad.

**To Red**: Bestowed (bear a mark) and First Incantation (cast through it)

**To Orange**: Read Deeper (have a rune deepened at a dais) and Wogart in Waiting (sign the Ascenders' roll in Aramoor)

**To Yellow**: Wogart (come out of your first ascent with a badge) and Blood Allies (be called Allied by a blood)

**To Silver**: A Crowned Rune (bear a crest) and Horns Lowered (beat one of Vritra blood)

**To White**: Emblem Bearer (bear an emblem) and It Comes Back (win Echeron's trial on the Victoriad's sand)

**Horns Lowered**: enter a Victoriad tournament in Vechor. Until you have the deed, the first champion drawn against you is a horned scion of Vritra blood. The trial of a Scythe or of Uto counts too.

**Echeron** keeps the Victoriad's sand for Silver cores: ask him.
