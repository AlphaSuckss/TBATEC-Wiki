A mage's power is a <span class="thing">mana core</span>: a sphere of mana beside the heart that holds your mana pool and refills it over time. Whether you have one is decided at birth (see [Heritage](/heritage/choosing)).

Without a core there is no mana, no regeneration and no spellcraft.

## Mana

Your mana shows in the orb at the bottom-left of the screen. It refills by itself each second, faster with every rank: in full standing still or holding a sustained art such as Iron Skin, at 0.6x on the move, and not at all while you channel, while a conjurer gathers, or while you fly. [Mana Rotation](/mana_core/rotation) lifts the first two limits, [meditation](/mana_core/meditation) triples the rate, and [suppressing](/mana_core/sensing) your signature halves it.

Spend it all, or too much at once, and your core [backlashes](/mana_core/backlash).

A core grows by [refinement](/mana_core/refinement), rank by rank, from Black to White. Each rank holds more mana, refills faster, and lets you push a spell further: more power, range, speed and duration.

Each colour's last step is a [trial](/mana_core/trials).
