The books know two kinds of backlash: a core run dry, and a core [overfilled](/mana_core/backlash#overfill).

Spend your core down below its limit and it gives out: about 10 mana (a tenth of a small Black pool, 2% of a pool past 500). Your held spells collapse, and for two minutes you are weak (-40% damage), slow (-35% speed), clumsy and quickly hungry, even once the mana is back. It refills at about a third of its speed meanwhile; milk won't help.

## An Asura

An asura's body lives on its mana. It backlashes far sooner, under a tenth of its pool, and refills at a fifth of its speed meanwhile.

If it burns on until less than a hundredth is left, it <span class="thing">starves</span>: no mana comes back by itself, the backlash does not settle, and its health drains every two seconds until mana reaches it from outside (an elixir, a crystal, a pearl) or it dies.

&#8203;<code>After the books, where a phoenix who emptied his core was dying of it; the numbers are the mod's.</code>

## Forcing Magic

Every spell you force through a backlashed core tears at you: a heart, then three, five, seven, more for a spell as big as your pool, and each extends the backlash. Enough of them will kill you.

Rest it off, or meditate to settle it twice as fast. The character screen shows the time left.

## Overfill

Every mana you pay for magic surges through your core, and the surge settles by a quarter of your pool a second. Draw more than **60% of your pool** through it at once (one big spell, or several spells fast) and it <span class="thing">overfills</span>.

Over your orb, **Straining** warns that the surge is three quarters of the way there, and **Overdraw!** that the spell you have selected would overfill it now.

## Worse Each Time

Each overfill within half a minute of the last is worse (the <span class="thing">Overfilled Core</span> effect counts them):

**I**: 1.5 hearts<br>**II**: 3.5 hearts, weak 10 s, held spells collapse<br>**III**: 5.5 hearts, blinded 2 s<br>**IV**: 7.5 hearts: the core ruptures, its mana spills out and Mana Backlash follows

Up to 3 hearts more for a surge far past the limit, through armour. Four in a row can kill.

## Big Spells, Small Cores

A small core feels this soonest: its spells cost a larger share of it. A Black core overfills past about 30 mana at once, a Yellow past about 500, a Silver past 3,000 or more. A spell pushed to its limits just after your core changes colour can cost more than that; a longer cooldown makes it cheaper.

*Notes: in the books conjurers backlash by overfilling and augmenters by running dry (Vol. 1 ch. 3); here every mage can suffer both. The 60% limit, the settling, the warnings and the escalation are ours (invented).*
