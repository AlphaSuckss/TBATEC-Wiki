A core is judged by its colour. From weakest to strongest:

**Black** - <code>Red</code> - Orange - Yellow - <span class="dim">Silver</span> - White

Black to Yellow are refined through three shades each, <span class="thing">dark</span>, <span class="thing">solid</span> and <span class="thing">light</span>. Silver climbs <span class="thing">initial</span>, <span class="thing">mid</span> and <span class="thing">high</span>; White is <span class="thing">pure</span>. Past White lies [integration](/mana_core/integration).

## Mana Pool

Mana held at each shade, and refilled each second:

**Black**: 50 / 58 / 66, 1-1.3<br>**Red**: 150 / 175 / 200, 2.25-2.9<br>**Orange**: 300 / 350 / 400, 4-5.2<br>**Yellow**: 750 / 875 / 1,000, 8.5-11<br>**Silver**: 5,000 / 7,500 / 10,000, 22-29<br>**White**: 25,000, 68 (an asura: 400,000 grown, its recovery in proportion)<br>**Integration**: 54,000, 136

Use and meditation grow a pool further, up to a cap set by its rank: see [Meditation and Capacity](/mana_core/meditation).

## Spell Limits

Each colour raises how far a spell's sliders reach. Against Red, Black gets 0.6x the power, Orange 2x, Yellow 4x, Silver 8x and White 12x; range runs from 0.7x to 4x, speed 0.8x to 2x, duration 0.6x to 8x. Each shade closes a third of the gap to the next colour.

Every shape and art also needs a core strong enough to hold it, and your class may ask for more: see [Augmenters and Conjurers](/mana_core/classes). The workshop's shape list badges anything you can't cast yet with the colour it needs.
