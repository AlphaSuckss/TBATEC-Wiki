## Domain

Tap <kbd title="Integration: Domain (tap) / Presence (hold)">Y</kbd> to raise your <span class="thing">domain</span>: eight blocks of your strongest mastered art round you, for 180 mana a second. A deviant you have awakened (on a mastered element) wins over the plain element.

Every foe inside is weakened, and your own spells of the art hit them a fifth harder (a deviant domain's parent element a tenth).

## The Arts

**Cold**: slows hard, freezes<br>**Gravity**: slows hard, drags fliers down<br>**Fire, magma**: burns (magma also slows)<br>**Wind**: thrusts foes back<br>**Lightning**: a spark leaps to one foe each second<br>**Earth, plant**: holds them<br>**Metal**: weakens further<br>**Water, sound**: slows

Foes are monsters, creatures hunting you, and players the server's PvP rules let you hurt. Its own particles fill the air; there is no circle.

*After Varay's cold and Mica's gravity \[lore\]; the rest is invented.*

## Presence

Hold <kbd title="Integration: Domain (tap) / Presence (hold)">Y</kbd> to let your <span class="thing">presence</span> out (60 mana a second): every lesser mana presence within ten blocks is slowed, mages and beasts up to S-class (PvP rules apply; your own beasts never). SS-class beasts and the asura feel nothing.

A weaker cousin of the asura's King's Force, which also weakens and puts the weak to flight \[invented\]. It and suppression turn each other off.
