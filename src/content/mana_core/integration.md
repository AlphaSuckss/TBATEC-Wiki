Past White lies one more stage: <span class="thing">integration</span>, where a core's mana no longer sits in the core but runs through the whole body. Few of the lesser races ever reach it.

**Lore**: the books name it. The Lances' artifacts kept them from it; Varay and then Mica reached it once Arthur helped break that seal, and their cold and gravity fill the air round them. The books say little else: the rest here is the mod's own \[invented\].

## The Way

To integrate you need, all at once:<br>**White**: pure, its capacity grown to the cap (peak)<br>**Mana Rotation**: learned from [Sylvia](/mana_core/rotation)<br>**Mastery**: one element at full mastery (or an awakened deviant of it)<br>**Purity**: 100, by [purification](/mana_core/purification)<br>**No seal**: see [The Lance Seal](/mana_core/lance_seal)

## Who

**Not for**: asura (their cores are one with bodies built for them), the reborn (their core broke in half) or wraiths.

**Half-bloods** integrate like anyone, and purify half again as fast.

Then meditate with a full pool: the [rite](/mana_core/purification#rite) begins.

## What Changes

Integrated, your core is rank 6 (Black 0 .. White 5):<br>**Mana**: 54,000 (1.6x a peak White pool), twice White's recovery<br>**Flow**: a fifth of every spell drawn from the air, not the pool; spells cast at once, as at White<br>**Body**: stronger than White's, mends itself<br>**No backlash**: never again, nor overfill

And two arts of your own, on <kbd title="Integration: Domain (tap) / Presence (hold)">Y</kbd>: tap it for your [domain](/mana_core/domain), hold it to let your [presence](/mana_core/domain#presence) out.

Integration stays below the asura and far below the godrunes: see [the scale](/mana_core/integrated_body#scale).
