A <span class="thing">White</span> core (every asura's) can carry you through the air.

Press <kbd title="Mana Flight / Hover">O</kbd> on the ground to switch mana flight on. From then on, jump while you are in the air and wings of mana spread: you glide and fly like an elytra, no elytra needed. Sneak with <kbd title="Mana Flight / Hover">O</kbd> on the ground to launch straight up into flight. In water, on it or under it, <kbd title="Mana Flight / Hover">O</kbd> drives you up and out into flight.

## In the Air

Hold forward to thrust ahead; sneak to brake. You leave a trail of your strongest element.

In the air, <kbd title="Mana Flight / Hover">O</kbd> switches between gliding and <span class="thing">hovering</span>: upright, slow and drifting, holding your height. Hold jump to rise and sneak to sink, gently; your movement keys drift you along at a walk. Falling, the key catches you in a hover. Sneak with it while hovering or falling to fold your wings.

## Landing

Gliding costs 10 mana a second and hovering 40 (half a second's worth to start), and your mana doesn't refill while you fly. Flight ends when you land, enter water, are levitated, mount something, change dimension, run dry, or fall under a wraith's pressure.

A controlled landing never hurts. Hit the ground at full speed and it hurts a little and cracks the ground; a dive digs a small crater where mob griefing is on.
