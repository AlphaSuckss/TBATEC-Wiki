A mage fills a core the slow way: sit, draw the mana in, then spend it. A mana beast never stops to rest; it keeps mana turning through its core while it runs and fights.

&#8203;<span class="thing">Sylvia</span>, the dragon hiding in her cave between the Beast Glades and the Elshire Forest, can teach you the knack, once, and no one else can. Find her on her throne and talk to her; you need a mana core, and a calm hand.

## Training

Her training has four steps (your <kbd title="Character Screen">I</kbd> screen shows where you are):

**1. Sit**: meditate 30 seconds within 12 blocks of her, the way you know.<br>**2. Walk**: she draws your core down to a tenth; walk 45 seconds while it refills.<br>**3. Fight**: defeat 3 hostile creatures and spend half your pool on magic, without sitting to rest or running dry (either starts the step over).<br>**4. Let go**: go back to her and hear her out.

## Learned

From the walk on, your core keeps turning while you move and cast, though not yet faster. Learned, your mana refills half again as fast, and keeps refilling at that rate while you move, hold an art, channel or gather. Talking to her mid-way reminds you of the step.

## In the Books

In the novel Sylvia caught the young Arthur falling from a cliff and kept him in this cave for months. She suggested he draw mana in while moving, which humans rarely practise; he learned to fight with it turning, and named it Mana Rotation himself. She also urged him to let go of his hatred.
