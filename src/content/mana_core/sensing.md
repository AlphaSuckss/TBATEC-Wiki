A mage gives off faint fluctuations of mana, a <span class="thing">signature</span>, and beasts and mages feel it. The stronger the core, the farther it is felt: a beast or mage stronger than you feels you through walls from about (8 + 4 per tier) blocks, 2 more for each tier it stands above you.

Tiers follow the beast classes: Black 0, Red 1, Orange 2, Yellow 3, Silver 4.5, White 6, integrated 7, an asura 8; a beast its class (E 0 to SS 7).

## Seen

While your signature shows:<br>- a stronger hostile beast that feels you comes for you, even out of sight (within its own range);<br>- a wild beast two tiers or more below you won't start a fight with you, sensing it can't win (it still fights back);<br>- the [danger sense](/heritage/danger_sense) of others feels you, if you are the stronger.

## Suppressed

Press <kbd title="Suppress Mana Signature (hold: release your mana)">J</kbd> to <span class="thing">suppress</span> it: you draw in no more than leaves you, so your core recovers at half speed. Mana beasts and mages notice you at half their usual range (one two tiers above you still does), nothing feels you through walls, and no danger sense picks you up. But weak beasts no longer keep away.

A spell you cast lets it show for 3 seconds; held body arts don't.

## Letting It Out

**Hold** the same key to do the opposite and <span class="thing">let your mana out</span>. While you hold it, it costs 2% of your pool a second and weighs on every living thing within 6 blocks, 1 more for each tier you stand on, by how far below you it is: 1 tier, slowed and weakened; 3, held nearly still, and creatures lose their quarry; 5 or more, held fast and hurt each second. Never your own beasts or allies. A grown asura's release is <span class="thing">King's Force</span> and reaches further.

Everyone within 48 blocks is told when a power flares near them, and on which side: a release, a hidden signature shown again, a boss beginning its fight. <code>\[the numbers are invented\]</code>

## At a Glance

A small closed eye on your mana orb shows you are suppressed; it opens, amber, while a spell gives you away. The character screen says how far you are felt.

[Mirage Walk](/spellcraft/pure_mana) is the full vanish: no one can pick you out beyond a couple of blocks.
