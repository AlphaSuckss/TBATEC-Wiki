## Where

On the forecourt of the [Mist-Door](/sites/elshire_shrine) and at Elenoir's gate shrines. The Elshire's mist turns away all but elves; elves need no guide and are never charged.

**Mist-ward** (5 emeralds): ten minutes in which the forest mist lifts for you (the canopy's pollen still wants a light or a Wind Ward).

## Escort

**Escort** (12): the guide walks ahead of you to the nearest Zestier or tree hall. Near her (12 blocks) both the mist and the pollen lift. Fall 14 blocks behind and she waits; out of sight for a minute and she turns back.

A guide who ends a walk far from her post slips away into the mist; another warden takes the post.
