## Registering

In the guild hall of [Xyrus](/sites/xyrus) (behind the glass counter, by the Quest Master) and the Etistin office. Adventurers hold the beast-class letters, <span class="thing">E</span> up to <span class="thing">SS</span>.

To register: the [assessment](/services/mana_assessor), an awakened core and 5 emeralds. You get an engraved <span class="item">badge</span>: use it any time to read your record.

## Bounties

Four <span class="thing">bounties</span> a day on your board, each "slay N beasts of class X" for a class from one below your rank to one above (one at most above). Carry up to three; kills by you or your contracted beast count; the pay waits at any clerk.

**E**: 6 beasts: 4 emeralds, 3 merit<br>**C**: 4: 12, 8<br>**A**: 2: 32, 20<br>**S**: 1: 64, 45<br>**SS**: 1: 96, 70

## Ranks

**Rank exam**: merit, a core of your new class handed in as proof, and a core colour:

**D**: 20 merit<br>**C**: 60<br>**B**: 150, Red<br>**A**: 320, Orange<br>**AA**: 600, Yellow<br>**S**: 1,000, Silver<br>**SS**: 1,600, White

## Cores and the Board

**Cores**: hold them and sell: E 2, D 5, C 9, B 16, A 28, AA 44, S 64, SS 96 emeralds each, and merit for the first 10 a day.

**The Quest Master**: his board still trades arms and armour for trophies; each of his bounties you finish adds 2 merit to your record.
