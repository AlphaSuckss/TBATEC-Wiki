## The Assessment

At the north end of the Xyrus guild counter. The guild's admission begins with a mana core assessment, and it is taken <span class="thing">once</span>: 8 emeralds.

Stand still: your mana rises until the barrier of your awakening rings you again, pressing outward for an augmenter, drawing inward like a vacuum for a conjurer.

## The Reading

She reads it aloud and writes a <span class="item">certificate</span> (a written book): race, core colour and stage, pool, refinement, grown capacity, augmenter or conjurer, each affinity with its mastery, deviant arts awakened and how near the latent ones are, Mana Rotation, a beast will in your core, an elf's danger sense.

A copy of the record: 1 emerald. Walk off mid-reading and the next one is free.
