## The Sales

Behind the podium on the stage of the sunken hall, in the Helstea Auction House on the City Square of [Xyrus](/sites/xyrus). A <span class="thing">sale</span> of six lots runs two days; a new one opens the moment it closes: two beast cores (B to SS), two rare beast materials, two pieces of gear or relics. <span class="item">Dawn's Ballad</span> turns up now and then.

## Bidding

Hover a lot to see it. Each opens at its reserve (55% of its worth). The house's other bidders answer every bid at once, by 5%, until it passes what they'll pay (80 to 125% of the worth); then they drop out and the lot is yours at your bid, unless another player outbids you.

Your bid's emeralds are held. Outbid, collect them here; win, and the lot waits here after the close.
