## Emitter Healer

A field medic of the Wall, an emitter.

## Where

In the dispensary tent of the medic camp behind [the Wall](/sites/the_wall). Emitters heal others with their own mana; the story's own emitter served as a medic at the Wall.

**The body** she mends whole: full health, and Weakness, Slowness, Mining Fatigue, Hunger, Nausea, Blindness, Poison and Wither gone.

## Price

**A core** is not hers to heal: Mana Backlash, an Overfilled Core, a Will's Backlash and Will Exhaustion each keep a quarter of their time (an overloaded will's Strain eases with it).

**Mending**: 6 emeralds<br>**Each core affliction**: +10<br>**A day**: 6 mendings, then she rests till morning
