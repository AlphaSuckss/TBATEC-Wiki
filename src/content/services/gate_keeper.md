## Where

Beside Xyrus's Sky Gate on the Gate Plaza, Etistin's harbour rotunda gate and Zestier's Royal Gate (an elven warden). Every gate takes a toll of its traveller's mana; for emeralds a keeper pays it and charges his gate to another city's: Xyrus, Etistin, Zestier, or the gate shrines of Elenoir and Darv.

## Passage

Walk through when the portal opens. Gate sickness comes as on any trip.

**Fare**: 2 emeralds, plus 1 per 300 blocks, at most 24.

He knows every city gate the world's network has heard from (its ground loaded once), found or not, and a trip with him reveals the far gates to you. Never the Flying Castle; never inside his own city.
