## Talking

Use one and they answer in chat; their offers show as <span class="thing">\[buttons\]</span>: click one, within 10 blocks. They pay and take <span class="item">emeralds</span> (blocks count as nine, change comes back).

They cannot be hurt. Strike one and it won't serve you for two minutes. Towns built before 1.27 get their people the first time you pass by.
