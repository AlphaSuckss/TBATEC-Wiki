A dragon bond grows the way Sylvie did. Bonded as a child it walks with you as a small <span class="thing">white fox</span>; as an adolescent it can take its <span class="thing">dragon form</span>, big enough to carry you; grown, it finds a <span class="thing">human form</span> too.

Ask it to **Change shape** from the ask wheel to choose which it wears day to day.

## Riding

Ask it to **Carry me** and it takes its dragon form and lets you on. Jump to beat off the ground and to climb; fly the way you look with the forward key; let go of it to hang and sink slowly; sneak to slide off (it lets you down gently). It breathes on whatever you are fighting.

## Its Arts

An adolescent dragon bond mends you with <span class="thing">vivum</span> when you are badly hurt (every 30 s), and in its dragon form its <span class="thing">King's Force</span> cows a crowd of foes (every 40 s). Grown, its bolt becomes a breath of aether.

Woken from its egg, it has <span class="thing">aevum</span> in place of vivum: time thickens round your foes and quickens you.

## Other Clans

Every other clan's bond keeps its people's humanoid shape as it grows. Once grown, a fight that turns hard calls out its race's <span class="thing">true form</span>, drawn smaller than a player's, and stronger.

**From the books**: Sylvie's fox, dragon and human forms, her riding, vivum, King's Force and aevum after her rebirth. **Invented**: the stages, the numbers, the other clans' forms.
