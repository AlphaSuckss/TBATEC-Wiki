## Regis

A shadow wolf, born of an acclorite and aether.

## Waking Him

Set an <span class="item">Acclorite</span> in your palm: use it with <span class="item">Uto's Horn</span> (the Retainer Uto's) in your other hand. Both are taken.

The acclorite sleeps until you hold aether, which only the <span class="thing">reborn</span> do. Then it drinks 300 aether from you, a little each second while your vessel is more than 40% full (three times as fast in the Relictombs), and <span class="thing">Regis</span> is born.

## In Your Shadow

He lives in your shadow: there he has no body and nothing can touch him. He comes out by himself when a fight starts and goes back once it is over; sneak and press <kbd title="Companion Command / Whistle">H</kbd> to call him out or send him back, or look at him and press it for his choices (stay in until called, carry me).

Beaten down he sinks back into the shadow to mend and will not come out for 30 seconds; he never dies. Out of a fight for 20 seconds, he goes back on his own.

## Growing

For his first 5 minutes he is a black flaming wisp. Then he takes a wolf's shape, and he <span class="thing">grows with your aether vessel</span>: a pup while it is small, a wolf, then a great wolf big enough to ride (use him with an empty hand). When your aether runs low he shrinks to a pup again.

His health, bite and armour all grow with the vessel.

## The Gauntlet

Ready <span class="thing">Destruction</span> (select it) or wield it, and he leaps onto your hand as the <span class="item">Regis</span> gauntlet, in a free hands slot or your empty off hand. While he is there the godrune's <span class="thing">Pull</span> is halved, as it always is with the gauntlet on. When Destruction has been quiet a few seconds, he lets go.

This gauntlet is him: it cannot be dropped, stored or kept.

## His Arts

**Aether bite**: every bite lands an aetheric part that gets past armour.<br>**Pounce**: from four to twelve blocks he leaps at his foe.<br>**Destruction breath**: with the godrune woken in you, and paid from your aether: two seconds of violet flame in a short cone. It burns creatures, never the ground.

## Manners

He fights what attacks you and what you attack; never you, your pets or your party, and another player only where you could hurt them yourself.

He speaks only to you, and he is rude about it.
