Use a companion with an empty hand to switch it between <span class="thing">Follow</span> and <span class="thing">Stay</span>. With food or a core it feeds instead; with a saddle, harness or chest, it puts it on.

&#8203;<kbd title="Sneak">Shift</kbd> + use opens its <span class="thing">screen</span>: portrait, contract, level, experience, loyalty and stats, its orders and stances, and its saddle, harness and saddlebag slots. While riding, <kbd title="Open/Close Inventory">E</kbd> opens it.

## Orders

**Follow**: it comes after you, and teleports to you when 12 or more blocks behind.

**Stay**: it sits, picks no fights, and naps.

**Guard**: it holds the spot, never straying more than about 12 blocks, and attacks any monster within 16.

## Stances

**Defend**: (the default) fights whatever hurts you or whatever you attack, and fights back.

**Hunt**: also attacks any monster on its own.

**Passive**: never fights; only a rider can call its moves.

## Whistle

&#8203;<kbd title="Companion Command / Whistle">H</kbd> while looking at a companion (up to 48 blocks away) cycles Follow, Stay and Guard.

Press it anywhere else to <span class="thing">whistle</span>: every companion within 48 blocks drops what it is doing and follows you.

Companions never despawn, and lie down to sleep when you sleep nearby (unless guarding).
