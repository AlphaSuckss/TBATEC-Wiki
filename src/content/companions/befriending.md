A child is never tamed. It has to come to trust you, and then it chooses. Use it with an empty hand to talk; it tells you how far its trust has come (out of 1000).

Food is refused: it is no beast to be fed by your hand. No core or contract binds it.

## Earning Trust

**Time**: near it and in its sight, slowly, up to 300 in all.

**Gifts**: what its race values, 3 a day, each kind once a day.

**Deeds**: monsters and hostile beasts you bring down where it can see, up to 150 a day.

**Protection**: bringing down what hurt or hunted it, worth the most, up to 240 a day.

## Gifts They Value

**Dragons**: enchanted and written books, amethyst, ender pearls, echo shards, dragon breath, glow lotus.<br>**Basilisks**: wither roses, obsidian, netherite scrap, echo shards, a sculk catalyst.<br>**Pantheons**: good weapons, shields, a spyglass, beast cores.<br>**Phoenixes**: blaze rods, fire charges, magma cream, torchflowers, a nether star.

**Titans**: diamonds, emeralds, gold, netherite, an anvil, beast cores.<br>**Hamadryads**: saplings and flowers, spore blossoms, aura fern, glowbark.<br>**Leviathans**: nautilus shells, coral, prismarine, turtle scutes, a heart of the sea.<br>**Sylphs**: feathers, phantom membrane, breeze rods, wind charges, a wind-ward charm.

## The Offer

At 1000 trust the child reaches into your mind for the first time and offers the bond: not master and beast, but equals. Accept, and a thread of light joins you, its thoughts are beside yours, and a mark like a seal settles on the back of your hand.

Its clan will not undo what a young mind has chosen.
