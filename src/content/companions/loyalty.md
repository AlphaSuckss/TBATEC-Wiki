Loyalty runs from 0 to 100 and shows on the companion's screen.

It rises a point for every minute you keep it within 32 blocks, for kills it makes near you (at most one every 20 seconds), when you feed it, heal it or breed it, with each core and each level. It drops by 6 every time you strike it.

## What It Changes

A loyal beast (80 or more) calls its moves faster when you ride it (x0.8 the wait); a distrustful one (below 20) slower (x1.5).

A servant with less than 20 loyalty may refuse to let you mount.
