Asura have few children, and guard them closely. In each clan's seat in Epheotus a few of its young, never more than 3, play near where the clan's asura wake: Everburn's gate, Battle's End, Ecclesia's square, Mapellia's north gate, the forecourts of Grandus Hold and the Kothan Halls, Featherwalk Aerie's approach and the Gate of Veils.

They are curious, they come to look at visitors, and they keep out of reach of a lesser they do not trust.

## Guarded

Strike a child and its trust in you is gone for good, and the clan's warriors come for you. A grown asura of its clan will tell a lesser who stands too close to keep away.

A child gone from its seat, with a partner or otherwise, is replaced in time.

## Who May Bond

Only a child will ever bond. A <span class="thing">grown asura</span> refuses anyone who asks (sneak and use it, empty-handed or with a gift), and an asura does not bond with another asura. A wraith can't bond: the children back away from what the Vritra made.

Each player can have <span class="thing">one</span> asura bond, for life.
