A mage binds a mana beast by <span class="thing">contract</span>. There are two, and a beast's nature decides which it will accept.

Only you can command, feed and ride a beast bound to you, and a bound beast never drops a core.

## Contract of Equals

Earned by kindness. Feed a beast the food it likes (each try may fail, and the food is eaten either way), or freely offer it a core of <span class="thing">its class or higher</span>. It mustn't be hunting you at the time.

It starts out loyal (65, a cub 85), waiting where it stands.

## Master-Servant

Earned by force. Beat any beast down to a fifth of its health or less, then use a core on it: one class below its own at most. It obeys at once, waiting where it stands, but its loyalty starts low (25) and must be earned.

Asuran beasts accept only an <span class="item">asuran core</span>. Never bound: mirror phantoms, black-horned mutants, shadow golems, aether millipedes, three-headed serpents, aether titans, demon leeches, questing beasts and questing monstrosities.

## Who Accepts What

**By food**: the 23 kinds under Favourite Foods, some only as cubs.

**By a core**: any of those, adults too, and nightmare fox, thunder hawk, black-fanged wolf, wyvern, shadow panther and phoenix wyrm.

**By force only**: every other beast.
