## Their Ways

**Helen**: arrows that burn with light: her marks glow; every few seconds a volley of three.<br>**Adam**: charges through foes with his spear.<br>**Angela**: blades of wind; she feels movement on the wind and shows every hostile thing near, and blows back what gets close.<br>**Durden**: spikes of stone from under his foes; when you are badly hurt the earth guards you.<br>**Jasmine**: disowned by the fire house Flamesworth for her wind; she steps behind her foe on it and cuts twice.
