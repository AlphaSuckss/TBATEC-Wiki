Some of the people from Arthur's story will travel with you, if you give them a reason: the [Twin Horns](/companions/twin_horns), [Caera](/companions/caera), [Chul](/companions/chul) and [Ellie and Boo](/companions/ellie_and_boo). Each one asks something first; talk to them to learn what.

## With You

At most 2 travel with you at once (Ellie and Boo count as one; Regis does not count).

Talk to one who travels with you to set them to <span class="thing">follow</span>, <span class="thing">stay</span> or <span class="thing">guard</span> a place, change their <span class="thing">stance</span> (defensive, aggressive, passive), or part ways. Look at one and press <kbd title="Companion Command / Whistle">H</kbd> to change the mode; press it looking at nothing to call everyone back.

## Their Ways

They fight what attacks you and what you attack (aggressive: any monster close by). They never hurt you, your pets or your party, and fight another player only where you could hurt that player yourself.

They never die: beaten down, they go home to rest for 10 minutes and leave you. At home nobody can hurt them.

## Travel

Those who follow you go with you to another world, and catch up if you leave them far behind. Ones set to stay or guard wait where they are.

**Invented**: all of this; the people themselves are the books'.
