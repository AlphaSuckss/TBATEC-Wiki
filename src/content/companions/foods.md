Every beast you can bond has a <span class="thing">food of its own</span>, made at a crafting table from what it eats in the wild. Feed it to a hurt companion of that kind and it mends at once, a good third of its health and more over the next few seconds, and grows a little fonder of you.

Any other beast turns its nose up at it; a beast that isn't hurt doesn't want it.
