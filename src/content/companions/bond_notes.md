From the novels: Sylvie, born of an egg Sylvia gave Arthur, bonded with him when she hatched (a seal appeared on his hand); they spoke mind to mind, felt each other's feelings, and she sensed when he was in danger. She grew from a hatchling into a dragon he could ride and took a child's human form with horns. The asura call the other races lessers, have few children, guard their young, and form no bonds with lessers.

Also from the novels: Sylvie gave her body to save Arthur's, leaving him part asura; she was reduced to an egg of stone he carried; aether broke the seals within it, and she came back looking older.

The children shown in the books are Taci and the pantheon children Arthur sparred with, and Sylvie herself.
