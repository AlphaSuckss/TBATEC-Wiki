Your bond follows you because it chooses to. It fights its own way: whoever hurts you or it, whoever you fight, and monsters that hunt you or come too close.

It is never lost in a fight. Brought down, it withdraws exhausted and comes back beside you when rested (a child in 2 minutes, grown in 1). It goes where you go, across dimensions and logouts, and no one else can lead, name or claim it.

## Its Own Way

**Dragon**: a bolt of aether that passes armour by.<br>**Phoenix**: a cone of fire.<br>**Basilisk**: a breath of withering decay.<br>**Titan**: a stamp that cracks the ground.<br>**Leviathan**: a lance of water; it puts you out if you burn.<br>**Sylph**: a gust that throws foes up.<br>**Hamadryad**: roots that hold a foe, mending you a little.<br>**Pantheon**: a sudden step to a foe's back and a heavy blow.

## The Mind Link

It speaks into your mind: it warns of danger you can't see (and outlines it for you), worries when you're hurt, feels your pain, and says what it thinks.

Use it to open the <span class="thing">ask wheel</span>, or type <span class="thing">/tbatecbond</span> for chat buttons: Come, Wait here, Scout ahead, Stay out of this. You ask; it answers, and it may refuse (it won't stay out of a fight you are losing).

## Growing Up

It grows up with the hours you play together: an adolescent after 8, grown after 24. A child has a little under half a grown bond's strength, an adolescent about three quarters.

Sneak and use it to see how it is and how long until it grows.
