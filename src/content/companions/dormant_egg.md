## Dormant Asura Egg

<div class="spotlight"><img class="icon big" src="/img/item/tbatec/asura_bond_egg.png" alt="Dormant Asura Egg" title="Dormant Asura Egg" loading="lazy"><div>

When your bond gives its body to save yours (see the aether path), it becomes a dormant egg of stone, bound to you.

</div></div>

The egg is kept when you die, never drops, never lies in the world, and won't go into any container: it stays with you. A lost copy is void, and the egg is back in your pack at once.

Hold use on it to pour your aether in. It needs 3000 in all; each third breaks one of the 3 seals inside the stone. Mana means nothing to it.

## Waking

When the last seal breaks, your bond wakes and stands beside you again, a stage older than it was. Aroa's Requiem can hasten the waking.
