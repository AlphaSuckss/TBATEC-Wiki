18 beasts can be ridden. On the ground: moon ox, blood-red ox, aurochs, skitter, clawed equine, iron-bite bear, silver coat bear, world lion, frost-claw bear, shadow panther and guardian bear.

In the air: flare hawk, bladewing, wyvern, griffon, phoenix wyrm, sky swimmer and skyray.

Put a saddle on an adult (use it on the beast, or in its screen), then use it to climb on.

## Controls

Steer as on a horse; sideways and backwards go slower, and sprinting spurs it on. A flier takes off when you jump, and climbs or dives as you look up or down while moving forward.

Only its owner can ride it, and a rider can't hurt their mount. Take the saddle off and the rider is thrown.

## Moves

&#8203;<kbd title="Mount Ability 1">Z</kbd> and <kbd title="Mount Ability 2">V</kbd> call your mount's two moves; the HUD shows their cooldowns. Cubs can't use them.

&#8203;<span class="thing">Lunge</span> is a leap forward; <span class="thing">Surge</span> a burst of flight. Its signature power takes the first key.
