## Chul

Half phoenix, half djinn; loud, proud, eager to fight.

## Meeting Him

You find him in the wrestling ring of <span class="thing">the Hearth</span> under the Beast Glades. Words do nothing for him: <span class="thing">spar</span> with him and bring him down to under a third of his health and he will follow you anywhere. A spar never kills; his blows are held back in it.

He will not travel with the dragons' blood, and he will not set foot in Epheotus.

## His Ways

He swings <span class="item">Suncrusher</span>, its blows burning with phoenix fire; every few seconds he brings it down and fire bursts round the blow.

**From the books**: his parents, his hair and eyes, his weapon, his wish to fight the Legacy, his hiding from the dragons, calling Arthur a brother in vengeance after a spar.

**Invented**: the ring as a meeting place, the numbers.
