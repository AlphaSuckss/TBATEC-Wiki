A companion grows from level 1 to 30: 31 experience for its first level, 1,151 for its last, 13,485 in all.

A kill is worth 50% of the victim's health, 35% more for each class a mana beast stands above E. A core you feed it is worth far more: 40 for an E-class core, doubling each class, up to 10,240 for an asuran one. A core also heals it 20% and adds 8 loyalty.

## Growing

Each level past the first adds 5% health, 4% damage, 0.3 armour, 0.1 toughness and 0.6% speed: at 30, x2.45 health, x2.16 damage and +8.7 armour. It grows up to 12% larger, and its moves come 1% sooner a level. Each level-up heals 35% and adds 2 loyalty.

Its stage shows on its screen: <span class="thing">Cub</span>, <span class="thing">Juvenile</span> (below 10), <span class="thing">Adult</span> (10 to 29) and <span class="thing">Prime</span> (30).

## Ascension

At level 30, feed a companion a core of a <span class="thing">higher class</span> than its own and it <span class="thing">ascends</span>: a whole class up, an Elder, larger, with x1.25 health and x1.2 bite, starting again from level 1.

A core that isn't higher is refused and kept. A beast can climb as far as SS; asuran beasts never ascend.
