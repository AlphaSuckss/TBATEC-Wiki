Everywhere else lies the <span class="thing">Sea of Clouds</span>, a band of cloud from about Y 90 to Y 109, scattered with karst pillars rising out of it and floating islets above, rarer the further out you go.

A [cloud](/items/cloud) can't hold you: you sink slowly through it, and it breaks any fall.

## The Grey

Under the clouds is <span class="thing">the Grey</span>: grey stone at the bottom of the world, grey sky, falling ash, dead brush and pale wisps. Nothing is born in or under the clouds.

Sink through the clouds into the open Grey and the sea of clouds bears you back up to the last solid ground you stood on (or the arrival pad).

The rescue never reaches inside Mount Geolus's root, where a hidden way leads down through the cloud to the Grey and Lord Indrath's training cave. See [The Ascent of Geolus](/epheotus/geolus).

Fly, glide or ride a flier and the clouds leave you be.
