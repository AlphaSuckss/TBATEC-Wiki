The arrival peak of Mount Geolus, from its summit (Y 372) down to Y 57 in the mountain's root.

On the summit, the <span class="thing">arrival shrine</span>: an open rotunda over a golden glass landing pad, a beacon throwing Windsom's column of light up through its eye, amid a blossom avenue, a grove and a tea pavilion.

## The Climb

A path winds round the peak's flanks past rest stations, training cliffs, a tunnel and a window hall, down to the upper gate terrace, a double stair, the foot court with its spring, and the [foot gate](/epheotus/roads).

Another path follows the ravine to the saddle between the peaks, past nests, a cascade and mist.

## The Hidden Way Down

From the saddle a crevice stair drops into a spiral well, then the root rift, crossed on rope bridges through a band of cloud, down to the Grey: dead brush, rotting logs, pale wisps.

A great dead root marks the hidden cave: <span class="thing">Lord Indrath's training cave</span>, with the sapphire aether pool, an aether wellspring and the Aether Orb.
