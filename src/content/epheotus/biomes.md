Every biome of the realm keeps its own beasts, by day and by night: hostile ones don't wait for the dark here. The [asuran beasts](/beasts/class_asura) are in gold.

**Mount Geolus**: meadow and cherry trees, aether crystals and blooms. Rabbits, sheep, goats. Beasts: <span class="thing">sky swimmers</span>, mountain wings.

**Everburn**: hot, rainless, orange-skied, red-leaved. Llamas, sheep, chickens. Beasts: mountain wings.

**Wildwood**: giant pines and dark growth. Rabbits, foxes, wolves. Beasts: <span class="thing">gloom crows</span>, <span class="thing">titan bears</span>.

**The Grey**: ash and dead brush under the clouds. Nothing spawns there.

**Cerulean Savanna**: cyan-blue grass. Horses, donkeys, ocelots, armadillos, llamas. Beasts: world lions, moon oxen, <span class="thing">raptor squirrels</span>, <span class="thing">silver panthers</span>, <span class="thing">gloom crows</span>, <span class="thing">ah'tules</span>, <span class="thing">bladegrass cats</span>, <span class="thing">titan bears</span>, <span class="thing">demon leeches</span>.

**Ecclesia**: turquoise water, sand and sandstone. Turtles, dolphins and fish. Beasts: <span class="thing">ten-thousand-legged crabs</span>, <span class="thing">flutter drakes</span>.

**Titan Highlands**: windswept, veined with crystal. Goats. Beasts: silver coat bears, world lions, <span class="thing">guardian bears</span>, <span class="thing">mountain golems</span>.

**Hamadryad Grove**: flower forest and glowing glowbark trees. Rabbits, foxes, bees. Beasts: forest hounds, skitters, <span class="thing">raptor squirrels</span>, <span class="thing">silver panthers</span>, <span class="thing">wood wights</span>, <span class="thing">gloom crows</span>, <span class="thing">titan bears</span>, <span class="thing">demon leeches</span>.

**Basilisk Spires**: purple-black sky and ash. Endermen. Beasts: shadow panthers, nightmare foxes, black-fanged wolves.

**Sylph Tempest**: pale sky, white ash and the tempest. Goats, parrots. Beasts: sonic hawks, thunder hawks, <span class="thing">raptor squirrels</span>, <span class="thing">sky swimmers</span>, <span class="thing">skyrays</span>.

**Featherwalk Aerie**: orange sky, cherry petals, terracotta. Parrots, goats, chickens. Beasts: flare hawks, <span class="thing">raptor squirrels</span>, <span class="thing">mountain golems</span>, <span class="thing">wood wights</span>, <span class="thing">skyrays</span>, mountain wings, <span class="thing">ah'tules</span>, <span class="thing">questing beasts</span>, <span class="thing">titan bears</span>.
