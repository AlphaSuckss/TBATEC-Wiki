&#8203;<span class="thing">Clan Eccleiah, the leviathans</span>.

Lord Veruhn's city on the Boundary Sea, half city and half bay, the lowest of the domains.

**Where**: (624, 784), plaza at Y 182, in the Ecclesia.

A jade-arcaded arrival square with a leviathan fountain (its water held up in tide membranes), the Avenue of Arches down to the Sea Gate, streets of coral, sandstone and pearl houses, and a market square with a tavern.

Veruhn's house on its promontory, with coral water channels inside and a tide-pool garden behind. <span class="thing">Aquinas, the World Serpent</span>: a leviathan arch at the Sea Gate, its ribs half-buried in the beach, and a pier of tail bones.

In the bay: the Temple of the Tides on its islet, crowned with spheres of water you can swim into; the harbour and fish market; a volcano with a crater path; a jungle headland; sea stacks and an arch inside the reef.

**Lord Veruhn** keeps the great hall of his house. Hand him the core of a great beast (A class or better) and it goes back to the sea, for standing with the Eccleiah: A 10, AA 15, S 25, SS 60, an asuran beast's 100. Speak to him with empty hands and, when his clan counts you Friendly, he gives you a [Mourning Pearl](/arms/asuran_artifacts); a second at Honoured, the third at Revered. Three in a lifetime, and they are found nowhere else.

A pearl heals you whole, ends a backlash and fills your mana; in someone with no core it forms one, White. Sneak and use it on another player to spend it on them. <code>\[the pearls and what they did are the books'; the offerings and the standing are invented\]</code>
