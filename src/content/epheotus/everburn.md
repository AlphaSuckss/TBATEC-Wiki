The dragon town of Clan Inthirah in the foothills, round the <span class="thing">Everburn Fountain</span>: a ring of stones round a bubbling pool, with an [aether wellspring](/items/wellspring) at its heart throwing violet fire and a geyser.

**Where**: (-304, -112), plaza at Y 226, in the Everburn.

## The Town

Smooth white and cream houses drawn up from the ground in rib-like arches, roofed in turquoise and cyan, with front yards, woven chairs and curtained doorways. The Gate of Ribs, the market, the library, the open three-storey estate with almost no walls, and the walled garden behind its hedge.

The pond cottage with its golden fish, the elder's hall on its terrace, and farmsteads among fields of golden-bulbed stalks.
