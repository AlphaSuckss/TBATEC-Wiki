&#8203;<span class="thing">Clan Thyestes, the pantheons</span>.

The pantheons' village. At its centre, the forest of balance posts where Kordri trains Ellie, in a sanded ring with lintel gates and banners.

**Where**: (992, 0), plaza at Y 214, in the Cerulean Savanna.

The village gate and watchtower; family halls and round huts; stables and paddocks of moon oxen, granaries on stilts and the common hall; the barracks quarter with its training hall, armoury and sparring ring.

Further out: the Hall of Champions and its statue court, Lord Ademir's clan hall on its terrace, the forge and the archery field, a sunken arena bowl, and the bathhouse and hot springs.

The savanna keeps the clan's peace: its <span class="item">living grass</span> lashes and binds monsters and hunting beasts that wade into it, and anyone who starts a fight on the savanna, for half a minute.
