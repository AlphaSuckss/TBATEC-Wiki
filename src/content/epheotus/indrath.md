Seat of the dragon clan, on Mount Geolus's northern peak at (0, -64), from Y 260 inside the mountain to the spire at Y 559. From the arrival shrine, cross the <span class="thing">many-coloured crystal bridge</span> and the blossom walk to the golden gate between two stone dragons.

## Outside

The master tower with its rose window and great stained-glass diamond; needle towers, Kezess's tower, the solarium tower and the Great Eight tower; mauve fin buttresses; and the dragon <span class="thing">Kezess</span> coiled round the spire, feathered wings raised.

## The Castle

The entrance hall; the great hall with the white throne burning with white fire; the library and the Hall of the Clans; suites, studies and the music room; the Gallery of the Dragon Lords; the Hoard and the Diamond Chamber; Kezess's study and the Path of Insight; and the Great Eight's meeting room.

## The Steward

The <span class="thing">Indrath Steward</span> in the forecourt gives invitations to the proven: a grown asura of any clan, or a mage whose core has reached White. The young of every clan, dragons too, are turned away until grown.

Nothing on the castle's grounds or its bridge can be broken or built, and nothing hostile spawns there.

## Inside the Mountain

**Level I**: the processional hall, the <span class="thing">Aetherial Council</span> chamber, Kezess's inner sanctuary, the armoury, the dragon treasury and the archives.<br>**Level II**: the Indrath Mausoleum, Sylvia's shrine, the household and the guard wing.<br>**Deep**: the prison and the obsidian Gathering chamber.

## Aether Pressure

The aether thickens with depth and crushes the unready: slowness first, then darkness and wounds, then death. A White core bears Level I, labours on Level II and is crushed in the deep; a grown asura goes down to the prison and feels the Gathering chamber; grown dragons of the clan bear it all.
