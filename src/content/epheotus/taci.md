## Taci

Taci of Clan Thyestes, Kezess's executioner.

## The Hunt

A pantheon prodigy, Kordri's pupil: shaven, black-eyed, in scarlet training robes, with a long blood-red spear. He comes when you are in the Overworld near <span class="thing">home</span> (your bed or anchor there, else the world spawn); after three days of waiting he finds you anywhere in the Overworld.

## His Coming

**The omen**: a word in chat, a bell and far thunder, then a red light falling onto open ground 12 to 20 blocks away. Thirty seconds later he comes down on that spot.

He fights only those he hunts and whoever strikes him, breaks nothing and burns nothing. Other refusers near join his hunt.
