## Kezess Indrath

Lord of Clan Indrath and of Epheotus.

## Kezess Indrath

He stands before the white throne in the great hall of [Castle Indrath](/epheotus/indrath), at (0, 384, -86). Silvery cream hair, amethyst eyes, pearl robes, a golden longsword.

He fights only the [reborn](/relictombs/rebirth) who have woken a [godrune](/relictombs/godrunes). Speak to him to challenge him; anyone else he pushes away unharmed.

24,000 health; the great hall is sealed while you fight.

**Beaten**, he is not slain: he steps out through a fold in space, leaves <span class="item">Kezess's Longsword</span> (always the first time, a quarter of the time after) and is back a day later.
