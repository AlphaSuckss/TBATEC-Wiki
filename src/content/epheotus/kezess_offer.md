## The Offer

In the books, after the asura's war reached Dicathen, <span class="thing">Kezess Indrath</span> offered Virion new artifacts for Dicathen's mages, if the asura's part in what had been done stayed hidden. Elder Rinia saw that the artifacts would bind whoever bore them to his will. Virion told his people, they voted, and they refused. Kezess sent Taci to wipe them out.

## The Terms

**When**: raised in Dicathen (not in Alacrya, not an asura), once your core is <span class="thing">White</span> or you walk the [aether path](/relictombs/rebirth), word reaches you that Windsom has brought Lord Indrath's terms to [Virion](/mentors/virion).

**Where**: speak to Virion at his manor in Zestier, or to the <span class="thing">Indrath steward</span> at Castle Indrath's golden gate. Either puts the terms to you.
