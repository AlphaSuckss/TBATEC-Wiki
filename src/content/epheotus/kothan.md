&#8203;<span class="thing">Clan Kothan, the basilisks</span>.

A terraced citadel of black and crimson stone. The Horn Gate of two colossal sweeping horns opens on a forecourt with the Horned Fire.

**Where**: (-896, -432), plaza at Y 230, in the Basilisk Spires.

**Lower terrace**: the Great Canopy, the clan's shared pavilion; the Hall of Spears; the <span class="thing">Obsidian Laboratory</span>, where shadow panthers and shadow prowlers are kept in cages; the cloister round the sacred Motherland Plot.

**Middle terrace**: the Black Library of the decay arts, the lords' quarters, and a west gallery over the clouds.

**Crown**: Lord Rai Kothan's audience hall with its horned throne under the Great Spire, and a high gallery 80 blocks up.
