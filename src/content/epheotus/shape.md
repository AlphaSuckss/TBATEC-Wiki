**Mount Geolus**: twin peaks on a north-south line. The castle peak, north at (0, -64), tops out at Y 379; the arrival peak, south at (0, 96), at Y 371. A ravine and saddle lie between them, and the mountain's root plunges as an inverted cone through the clouds, ringed by sixteen blossom-crowned <span class="thing">karst towers</span>.

## The Heartland

Around the mountain spreads the <span class="thing">heartland</span>, a floating land some 1,000 blocks across, its ground near Y 218, rising to Y 250 at the mountain's foot. Its underside is an inverted mountain hung with stalactites.

The <span class="thing">Everburn</span> covers its west and north, the <span class="thing">Wildwood</span> its east and south.
