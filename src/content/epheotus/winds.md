The air of Epheotus moves.

**Updraft vents**: in Featherwalk Aerie throw you up to 32 blocks.<br>**Wind runes**: in the Pavilions of Aerind (and Xyrus Academy) lift you up to 18.

Sneak on either to sink gently back down.

## Gale Streams

**Gale streams** run between the Pavilions of Aerind and its floating islets. You can't see them. A glider rides them fast, and anyone with a [Wind Ward](/items/wind_ward_charm) is carried along at their level; everyone else is tossed about and dropped.

## Tempests

Over the <span class="thing">Sylph Tempest</span> the wind gusts on a slowly turning heading and shoves anyone on foot, harder in the air. Swimming, gliding or a Wind Ward keeps you steady.

The blocks themselves are under [Currents](/items/currents).
