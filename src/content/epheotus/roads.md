Every road in Epheotus starts at the <span class="thing">foot gate</span> at the bottom of the climb down Mount Geolus, at (0, 250, 194). From there they run to Everburn, round the mountain to Lady Myre's, and out along each rock bridge to the main entrance of each domain.

## The Way

Five blocks wide, paved in pearlstone and diorite with a chiselled centre line and andesite kerbs, never steep, and arched over every gap.

&#8203;<span class="thing">Lamps</span> line them every 21 blocks: a diorite post under a floating golden light.

Pairs of <span class="thing">waystones</span>, gem-set rune obelisks crowned with aether, stand at the foot gate and where each bridge leaves the heartland.
