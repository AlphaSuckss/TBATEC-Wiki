&#8203;<span class="thing">Clan Mapellia, the hamadryads</span>.

A living palace grown from silver-barked, gold-leaved trees, up to Y 380.

**Where**: (-224, 976), plaza at Y 222, in the Hamadryad Grove.

The <span class="thing">Palace Tree</span>, some 140 blocks tall, stands on an island in a ring river crossed by seven root bridges. Inside: the Hall of Living Columns and the throne grown from the wood, the Heartwood Stair, burl chambers, the Canopy Court, the Lady's Bower, and galleries out along the limbs.

Lesser trees round it (the Seed-Vault, Dwelling, Archive, Stillroom and Hall trees, and three house trees) hold pod homes joined by branch walks.

Round the palace: the Gate of Roots, a circle of six portal trees (one spent and rotting), the moonlit glade and lotus spring, and gardens of glowing bulbs.
