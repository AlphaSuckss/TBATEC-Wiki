The hidden realm of the asura, sealed away from Dicathen: a floating continent over an endless sea of clouds, with Mount Geolus at its heart and Castle Indrath on its peak.

Its shape never changes: every world has the same Epheotus, so the places in this book are always where it says.

## Getting There

<div class="spotlight"><img class="icon big" src="/img/item/tbatec/asura_sigil.png" alt="Asura Sigil" title="Asura Sigil" loading="lazy"><div>

Hold use with an <span class="item">Asura Sigil</span> for two and a half seconds to pass into Epheotus. Use it again there to go back to exactly where you left. It is never used up.

An [asura](/heritage/asura) born in Epheotus is the exception: for them the way out opens only once they are grown and have Lord Indrath's leave.

</div></div>

## Arrival

You arrive on the landing pad of the arrival shrine, high on Mount Geolus's southern peak at (0, 372, 96), facing north over the crystal bridge to [Castle Indrath](/epheotus/indrath).

By default anyone may pass, core or not; server owners can demand a stronger core. The sigil's recipe is under [Asura Sigil](/items/asura_sigil).

## Home of the Asura

An [asura](/heritage/asura) is born on its own clan's ground, which stays its respawn point: dragons at Everburn's gate, pantheons at Battle's End, leviathans in Ecclesia, hamadryads at Mapellia, titans before Grandus Hold, basilisks before the Kothan Halls, phoenixes below Featherwalk, sylphs at the Aerind pavilions.

Beds work in Epheotus; respawn anchors don't. The asuran clans live in its sites, though none will speak to you, and monsters still come out in the dark.
