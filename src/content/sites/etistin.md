Sapin's royal capital and port, on a beach in Sapin, at sea level.

**Where**: beaches and stony shores, only in Sapin's territory<br>**Rarity**: ~1 per 3,200 blocks<br>**Height**: at sea level

## Harbour and Palace

Three piers with a royal galleon, a merchant cog, a paddle steamer and fishing boats; warehouses, the customs house and the fish market.

On rocky crags above the bay stands the <span class="thing">palace</span>: the throne room, the great balcony over the parade plaza, the circular Strategy Room with its war table, the Glayder study, library and royal apartments, the treasury vault, and the Teleportation Room.

## The City

Six terraces of houses joined by steep stone stairs, and along them the harbour rotunda under its copper dome, the market, the temple and its bell tower, the guildhall (with its Quest Master), the tavern, barracks and stable.

Curtain walls and round towers, a twin-towered land gate, and a headland with cottages, a shipwright and the lighthouse.

## Gates

Gates: the Royal Palace and the Harbour Rotunda. Sapin knights garrison it; its streets are full of townsfolk, adventurers and hired swords.
