The ancient mages' hidden refuge under Darv, where the survivors of the lost war hid. On the surface there is only a low rock in the dunes with a cleft over the stair; <span class="thing">Virion's Map</span>, found now and then in elven archives and royal chambers, marks the nearest one.

**Where**: the Darv Abyss<br>**Rarity**: ~1 per 1,280 blocks<br>**Height**: at Y -10

## The Village

A square stairwell, a long passage and the round gate hall, where elven and dwarven guards keep watch. Then old stone streets, the Town Hall on its square, the garden and a stream, light through holes in the roof, and the tombs of Aya and Feyrith. Refugees of every race live here: elves, humans and dwarves.

## The Gate

A tunnel held by an <span class="thing">aether ward</span>: fear, darkness, heavy limbs, and a force pushing you back. Keep walking and you get through. Past the gem-inlaid Relictombs hallway lies an ancient cave no one found, with a small [dormant gate](/items/curiosities) in its far corner. Djinn relic chests may hold an Aetheric Key.
