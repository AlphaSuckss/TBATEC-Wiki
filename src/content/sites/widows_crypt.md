The Glades' beginner dungeon. The <span class="thing">Dragonspine Inn</span>, a palisaded adventurers' lodge with stables, smithy and training yard, sits above it, and a trail runs down a ravine to the crypt's mouth.

**Where**: the Beast Glades<br>**Rarity**: ~1 per 1,280 blocks<br>**Height**: on the ground, the crypt below

## The Floors

Ten frozen floors: the Glimmering Hall, the ice bridges, the frozen falls, the web galleries, a dead party's camp, the old diggings, the bone fields, a ruined shrine, the Ice Cathedral, and the <span class="thing">Queen's Cavern</span>, where the [Widow Queen](/beasts/class_b) waits before her ice throne.

## Getting Back

Snarlers, cave rats and giant spiders breed on the way down. Guild camps mark checkpoints, and a one-way stairway hall leads from the bottom back up to the first floor.
