## Elder Rahdeas

An elder of Darv who served Agrona.

## The Traitor

In the books, Rahdeas raised Olfred and the boy Elijah, schemed for years to bring Darv under Agrona, spoke for Darv on the Council, and, exposed and jailed, was silenced by black spikes before he could be questioned.

Here he holds the portal cave under [Vildorial](/sites/vildorial)'s plaza, down the grand stair.
