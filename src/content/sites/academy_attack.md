## In the Books

In the books the Radicals' robed mages, with corrupted beasts, broke into Xyrus Academy: a barrier became a red cage over the campus, students were taken, Draneeve came for his own ends, and Lucas Wykes, swollen with Draneeve's elixir, burned the Academy until Arthur stopped him at the bell tower.

## When It Comes

It comes for a student with five lectures sat, a rank of 15 or better (or the Committee) and a red core, the next time they are on the campus. Every student there joins in.

The bell rings, the duelling rings' barrier turns red, and half a minute later the cage closes: none in the fight can leave the campus until it ends.

## The Waves

**The plaza**: four Radicals and three of their beasts<br>**The captives**: three students at the library, the dining hall and the dorms, two guards each: drive them off, then speak to the captive<br>**Draneeve**: at the plaza with his pets; brought to a third of his health he leaves through a glowing gate<br>**Lucas**: before the bell tower

## The Fight

Radicals: bolts of discoloured fire, a stone spike out of the cracking ground, a step into the shadows that brings them out at your side.

Fall, or stay off the campus a minute, and you are out; if all are out it is lost and comes again a day later. It breaks nothing.
