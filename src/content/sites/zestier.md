The elven capital, deep in the canopy: a moss-floored city under the trees, lantern-lit, inside a <span class="thing">living wall</span> of woven wood on white stone that joins the forest giants round it.

**Where**: the Elshire Canopy, only in Elenoir<br>**Rarity**: ~1 per 1,760 blocks<br>**Height**: on the ground

## The Royal Tree-Castle

A white trunk 98 blocks to its crown deck. Inside: the Atrium with two curved stairs round a great chandelier of lanterns, the Throne Hall, guest rooms with waterfall showers, the royal apartments and archive, the great hall, the conservatory, and a jade walkway spiralling twice round the trunk.

## The Vault

In the taproot lies the <span class="thing">Royal Vault</span>, barred by a [Plant-Binding Rune Seal](/items/warded_blocks) that only plant magic opens.

Round the palace: the back courtyard with its lotus fountain, the training courtyard, the forecourt, and the <span class="thing">Royal Gate</span>, the city's teleport gate between two twined trunks.

## The City

Down the main road: the market square (armour shop, apothecary, tavern) and the library, with a tree growing through it. Round the city: Elder Rinia's hut, Virion's manor, branch-homes joined by rope bridges, the stream and its jade bridge, the carved jade city gate, and the free-standing Mist-Door in the outer forest.

Elven wardens guard it; its townsfolk and a few human adventurers fill the streets.
