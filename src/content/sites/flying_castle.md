The djinn-built castle of the Council and the Lances, floating high over the deep Glades on its own rock island, well clear of every hill under it, its silhouette a giant bird of prey. An iron stair tower climbs from the ground through the rock to the landing chamber and the no-magic ward gate.

**Where**: the Glades Depths and Highlands<br>**Rarity**: ~1 per 2,400 blocks<br>**Height**: high in the sky

## Inside

The Council chamber under a glass dome, its charwood table under the banners of the three kingdoms; the Teleportation Chamber; the war room and Virion's office; the prison with its great basilisk cell; the Endless Hall of 34 doors; the medical rooms; the Lances' quarters; flight bays; the training room; and the arena in the tail.

## Garrison

Knights of Sapin, elven wardens and dwarven guards garrison it together.
