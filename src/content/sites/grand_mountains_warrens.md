## The Warrens

A cave mouth in a rock outcrop, a gnoll camp before it, and tunnels down into the mountain: a guard cave, the gnolls' den, the orcs' war camp, a rope bridge over a chasm, and the throne cave of the <span class="thing">Black-horned Mutant</span>.

**Where**: the mountains, peaks, windswept hills and the Glades' highlands<br>**Rarity**: ~1 per 2,500 blocks

## Inside

Gnolls and orcs breed in its dark; there are chests in the den, the camp and the hoard behind the throne. <span class="thing">/tbatec locate tbatec:grand\_mountains\_warrens</span> finds one (operators).

## The Mutant

**Lord (100%)**: its own pounce, slam and roar<br>**The horde (66%)**: every half minute it rears and roars, then gnolls and orcs pour in from the tunnels (six at most)<br>**Black horns (33%)**: quicker and harder; it paws the ground while a dark lane marks its charge: step aside. Into a wall it is stunned

## Spoils

It keeps to its cave and heals if left alone. Slain: its horn, emeralds, its will now and then, +40 standing and war merit; its horde is leaderless.
