A vast campus at one end of Xyrus. Through the gatehouse (with the Academy gate) the Grand Allee of autumn trees runs to the central plaza.

The main building has twin towers, an auditorium for a thousand, the Grand Ballroom and the academy vault, with the bell tower and the dorms round it.

## Library and Stadium

The <span class="thing">Grand Library</span>: three gallery tiers round an open reading circle under a ribbed copper dome, with an [Aetherial War Table](/items/curiosities) at its heart.

The <span class="thing">Main Stadium</span> and three duelling rings with barrier pylons are where students fight.

## Practice Rings

At the campus's north edge stand four <span class="thing">practice rings</span>: a floor of [wind runes](/items/currents) whose updraft lifts you through three floating hoops, 6, 11 and 16 blocks up. Sneak to sink back down.
