The dwarven capital, inside one gigantic funnel-shaped cavern under the Darv desert. A gatehouse in the dunes, a spiral shaft and stone gates lead down.

**Where**: the Darv Abyss<br>**Rarity**: ~1 per 1,540 blocks<br>**Height**: at Y -38

## The Highway

Red paving spirals four times round the cavern wall, from the great plaza up to Lodenhold. The wall is carved with hundreds of homes, shops, smithies and taverns, and the arena hangs in the middle of the cavern on beams and chains.

## Lodenhold

The throne room, the royal vault, the glowing-mushroom garden and hot springs, and the Hall of Lords in its geode. Beyond: the Earthborn Institute, and the portal room down a grand stair with its [dormant portal](/items/curiosities).
