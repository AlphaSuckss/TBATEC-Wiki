Sapin's colossal fortress against the Beast Glades, spanning the gap between two snow-capped spurs, 20 blocks thick and six storeys tall.

## Inside

Stores, kitchens and mess halls; barracks and officers' quarters; the forge level, a long hall of forges; armouries; the signal room, conjurers' halls, the war room and a shooting gallery. Switchback stairs and haul shafts with chains and pulleys join every floor.

## The Gate

The gate: a sliding iron gate, murder holes, a winch hall and horn chambers in the gate towers.

On the Glades side: two bastions, then scorched killing ground with ditches and stake fields, a river gorge, and a hidden tunnel exit.

Sapin knights and a Quest Master hold it.
