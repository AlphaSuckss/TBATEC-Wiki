## Enrolment

Xyrus Academy, at one end of [Xyrus](/sites/xyrus), takes humans, elves and dwarves. Its teachers keep their posts on the campus: <span class="thing">Director Cynthia Goodsky</span> in the main building enrols new students.

**Who**: any mage with a core, but not the asura, the reborn or a wraith.

## Exams or Fee

**The exams**: three written questions (two right to pass), then the practical: spend mana on any spell or art near the Director, 10 for a black core up to 300 for a white, within a minute.

**Or the fee**, in emeralds by core: black 40, red 28, orange 18, yellow 10, silver 4, white nothing. A black core sits as a scholar mage, a red or better as a battle mage.

## Lectures

**7:00**: Mana Theory, Professor Avius, the Grand Library: core refinement<br>**9:00**: Mana Manipulation, Professor Geist, the Main Stadium: refinement and fire<br>**11:00**: Team Strategies, Professor Vanesy Glory, the north field: fire and earth<br>**13:00**: Wind, the rune rings; 15:00 Water, the reflecting pools

## Sitting a Lecture

Stay within 12 blocks of the teacher for a minute while the lecture runs (each lasts two in-game hours). Each subject counts once a day: +2 mastery in its element (Strategy +1.25 in each of two), or refinement worth 4% (Theory) or 2.5% (Manipulation) of your stage.

## The Ladder

At the duelling rings students stand for the <span class="thing">ranking ladder</span>: places 1 to 20 (21 is unranked). Speak to a student to challenge them; as in the books, you may only challenge someone ranked above you. Nobody dies: the duel ends at 15% of their health or a fifth of yours. Win, and their place is yours (+5 with Sapin).

## Duels

A student's health and blows grow with rank: 62 health at rank 20, 290 at rank 1. The top ten also loose volleys, the top five rush you. Lose, and rest two minutes before the next challenge.

Titles: Top Ten, and First of Xyrus Academy.

## The Committee

&#8203;<span class="thing">Claire Bladeheart</span> leads the Disciplinary Committee, which keeps the peace the Student Council's rules set. She takes on students with three lectures and a rank of 15 or better.

**Patrols**: once a day, four posts round the campus in ten minutes; a quarrel at one of them is settled by speaking to it.

## Perks and Graduation

**Perks**: 6 emeralds and +10 with Sapin a patrol, lectures give a quarter more, the title.

**Graduation**: beat off the attack on the Academy, sit twelve lectures and hold rank 10 or better; the Director then makes you a graduate (+50 with Sapin, a title).
