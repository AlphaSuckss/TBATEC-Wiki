Sapin's floating city, some 600 blocks across, hangs 95 blocks above the plains on its own lobed island of rock. Nobody climbs to Xyrus: the <span class="thing">Ground Gate</span>, a domed pavilion with Sapin's roads off its four sides, is its front door.

**Where**: Sapin's plains, only in Sapin's territory<br>**Rarity**: ~1 per 2,560 blocks<br>**Height**: 95 blocks above the ground

## The City

The [Academy](/sites/xyrus_academy) fills one end. West lie the <span class="thing">noble quarter</span> (the Noble Plaza and its gate, Helstea Manor with its ballroom, the Glayder royal mansion, Flamesworth House) and downtown with the Adventurers' Guild.

East: the <span class="thing">City Square</span> with the Founder's Fountain and the Helstea auction house; Market Street and the shops; the artificers' district and the elixir alley; and the <span class="thing">Gate Plaza</span> with the Sky Gate on the rim.

Sapin knights garrison it, and the Quest Master keeps the guild's board.

## Gates

Xyrus has twelve gates: the Academy, Noble Quarter, Market Square, Adventurers' Guild, Sky Gate and Ground Gate, and six road gates on the Gate Plaza whose banners name the roads they serve. See [Teleport Gates](/lands/gates).
