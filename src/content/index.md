<div class="home-hero"><img src="/icon.png" alt=""><p><strong>TBATEC</strong> brings the world of <em>The Beginning After the End</em> to Minecraft: mana cores that grow from Black to White, spells you shape yourself, the mana beasts of the Beast Glades, the kingdoms of Dicathen, the asura's hidden realm of Epheotus, the continent of Alacrya and the djinn's Relictombs.</p></div>

This wiki holds everything the mod's in-game guidebook, <em>The Mage's Codex</em>, holds: every system, creature, place, item and person, with recipes and the keys you press. It is kept in step with the Codex, so what you read here is what the game tells you.

> [!NOTE]
> This wiki describes TBATEC **1.29.0** for Minecraft **1.21.1** on **NeoForge**.

## Where to start

- New to the mod? Read [Installing](/guide/installing), then [Your First Day](/first_steps/first_day).
- Want the big picture? [The road from Black to White](/guide/progression) walks the whole game in order.
- Looking for something? Use the search box at the top (press <kbd>/</kbd>), or open a chapter in the sidebar.

## About the mod

TBATEC is made by AlphaSucks. It is a fan work based on the novel <em>The Beginning After the End</em> by TurtleMe and is not affiliated with TurtleMe or Tapas. What the novel describes is drawn from its text; anything the mod had to invent to make a game of it is marked as the mod's own in the Codex.
