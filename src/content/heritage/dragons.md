The only race that can wield <span class="thing">aether</span>, though it answers few of them. Their Indrath clan leads the Great Eight, and in true form they are great winged dragons.

At birth it is drawn whether aether answers you (one dragon in four) and in which branch: **Aevum** (time), **Vivum** (life) or **Spatium** (space). Only that branch's arts open to you. **Realmheart** wakes in one in five of those. You are told which when you are born. <code>\[the odds are invented\]</code>

**Clan**: Indrath<br>**Art**: Pure mana; for a few, aether<br>**Signature arts**: Dragon's Breath, King's Force

## Born With

+20 hearts, +10 armour, +4 attack, Resistance I always. A little taller, mines 20% faster, jumps 10% higher, reaches half a block further. Horns; the wings belong to the true form.
