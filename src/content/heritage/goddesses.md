*Ours: no part of the books.*

A winged being of light. Three pairs of small white wings, gold at the tips, stand from her back, and a golden aura rises off her wherever she goes; it sinks low while she crouches.

A goddess is never born from the heritage menu and never rolled. She is made by command (<span class="thing">/tbatec origin &lt;player&gt; race goddess</span>), or the server lists her: a player on the list <span class="thing">goddessPlayers</span> is made one whenever she stands without a race, on joining and again after a rebirth.

## The Core

She is born with a <span class="thing">White core</span> and it stays White; its pool is twice a grown asura's, and she flies on it as any White core does.

Like an asura she is bound to one art, <span class="thing">Ark</span>, the white flame, on fire: she casts every common spell form through it and nothing else. She is no clan of Epheotus: no home there, no age, no true form, and no leave to ask.

## The Ward

Whatever she has not struck first deals her a fifth of its blow. Strike a creature, by hand or by spell, and the ward no longer holds against that one until it dies or is gone.

Only blows that come from a creature are warded: a fall, fire and the void are not.

## The Body

Forty more hearts, armour like an asura's and a blow past a titan's. Her body mends like an asura's, she falls slowly and unhurt, and <span class="thing">wither</span>, <span class="thing">darkness</span> and <span class="thing">blindness</span> find no hold on her.

## The White Flame

Her spells strike half again as hard as an asura's art. What the white flame touches burns on and is marked by its light, and the <span class="thing">undead</span> take double from it and falter.

## Her Spells

**White Flames**: a wide sweep of white fire.<br>**Ark**: a sphere of golden light that holds what it catches, seals its mana and sears it.<br>**Invigorate**: mends her and her allies and lifts poison and rot.<br>**Disintegration**: one white beam that takes a share of a creature's whole life and unmakes a lesser one left under a third. It is costly and slow to come again.

All four are in the Workshop under <span class="thing">Ark</span>.
