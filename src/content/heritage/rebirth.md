## Crystal of Rebirth

<div class="spotlight"><img class="icon big" src="/img/item/tbatec/rebirth_crystal.png" alt="Crystal of Rebirth" title="Crystal of Rebirth" loading="lazy"><div>

Hold use for two seconds to be reborn; let go early and nothing is spent. The crystal shatters.

</div></div>

Rebirth reopens the heritage menu and rolls everything again: race, core, elements, gifts, class and the practice toward deviant arts.

Your core starts over at dark <span class="thing">Black</span> with no mana. Every spell you have up ends and every TBATEC effect on you is lifted.

You keep your [elemental mastery](/spellcraft/mastery) and your spell library, less the spells of a magic you no longer hold: those leave the Workshop by themselves.

An asura who is reborn loses its home in Epheotus: its respawn point goes back to the world spawn.

A wraith can't be reborn. The recipe is under [Crystal of Rebirth](/items/rebirth_crystal).
