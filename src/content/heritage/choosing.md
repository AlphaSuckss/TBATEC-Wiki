Your heritage is chosen once, in the menu that opens when you first join. Until you choose, nothing can hurt you.

Pick [Human](/heritage/humans), [Elf](/heritage/elves) or [Dwarf](/heritage/dwarves), or the fourth card, **Feeling Lucky?**, which lets the mana pick your race as well.

## Feeling Lucky?

First the mana decides whether you are an [asura](/heritage/asura) at all: **1%**. Only then is the asuran race drawn:

**Phoenix, Titan, Hamadryad, Leviathan, Sylph**: 0.15% each<br>**Pantheon**: 0.12%<br>**Dragon, Basilisk**: 0.065% each, the rarest of all

Otherwise: human, elf or dwarf, 33% each. Luck is the only way to be born an asura.

## The Roll

Then the mana rolls the rest:

1. Does a <span class="thing">mana core</span> awaken?<br>2. If so, your first <span class="thing">element</span>, weighted by race.<br>3. Each further element, only if the one before came.<br>4. A rare <span class="thing">gift</span> (mages only).<br>5. Your class: [Augmenter or Conjurer](/mana_core/classes), even odds.

A reveal shows what you got, with a line for how rare your elements are: single, dual, tri or <span class="thing">quadra-elemental</span>.

&#8203;<span class="thing">Deviant arts</span> are not rolled: they awaken later, through practice. See [Deviant Arts](/spellcraft/deviant_arts).

To start again, see [Rebirth](/heritage/rebirth).
