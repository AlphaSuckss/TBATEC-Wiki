Some are born able to feel hostility. Any hostile creature within 24 blocks that has you as its target glows for three seconds (at most once every ten seconds each). Out of sight, you hear it and read <span class="thing">You sense danger...</span>

30% of elves have it, and every Diviner and Pantheon.

It also feels a [mana signature](/mana_core/sensing) stronger than your own nearby (up to 48 blocks for the strongest), from a beast that could fight or another player (where players can fight), and tells you which way it lies: <span class="thing">a powerful mana signature</span>, or <span class="thing">an overwhelming presence</span> when it is far beyond you. At most every 15 seconds, once a minute for each.

A suppressed signature, Mirage Walk and Sylvia slip past it.
