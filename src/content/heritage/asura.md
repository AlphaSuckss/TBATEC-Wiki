The gods of [Epheotus](/epheotus/realm), ruled by the clans of <span class="thing">the Great Eight</span>. An asura can only be born by luck: **Feeling Lucky?** in the heritage menu, 1 in 100.

Every asura is born with a <span class="thing">White core</span> and is given an [Asura Sigil](/items/asura_sigil).

## Growing Up

Asura do not purify their cores by training as lesser mages do: a core matures as its asura grows up. A new asura is a <span class="thing">child</span>, an <span class="thing">adolescent</span> after 10 hours of play as an asura, and an <span class="thing">adult</span> after 30.

A child holds 41% of an adult's mana, an adolescent 72%: a grown asura holds 400,000 (a dragon 600,000, a basilisk 520,000, a titan or pantheon 340,000). Its body is smaller and its gifts weaker (55%, then 80%), and its arts hit somewhat less hard. Even so, a child outclasses any White-core mage of Dicathen. Left alone for five seconds, an asura's body mends a health a second (a young one less).

Its art opens as it grows <code>\[invented\]</code>: a child shapes the first third of its clan's spells, an adolescent two thirds, an adult all of them, and King's Force.

## How You Look

Open the Character screen and press **Look** to choose which of your race's features show on you: horns, frills, crests, extra eyes and the rest, each on or off. Everyone sees what you chose. It changes nothing but looks.

A dragon's wings never show on its human form: in the books the Indrath are winged only as dragons, and some have horns in human form and some do not.

## Home

When the reveal closes, an asura wakes on its own clan's ground in [Epheotus](/epheotus/realm): a dragon at Everburn's gate, the other races at their clan seats. That becomes its home and its respawn point, beds and all, for as long as it stays an asura.

The asura do not walk among the lesser races: one born here leaves Epheotus only once it is grown and has Lord Indrath's leave, which is given to those who pass [Kordri's](/mentors/kordri) three trials <code>\[invented\]</code>. Until then the sigil stays dark in Epheotus.

## Its Magic

An asura has no class, so nothing is locked behind one, and it never gathers mana before a cast.

It wields <span class="thing">its race's art alone</span>: every common spell form, cast through that art, plus two [signature arts](/spellcraft/asuran_arts). The [Pure Mana](/spellcraft/pure_mana) arts are open to it too.

Through its art an asura's spells hit 50% harder (aether a further 25%), and they can be pushed far past any lesser mage's: 75% more power, 40% more range, 25% more speed, 50% more duration.

A few forms belong to one race: Mana Brand to the phoenix; Rending Fissure and Earth Bastion to the titan; the spear and the field to phoenix and leviathan; Wind Gust to the sylph.
