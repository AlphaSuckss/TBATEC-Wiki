Shadow given will. A wraith is cloaked in darkness from horn to heel, with two white eyes burning out of it and bone horns curving up from its temples.

A wraith is never born from the heritage menu: it is made by command (<span class="thing">/tbatec origin &lt;player&gt; race wraith</span>), or by the server's list of wraith players.

## No Magic

A wraith never has a <span class="thing">mana core</span> and never will. It casts nothing, and magic cannot touch it: no spell or spell effect, no aether, no potion or harming, no healing art. It can't be reborn with a <span class="item">Crystal of Rebirth</span>.

## The Body

What it has is a body: twenty more hearts, armour like plate, and only 60% of any blow that isn't magic gets through. Its blows tear away part of a creature's very life (3% of its health, up to 60), so even the great beasts fall. It is fast, sure-footed and hard to move, and after five seconds unhurt it knits itself back together, a heart every two seconds.

## Spiritual Pressure

Press <kbd title="Spiritual Pressure (Wraith)">N</kbd> to bear down with the wraith's will for 20 seconds. It rises with a burst: everything within 7 blocks is thrown back, and a ring of white light and wind blasts out over the ground.

A white aura spirals up around the wraith while it lasts. Press it again to end it early.

Everything within 14 blocks is crushed under it. The spells of any core below <span class="thing">White</span> come apart inside it: bolts, beams, walls, traps, shields, flight. Nothing below White can be cast there.

Everything alive slows under the weight. A Black core is nearly pinned, and a White core feels only a mild drag; beasts by their class.

Those caught inside see the world drain to grey and darken at the edges. Lines of force streak down their sight, the picture sinks toward the ground on every beat of the pressure, and their view shakes under it.

As the pressure runs out it eases off over its last seconds rather than vanishing. It takes 60 seconds to recover.

## Projection

Press <kbd title="Projection (Wraith)">B</kbd> to leave the body standing and let the wraith's sight drift free. Fly it with the movement keys, through walls, up to 48 blocks away.

The body can't act while it's empty, and the first blow it takes drags the wraith back into it. Anyone who looks at the spot where its sight hangs sees the air bend.

## Sonido

Press <kbd title="Sonido (Wraith)">Y</kbd> to cross up to 16 blocks so fast the wraith vanishes: to the spot it looks at, or right up to the creature it looks at. The air cleaves with a sharp zip, and for a moment the wraith can't be seen at all.

It stops dead where it reappears. No mana, and it's ready again in about a second.

## Super Jump

&#8203;<kbd title="Super Jump on/off (Wraith)">J</kbd> switches the Super Jump on or off. While it's on, every jump clears two full blocks. Hold <kbd title="Sneak">Shift</kbd> on the ground and a meter fills under the crosshair; jump while it's full, or a moment after letting go, and the wraith goes as high as the charge, up to 24 blocks.

Coming down from it never hurts.
