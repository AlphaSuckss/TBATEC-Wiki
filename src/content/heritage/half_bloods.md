Now and then a human, elf or dwarf has an asura among their forebears. When you pick your race, by its card or **Feeling Lucky?**, there is a **5%** chance you are a <span class="thing">half-blood</span>.

Asura and [wraiths](/heritage/wraiths) never are. There are no half-dragons and no djinn-blooded.

**Lore**: the books' half-bloods are few: Chul, half phoenix, and Alacrya's Vritra-blooded. The other bloods and every number here are the mod's own \[invented\].

## The Blood

Then the blood is drawn, the better the rarer (weights out of 96 in Dicathen):<br>**Sylph**: 24: 1.25%<br>**Hamadryad**: 22: 1.15%<br>**Leviathan**: 20: 1.04%<br>**Titan**: 14: 0.73%<br>**Pantheon**: 10: 0.52%<br>**Phoenix**: 6: 0.31%

It is drawn once you know where you were raised. Only a human [raised in Alacrya](/alacrya/upbringing) can carry the <span class="thing">Vritra</span>'s: weight 4, so 4 in 100 of Alacrya's half-bloods (0.2% of all).

## What It Gives

A half-blood always has a <span class="thing">mana core</span> \[invented\].

**Mana**: half again a pure mage's pool, same race and rank.<br>**Body**: three more hearts, and a small trait.

**Blood art**: its clan's [asuran art](/spellcraft/asuran_arts), at 60% of a grown asura's strength. It rides on any spell of its element from the first day; its own spells open at a <span class="thing">White</span> core. Every normal spell stays yours.

## Alacrya

Raised in Alacrya, a mage designs nothing: the art comes as <span class="thing">blood spells</span> beside the runes, a bolt of it from the first day and the clan's own spells at White.

A Vritra-blooded is born with <span class="thing">latent</span> Vritra blood that manifests at Silver, as any Alacryan's: black horns, and the Vritra runes open. Their art is <span class="thing">Decay</span>, without Soulfire.

## Half-Sylph

**Art**: Sylph Gale

Falls 8% lighter and jumps 5% higher.

**Looks**: A wind-pale streak over the crown, a feather at the temple.

## Half-Hamadryad

**Art**: Living Wood

Sunlight heals half a heart every 5 seconds.

**Looks**: Freckles of bark and leaf.

## Half-Leviathan

**Art**: Leviathan Tide

Breath lasts twice as long; swims a third faster.

**Looks**: Fine scales at the neck.

## Half-Titan

**Art**: Titan Earth

8% bigger, +1 armour, harder to knock back.

**Looks**: A heavier frame, stone-grey knuckles.

## Half-Pantheon

**Art**: Force

+1 attack damage.

**Looks**: Amber eyes.

## Half-Phoenix

**Art**: Phoenix Fire

Fire burns half as long. Once a day a killing blow flares you back up at a quarter health.

**Looks**: One ember-orange eye, ember feathers at the temples.

## The Scorn

The asura think mixing with lesser races thins the blood, and they say so \[lore\].

**Prices**: asura traders ask half again (a stack of emeralds at most).<br>**Words**: the clans, the steward, [Windsom](/mentors/windsom) and Kordri each have theirs.

## The Way In

Epheotus is harder to enter, never closed \[invented\].

1. An [Asura Sigil](/items/asura_sigil) only answers a half-blood's <span class="thing">Silver</span> core.<br>2. The Indrath steward never writes your name himself.

3. With a White core he will, if an asura speaks for you: your [asura bond](/companions/bond_of_equals), or Windsom.<br>4. Windsom gives his word a day after he first met you, and only carries you about once you are White and an asura stands for you.

With the invitation the way on is a lesser's: Windsom speaks to Kordri, and the training cave opens.
