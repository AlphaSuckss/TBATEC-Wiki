Every asura has a humanoid form and a <span class="thing">true form</span>. Most keep the humanoid one, which spares their mana; dragons can take any shape they like.

Press <kbd title="True Form (tap: take it, or its art; hold: let it go)">Left Alt</kbd> to take yours. Tap it again in the form to loose the form's art; hold it, or sneak and press it, to return.

## The Cost

Taking the form costs 8% of your mana, and holding it 0.5% a second. It lasts 150 seconds at most (75 for an adolescent, whose form is smaller and weaker). A child can't hold one yet.

Afterwards you must rest: 60 seconds, plus half the time you spent in it, up to 180.

## Limits

The form breaks if you fall under 15% health, if your mana runs low, or if the place closes in round it, and then you rest 60 seconds longer. It needs room to be taken; a great dragon won't fit in a corridor.

Not while riding or asleep, nor in the aether orb or the Relictombs. Half-bloods, wraiths and the reborn have no true form.

## The Forms

**Dragon**: a great dragon, white-feathered and gold-marked like the Indrath. It flies and breathes aether.

**Basilisk**: a vast horned serpent with eight eyes. Its wings carry a glide; its bite and breath rot.

**Phoenix**: a bird of living flame. The fastest flier; its art bursts into fire round it.

**Leviathan**: a sea serpent with forelimbs and tentacles. Swift in water, slow on land; it raises a wave.

**Titan**: grows to giant height, stony and hard to move; it stamps the ground.

**Pantheon**: horns over the face, a second pair of arms, more eyes and golden scales that heal; four-armed blows.

**Hamadryad**: a tall tree given life, bark-armoured; roots seize its foes and it mends.

**Sylph**: a body of cloud and gale that flies and turns arrows; a cyclone throws foes off.

## Flight

In a flying form, jump in the air (or double-tap jump) to take wing; jump climbs and sneak sinks, and flying forward the nose leads: look up to climb, down to dive.

Spells, mana arts and your race's own arts work as before in the form. A beast form holds nothing in its hands.
