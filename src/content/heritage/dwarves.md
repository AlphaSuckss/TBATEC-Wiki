The stout folk of [Darv](/lands/darv), who live beneath the mountains.

**Mana core**: 50%.<br>**Elements**: earth and fire come easily, water and wind rarely. A second comes 20% of the time, a third 5% of those, a fourth 1% of those: about 1 dwarf mage in 10,000 is quadra-elemental.

**Training**: fire and earth 50% faster; water and wind 20% slower.

**Deviant arts**: Metal (1,500 practice, and any element counts toward it), Magma (9,000), Gravity (15,000).

**Body**: 1.5 blocks tall, +1 heart, mines 35% faster, hard to knock back.

## Dwarven-forged

Any tool, weapon or armour a dwarf crafts comes out <span class="thing">dwarven-forged</span>: 25% more durability and one more level of Unbreaking (up to III), marked in its tooltip.
