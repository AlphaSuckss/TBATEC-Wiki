**Lore**: a mage gives off mana, a signature a keen sense picks up: roughly how strong, and from where. A mage can hold theirs in to pass unnoticed.

With a <span class="thing">Yellow</span> core or better you feel every signature within reach, through walls: a pip round your crosshair on its side.

## The Pips

**Grey**: weaker than you<br>**White**: about your strength<br>**Gold**: stronger<br>**Red**: overwhelming<br>**Teal**: a party member

Larger is nearer; hollow is veiled.

**Reach**: the stronger the signature and the keener your core, the further: a Yellow core feels a White mage about 24 blocks off, a White core 32, never past 64.

Other players only where PvP is on (party members always).

## Hiding

**Suppression** (<kbd title="Suppress Mana Signature (hold: release your mana)">J</kbd>, see [Sensing](/mana_core/sensing)) hides you from this sense too, but one two tiers above you still feels you, veiled, at half the reach. Holding yours in halves your own reach; a spell shows you for 3 s; Mirage Walk hides you from all.
