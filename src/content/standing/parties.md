A party is a band of up to **6** players who cannot hurt each other, travel together and talk among themselves. Nothing else is shared: no loot, no experience, no Relictombs insight.

**/party invite &lt;player&gt;**: the leader asks<br>**/party accept**: or click Accept

## Commands

**/party leave**: go; the lead passes on<br>**/party kick &lt;player&gt;**: the leader removes one<br>**/party promote &lt;player&gt;**: hand the lead on<br>**/party disband**: the leader ends it<br>**/party list**: who is in it

**No friendly fire**: blows, arrows and other projectiles, spells, pets, golems and asura bonds of one member never hurt another, and harmful effects from a member do not take. A member's pets never pick another member as a target.

## Your Allies

A spell that <span class="thing">helps</span> (a healing art, a field that strengthens those inside it) reaches you, every member of your party, and every beast, companion or golem that answers to one of you.

With no party, it also reaches players who are in no party themselves and are not fighting you, and their tamed animals. Never a Wraith.

A spell that harms no longer picks your party out as targets.

## Party Chat

**/pc &lt;message&gt;**: one line to the party<br>**/party chat**: a toggle: everything you type goes to the party

## Travelling Together

**Gates**: when the leader goes through a [teleport gate](/lands/gates), every member within 10 blocks goes too (<span class="thing">/party follow off</span> to stay).<br>**Summon**: <span class="thing">/party summon</span> asks every member in the same dimension; one who clicks stands still and unhurt for 3 s and is set down beside the leader (every 2 minutes).

A summon never reaches into or out of the Relictombs or a mind realm, and never crosses dimensions.

**The Relictombs**: unchanged. A party does not replace the [simulets](/relictombs/ascents): link them to go in together.

The party shows top left on your screen.
