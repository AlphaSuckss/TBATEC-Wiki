**Lore**: the asura gave each royal house of Dicathen two artifacts, bestowed on its two strongest in a knighting and bound by oath: the [Lances](/alacrya/lances), two of each race, known by code names. In the war the Council of the three crowns, with Virion as its commander, sends them out.

The book's six keep their seats. In this mod the Council names more \[invented\]: anyone proven is bound to the crown that stands best with them.

**To be named**:<br>**Raised**: in Dicathen; not an asura or a Wraith<br>**Core**: Silver or better (or the aether path)

**Merit**: 100 war merit: Alacryan soldiers (3 each), Wraiths, Retainers (50), Scythes (100), the Council's work; a Lance beaten in a duel counts 50.<br>**Standing**: Honoured with one kingdom (your seat), no worse than Neutral with the other two.

## The Appointment

Once you qualify, [Virion](/mentors/virion) tells you the Council has noticed you. <span class="thing">Sneak and use him</span> for Council business: what you still lack, then the appointment. Pick one of the code names he offers, or give your own (3 to 16 letters; nothing coarse; none of the six's).

You receive the artifact of an empty seat, as Virion once offered Alea's to Arthur \[book\]: <span class="item">Alea's Artifact</span>, or for Darv <span class="item">Olfred's Artifact</span>. It is bonded to you there, with the crown's blood and yours: a Silver core becomes White on the spot, and its limiter is the Lances' seal. And an <span class="item">Adamantine Lance Tag</span>. Your code name becomes a title; the whole server hears of it.

## Missions

Sneak-use Virion for a <span class="thing">Council mission</span>, three a day: break a battlegroup (12 soldiers), cut off its officers, cleanse corrupted beasts, hunt Wraiths, defeat a Retainer, hold the Glades. Each pays emeralds, standing with your seat (and a little with the others) and war merit.
