Sneak and use a <span class="thing">guard</span> for its people's work, once they count you **Friendly**: a kingdom's knights and guards, Alacrya's town guards, an asura clan's warriors. One task at a time; three a day; your own kills and your pets' count (a party shares none).

## Requests

**Kingdoms**: slay 8 hostile beasts (D or above) in their land: 8 emeralds, +25. Honoured: 5 Alacryan soldiers, 16, +40.<br>**The Bloods**: 8 wild beasts in Alacrya: 8, +25. Honoured: 4 of class B or above, 16, +40.<br>**A clan**: 6 hostile beasts (A or above) in Epheotus: 10, +30. Honoured: 2 of class S, 20, +50.

Turn a finished task in where you took it. Sneak-use the guard again to see how far along you are, or to give it up.
