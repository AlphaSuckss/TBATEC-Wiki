Every people of the world keeps a <span class="thing">standing</span> for you, from **-1000** to **1000**: the three kingdoms (<span class="thing">Sapin</span>, <span class="thing">Elenoir</span>, <span class="thing">Darv</span>), <span class="thing">the Alacryan Bloods</span> (Alacrya's noble houses and towns), <span class="thing">the Vritra</span> and the asura clans, one per race.

See yours on the character sheet (<kbd title="Character Screen">I</kbd>) or with <span class="thing">/reputation</span>.

## The Tiers

**Hated**: -600 or worse<br>**Hostile**: -250<br>**Unfriendly**: -50<br>**Neutral**: -49 to 49<br>**Friendly**: 50<br>**Honoured**: 250<br>**Revered**: 600 and up

## What It Changes

**Prices**: Unfriendly x1.2, Neutral x1, Friendly x0.95, Honoured x0.9, Revered x0.8, at their traders and services.<br>**Hostile**: they will not trade with you or serve you, and their guards attack on sight.<br>**Guards**: eye you when Unfriendly, salute you when Honoured.<br>**Work**: requests from Friendly, better ones from Honoured.

## Where You Start

**Raised in Dicathen**: your race's kingdom Friendly (+100), the others Neutral, the Bloods Unfriendly (-100), the Vritra Hostile (-300).<br>**Raised in Alacrya**: the kingdoms -100, the Bloods and the Vritra +100.<br>**Asura**: your clan Honoured (+300), the others +50, the Vritra Hostile.

## Up and Down

**Their people**: killing townsfolk -40, guards and knights -60, an asura -80 (and -20 with every clan).<br>**Their enemies**: an Alacryan soldier +3 for the kingdoms (one raised in Dicathen); a corrupted beast +2; a C-class beast or worse in a kingdom +1 with it. At most 60 a day per faction.<br>**Trade**: +1 a trade, at most 20 a day.

## Deeds

**A Scythe slain**: +60 with each kingdom, the Vritra -150.<br>**A Retainer slain**: +30, the Vritra -80.<br>**Others**: a Wraith squad, Olfred, a Lance beaten in a duel, the Unsealing, an asura bond (+100 with its clan), Windsom's word, Guild ranks, blood favour and rank.

## The Scorn

A [half-blood](/heritage/half_bloods) pays the asura a <span class="thing">scorn</span> on top of every price: half again at first. Every point of standing you earn with any clan wears it down, to +10% after 1,000 points; it never goes. And no clan will ever revere a half-blood (Honoured at most).

**Lore**: the scorn is the book's; the numbers are the mod's.
