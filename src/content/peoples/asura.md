Every clan's seat in Epheotus is home to its people: townsfolk who trade what their race makes, and warriors who wield their race's two arts. They leave a peaceful visitor alone. Strike one, and it lets its pressure out - a blast that throws you back - and its clan's warriors come; a warrior turning on you makes you falter.

Their trades are rich and dear: aether and old lore, gems and forged steel, pearls and tridents, wind charges and feathers. An asura of the same race pays a quarter less.
