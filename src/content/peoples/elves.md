## Elven Villager

Elven villagers live in the Forest of Elshire and in its halls and capital.

## Trades

Each elven villager offers seven of these, restocked every morning:

**1 emerald**: 16 arrows, 12 sweet berries, 8 glow berries, or 4 oak saplings<br>**3 / 4 emeralds**: a Potion of Swiftness / of Regeneration<br>**6 emeralds**: an enchanted bow, or an E-class core

**9 emeralds**: a golden apple<br>**14 emeralds**: a D-class core<br>**20 wheat or 14 string**: 1 emerald

An elf pays a quarter less.

## Elven Warden

Wardens guard their people against anything that threatens them.

## Elven Warden

30 health. Zestier's city guard wear its black uniform with green trim and a golden guard on the left shoulder, and fight with the rapier; the mist wardens of the Elshire go hooded in grey-green with a longbow that fires every second or so at up to 16 blocks.

Wardens are found in the forest, and kept at their posts in Zestier, the Elven Tree Halls, the Elshire Shrines, the Flying Castle and the elven gate shrines.
