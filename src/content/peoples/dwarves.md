## Trades

Each dwarven villager offers seven of these, restocked every morning:

**1 emerald**: 4 iron ingots, 16 coal or 12 redstone<br>**2 emeralds**: 3 gold ingots<br>**6 emeralds**: an anvil, or an enchanted crossbow<br>**7 / 8 emeralds**: an enchanted iron pickaxe / iron axe

**8 emeralds**: a diamond<br>**24 emeralds**: a C-class core<br>**6 raw iron or 4 raw gold**: 1 emerald

A dwarf pays a quarter less.
