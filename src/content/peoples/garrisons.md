## Garrison Standard

A <span class="item">garrison standard</span> keeps its post manned: up to three villagers or guards of one kind (sometimes more), refilled one at a time while a player is within 48 blocks. Its people never stray far from it.

</div></div>

## Keeping the Peace

Villagers flee monsters and wild beasts. Strike one, and every guard of its people within 28 blocks comes for you.

Guards fight monsters (except creepers) and wild hostile beasts on sight. They never trouble a peaceful player, and only turn on one who strikes them or their villagers.

## Good to Know

Kin don't breed, and they never despawn.

Every trader gives a quarter off to someone of their own race.

Neither the standard nor its posts can be broken in survival.
