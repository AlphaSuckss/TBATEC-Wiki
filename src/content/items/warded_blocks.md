## Finding It

The Aetherial Council's chest in Castle Indrath always holds one, and djinn relic chests (in the Djinn Sanctuary, the Flying Castle, the Hearth and Sylvia's Cave) sometimes do.

A rune gate won't close on anyone standing in it. Without the key, the runes refuse you.
