## Beast Cores

<div class="spotlight"><img class="icon big" src="/img/item/tbatec/beast_core_a.png" alt="A-Class Beast Core" title="A-Class Beast Core" loading="lazy"><div>

The crystallised core of a mana beast. Its colour tells its class (see [Beast Classes](/beasts/classes)).

Hold use to <span class="thing">absorb</span> it and refine your own core.

</div></div>

## Absorbing

Each class refines a share of your current shade, and takes longer to absorb:

**E**: 4%, 1.2 s<br>**D**: 7%, 1.6 s<br>**C**: 12%, 2 s<br>**B**: 20%, 2.4 s<br>**A**: 32%, 2.8 s<br>**AA**: 50%, 3.2 s<br>**S**: 80%, 3.6 s<br>**SS**: 125%, 4 s<br>**Asuran**: 200%, 4.4 s

## Using Them

You need a core to absorb one, and a White core takes no more. Refinement stops at a [trial](/mana_core/trials) until you pass it.

Feed a core to a companion for experience and loyalty, or use it to bind a beast: see [Contracts](/companions/contracts).

## Finding Them

Mana beasts drop the core of their class (see [Beast Classes](/beasts/classes)), and awakened creatures of the Glades sometimes do.

Elves sell E- and D-class cores; dwarves sell C-class. Guild vaults, auction vaults, hunters' caches and the treasuries of the great sites hold more.

No wild beast carries an <span class="thing">SS</span> core: they lie only in dragon hoards and asuran treasuries. Asuran cores come from the beasts of Epheotus, and from dragon hoards.
