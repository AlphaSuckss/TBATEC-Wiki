## Light Orbs

<div class="spotlight"><img class="icon big" src="/img/item/tbatec/light_orb.png" alt="Light Orb" title="Light Orb" loading="lazy"><div>

Floating orbs of light with nothing to bump into. Warm <span class="item">light orbs</span> hang over Epheotus's roads and halls and in Xyrus; violet <span class="item">aether orbs</span> crown waystones; cold <span class="item">pale orbs</span> drift as the Grey's wisps.

</div></div>

## Taking Them

They can be picked up and placed again (even underwater). Light orbs turn up in elven royal chests and dragon homes, aether orbs in djinn relics.

**Aether flame** (violet) and **white flame** burn forever without spreading or burning anything; they vanish when broken.
