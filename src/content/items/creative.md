The creative tab holds a spawn egg for every beast and for each of the kin.

It also holds nine <span class="thing">debug items</span>, one for each common spell shape: right-click one with an element item in your off-hand (a water bucket, lava bucket or blaze powder, dirt or cobblestone, a feather...) to cast that shape, free of mana and cooldown.
