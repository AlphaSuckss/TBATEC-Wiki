## Aether Wellspring

<div class="spotlight"><img class="icon big" src="/img/item/tbatec/aether_wellspring.png" alt="Aether Wellspring" title="Aether Wellspring" loading="lazy"><div>

A spring of raw aether. It throws violet flame and, every so often, a geyser; everyone within 5 blocks regenerates. Mine it with a pickaxe to take it with you.

</div></div>

Two are known: at the heart of the [Everburn Fountain](/epheotus/everburn), and in the sapphire pool of Lord Indrath's training cave under [Mount Geolus](/epheotus/geolus).
