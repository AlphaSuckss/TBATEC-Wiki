A totem of undying, diamonds, an eye of ender, and one rare thing of each continent.

## The Three Materials

**An Aether Orb**, from the relic rooms the djinn left in Dicathen.

**A Vritra Horn**, from the crypts, armouries and basilisk laboratories of Alacrya.

**A Mourning Pearl**, which only Lord Veruhn gives, in [Ecclesia](/epheotus/ecclesia): three in a lifetime, to those the Eccleiah think well of.

&#8203;<code>The crystal is the mod's own; so is its recipe.</code>

## Finding One

Crystals also turn up in Kezess's sanctuary in Castle Indrath, and now and then in the vaults and treasuries of the great sites, Dicathen's and Epheotus's alike.

Wraiths can't use one. After a rebirth, a short cooldown passes before another.
