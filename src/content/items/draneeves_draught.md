## Draneeve's Draught

The Alacryan drug the radicals of Xyrus Academy passed round. Drink it: for **2 minutes** every spell costs half its mana and hits a quarter harder.

## The Price

Then it is paid for, always: your core <span class="thing">backlashes</span> for a minute, and your body gives up <span class="thing">life</span>: a quarter of your health for five minutes, a quarter more for each draught drunk before the last is paid (three quarters at worst).

Draneeve always carries one, Lucas Wykes often, a radical sometimes.
