## The Ascenders

Alacrya's <span class="thing">ascenders</span> are the mages licensed to climb the Relictombs. The <span class="thing">Ascenders Association</span> in Aramoor (a rhombus of halls round a court, statues of battle mages on its approaches) keeps the register, sets the assessment and finds candidates their principals.

Three steps, as the books tell it: **register**, pass the **assessment**, survive a **preliminary ascent**. Then the badge is yours.

## Registering

Talk to a <span class="thing">clerk</span> in the clerks' hall. On the Alacryan path your blood name is enough. Raised in Dicathen, you need someone's word: forged papers or a blood's vouch (see [Dicathen-born Ascenders](/relictombs/dicathen_born)).

Ascenders are mages: a coreless person is turned away, and the Relictombs never open to an asura.

## The Assessment

The <span class="thing">examiner</span> at the desk by the Proctors' Tower calls you down to one of six numbered doors along the hall's west wall. Through it lies a cube about fifty yards a side; observers watch from a gallery behind glass near its top.

You wear a <span class="thing">runed suit</span>: it takes half of every blow and will not let you die (a killing blow ends the trial instead). Nobody tells you what to do.

## Three Trials

Three trials, as Arthur met them:

**Arachnids**: three giant rune-armoured spiders rise as the floor tiles slide back; drive each to half its strength<br>**The flood**: tiles rise toward a crystal under the ceiling while the hall fills with water: touch it first<br>**The tiles**: a field that drains your core; each safe tile lights the next for an instant; the ones behind fall away

## Grades

The examiners grade five things, as they graded Arthur: **offensive flexibility** (damage and kinds of attack), **mana manipulation** (the core kept on the tiles; hiding your signature helps), **athleticism** (the climb), **mental acuity** (the tiles) and **survivability**.

Each is out of 20; 50 of 100 passes. A failure may try again after a day. The total is also your first ascender XP.

## The Preliminary Ascent

Passed, you are a <span class="thing">candidate</span>. The <span class="thing">principals</span> wait by the board in the clerks' hall: talk to one to join their group (up to four). Set out, or fill the group, and they lead you up.

The preliminary ascent is short: the <span class="thing">Proving Hall</span>, a trial of beams or plates, a sanctuary, and out at Maerin, where <span class="thing">Chief Mason</span> issues your badge. Your first simulet comes free.

## The Proving Hall

One square room, seen whole from where you arrive. The principal says what will happen first. Then two rounds come out of the four dark arches: <span class="thing">deadly shrews</span>, then a few <span class="thing">carallians</span>. Clear them and the north door opens.

A party with an <span class="thing">aether</span> user in it is not given this room. The tombs answer aether with the long hall of waking statues, and say so as you set out. (The room is the mod's own; the books do not describe an ordinary first ascent.)

## A Word of Warning

A principal is paid a stipend for each candidate, and some, the books say, have taken the stipend, killed their candidates and blamed the Relictombs.

One principal in ten (a server can change it) turns on the group as the second zone opens. The suit is gone by then. If you live, Maerin still gives you your badge.
