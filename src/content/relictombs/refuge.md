## The Djinn's Refuge

Long ago the asura of the Indrath clan wiped out the <span class="thing">djinn</span>, a people of aether mages. Before the end, hundreds of them built the <span class="thing">Relictombs</span>: a refuge, and a store of everything they knew, laid out zone after zone so that each one teaches something about aether.

Time, space and life do not behave there as they do outside. Its air is thick with ambient aether.

## Barred to the Asura

The djinn made it so that <span class="thing">no asura can enter</span>: its wards of space turn them away. The mod keeps that rule: an asura is refused at every way in, and one carried inside by other means is sent straight back out.

Today the Vritra let lesser mages, the <span class="thing">ascenders</span>, loot it for them, and the only known ways in are in Alacrya: the Ascension Chamber in Aramoor leads in, the Descension Chamber in Maerin is where ascenders come out.

## The Shape of It

In the game the Relictombs are a world of their own, beyond a void, under a bright sky with no sun, moon or stars.

**The First Level**: quarries, fenced fields of blood oxen and the guarded glass portal upward<br>**The Second Level**: a district of the city under the sky: inns, shops, the Association, and the arch<br>**The ascents**: zones raised for each party, far out in the void, and gone when they leave

## The Air

Mana is slower to come back inside: about three quarters of its usual rate (the thick aether, the mod's reading; the books leave the question open). Aether, for those who can hold it, is quick to gather.

Each zone of an ascent has its own sky: the levels' gold-white, a convergence's crimson, the platform zone's purple, the caldera's blizzard.
