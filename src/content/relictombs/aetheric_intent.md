## Lore

The asura's <span class="thing">King's Force</span> is an intent that presses on the mind until the weak submit; a strong enough foe resists it. Arthur's own version, his <span class="thing">Aetheric Intent</span>, made the air heavy round him: weaker soldiers sank to their knees, choked or froze where they stood, and no one cared to come near.

## The Key

Tap <kbd title="Aetheric Intent (the reborn)">Y</kbd> to let your intent out or draw it back in (Y at first: the integration key, which no reborn can use).

It costs 2 aether a second, and a quarter more for each foe it presses (at most 1.5 more). It slips away when your core runs dry, and on death or a change of world.

## Its Weight

Once a second it weighs on every foe in reach whose presence (the scale mana senses use: White 6, integrated 7, asura 8) is below your intent's weight:

**Layer I**: 6 blocks, weight 7.5<br>**Layer II**: 8 blocks, weight 8<br>**Layer III**: 10 blocks, weight 8.5<br>**Layer IV**: 12 blocks, weight 9

An integrated mage's presence weighs 7, so your intent outweighs it from the first layer.

## What It Does

By how far below your weight a foe stands:

**Below**: slowed<br>**One below**: slowed more, and weakened<br>**Two below**: slowed hard, weakened, and from layer II its spell, drawn bow or creeper's fuse is broken off every 2 s<br>**Three below**: from layer III frozen where it stands, 1 s (1.5 s at IV), then 8 s before it can be held again; a player 0.75 s, then 10 s

## Who Feels It

Only foes feel it: monsters, hostile beasts and anything after you or your party; players only where the server's PvP rules let you fight them, never your party or your own beasts.

Bosses and asura are only slowed a little; from layer III a boss lighter than your intent is also <span class="thing">pinned</span> a breath (0.5 s, 0.75 s at IV), then not again for 20 s (15 s). <span class="thing">Kezess Indrath</span> and <span class="thing">Agrona Vritra</span> shrug it off entirely, and the Aether Aura's heavy air with it, even with their thread cut.
