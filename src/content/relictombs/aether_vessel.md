## Lore

Arthur's broken core could not draw in mana at all. He lived on aether: he ate it off arrows and slain chimeras to heal, then built an aether core round the shards of the old one, reddish magenta with a purple sheen, that breathed in the aether around him. Without Sylvia's will he could use neither Static Void nor Realmheart.

## After

**No mana, ever**: the pool is empty. Spells, the mana arts, your class's gifts, Alacryan runes, Mana Flight and meditation's refinement all stop, and any beast will dies with the core. A broken core gives off no mana: mana senses find nothing, and there is nothing to suppress.

The broken core is your <span class="thing">aether vessel</span>: aether is your whole life now.

**Holds**: 400 aether, +80 a woken godrune, +200 with the Fate insight, +250 for each of the core's three further [layers](/relictombs/aether_core); 1,750 at most.

## Filling It

Aether crystals give 30, motes 6, aether fruit 40 (it hurts going in). A creature of the Relictombs gives up its aether when you slay it: 4 for each class from E.

**The egg** drinks 1 aether a second from your core while it holds more than 40%, and you can pour more in by hand; 6,000 wakes it, and your bond returns, older.
