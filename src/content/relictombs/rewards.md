## Caches

A cleared zone's <span class="thing">reliquary caches</span> wake: carved stone chests with a gold seam. Each member opens a cache once and takes their own share, straight into the satchel. Deeper caches give more:

**Mostly**: aether crystals and motes, djinn trinkets<br>**Often**: beast cores (better ones deeper), aether fruit<br>**Rarely**: a djinn relic: 2% of caches at depth 1, 16% at depth 5

## Trinkets

&#8203;<span class="item">Djinn trinkets</span> are worth gold and nothing else: at the Descension Chamber they are sold onto your Runecard.

**Djinn Coin**: about 2 G<br>**Djinn Figurine**: about 7 G<br>**Djinn Star Chart**: about 18 G, depth 3 and deeper

## Djinn Relics

&#8203;<span class="item">Djinn relics</span> are the prize: the relics of the armoury (Silverlight only from depth 4). A convergence gives each member one for sure, and the ruins and the maze's shrine give their own.

Three relics in ten come out as a <span class="item">Dead Relic</span>: grey, cold and inert. Aroa's Requiem can wake one into a relic of its tier.

## Declaring Relics

**Declaring**: the djinn's relics belong to the Sovereigns, and every ascender comes out past Chief Mason's inquirer, which senses each one you carry. Give it up for gold and favour, or keep it and pay the levy (see [Ways In and Out](/relictombs/ways)).

What you have not declared still sits in the satchel until you leave by an exit; die or abandon the ascent and it is gone.
