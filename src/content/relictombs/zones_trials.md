Trials test the head more than the hand.

**Platform Zone**: depth 3-5<br>**Bridge of Faces**: depth 2-5<br>**Hall of Mirrors**: depth 2-5<br>**Hall of Beams**: depth 1-5<br>**Rune-plate Court**: depth 1-5<br>**Fading Path**: depth 1-5

## The Platform Zone

**The Platform Zone**: rows of floating platforms climb a purple sky. Finish one platform on each row and a stair of light climbs to the next. You cannot fly here; a fall puts you back at the start.

**White**: safe; a little aether<br>**Red**: hold on for 20 s against a curse on mind or body<br>**Orange**: fights that grow<br>**Blue**: stop three spinning discs on their runes<br>**Black**: the Shadow Golem

## The Black Platform

The top row is black: wires seal its edge and the Shadow Golem rises from the centre. Beat it the first time and you are given the <span class="thing">Destruction keystone</span> (its trial gives the godrune). The portal at the top leads to a convergence.

## The Bridge of Faces

**The Bridge of Faces**: a long bridge over a chasm, its sides carved with faces and climbed by serpentine ghouls. Walk on and you find yourself back where you started. Among the faces stands a statue with each member's own face: when all of you have touched your own, the great face's mouth opens.

## The Hall of Mirrors

**The Hall of Mirrors**: a low hall of mirrors round a salt fountain, and it loops. Touch a mirror and you are trapped behind it while a phantom wearing your skin, with violet eyes, takes your place. Get out through the mirror inside your cell, or when your phantom dies; the mirror you came through cracks. Touch the fountain's statue and the true mirror shines for 10 s: touch it to go on. Some halls hold a trapped ascender's phantom.

## Beams and Plates

**The Hall of Beams**: djinn emitters throw a beam of light. Use a mirror to turn it 45 degrees (sneak to turn it back) until every receiver glows.

**The Rune-plate Court**: plates light in an order; step on them in the same order. Three rounds, each one longer; in the last, the studded plate must stay weighed down (sneak and use the statue to push it on, or have someone stand there). A wrong step stings and the round is shown again.

## The Fading Path

**The Fading Path**: runic tiles over the void. The safe ones light a few at a time ahead of you, and each crumbles two seconds after you step on it; a dark tile drops away under you. No flying. A fall throws you back to the start, hurt, and one stack in your satchel is lost.
