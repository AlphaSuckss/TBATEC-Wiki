## Raised Elsewhere

The Association registers people with a blood name, or people someone will answer for. Raised in Dicathen, you have neither. There are two ways round that, as there were for Arthur: a retired ascender's word, or a blood's.

## Alaric's Papers

&#8203;<span class="thing">Alaric Maer</span> drinks at the <span class="thing">Wogart's Rest</span> in Aramoor: a grizzled old ascender, bleary, barefoot more often than not. For a human he will have a friend forge papers that make you his nephew, who would not follow his father into trade, and vouch for you himself.

He asks sixty percent of your earnings. Say forty and he takes it.

## The Price

His cut comes off the gold from every ascent (relics given up and valuables sold at Maerin) until he has had 400 G or you have finished 25 ascents. You can buy him out for what is left at any time.

He gives advice for the assessment too, and while you carry his papers it comes back to you when you are called down. Elves and dwarves will not pass as his nephew.

## Vale's Word

&#8203;<span class="thing">Blood Vale</span>, Aramoor's military blood, will vouch for anyone it calls **Allied**, of any race: earn its favour through its contracts.

Either way, the war's rules still hold in Alacrya: casting Dicathen's spells where its guards can see is still exposure.
