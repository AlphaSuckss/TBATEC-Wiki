## Two Powers

Two powers share the name. <span class="thing">Realmheart</span> is the dragon will's Integrate phase (Sylvia's gift), lending mana sight, control and gravity. The <span class="thing">Realmheart Godrune</span> is a godrune: in the book Arthur, having learned it in the second keystone, found the djinn's version incomplete and named it after the Realmheart he had known before.

## Together

**Sight**: the larger reach of the two, with the godrune's colours<br>**Spells cancelled**: only the godrune unweaves them (and tears casts apart), paid in aether; the will's own unravel rests meanwhile<br>**Control**: the will keeps its lower costs, its stronger spells, its quicker cooldowns and its lent gravity<br>**Looks**: both are drawn: the will's runes and the godrune's marks under the eyes
