## Hazards

Some zones fight you with the place itself.

**Two-way Jungle**: depth 1-4<br>**Meadow and Chasm**: depth 1-4<br>**Frozen Caldera**: depth 3-5<br>**Red-vein Maze**: depth 3-5<br>**Lightless Vault**: depth 2-5<br>**Flooding Cistern**: depth 2-5

## The Two-way Jungle

**The Two-way Jungle**: a cavern with a jungle on its floor and another hanging from its ceiling. Under the canopy you fall slowly and jump high. Aether fruit hang from the vines (use one to pick it): sweet with aether, but it hurts going in. Fly-trap lures wait under the bait fruit and two-tailed monkeys climb about. The door is on a ledge up by the ceiling.

## The Meadow and Chasm

**The Meadow and Chasm**: blue grass and white flowers, and shrews that burrow up in hordes. The gate is across a chasm of lava, where broken rune-chains hang.

Kill thirty and the chains draw taut for 40 seconds, and up to 10 more while someone is still on them. God Step crosses on its own, and Aroa's Requiem on a chain's anchor mends it for good.

## The Frozen Caldera

**The Frozen Caldera**: a blizzard in a crater, and a dome round a broken arch. Away from a fire and outside the dome the cold hurts and slows you. The arch's four pieces are held by the <span class="thing">Shadow Claws</span> (their champion is Three Steps), the <span class="thing">Four Fists</span> (their chieftain), a ghost bear's den, and the frozen lake. Win all four and the arch stands again; read its mural, and Aroa's Requiem mends it with only two.

## The Red-vein Maze

**The Red-vein Maze**: tunnels lit by veins of red rock, held by a gang of rogue ascenders, with shadow claws that step out of the dark to hunt. A shrine in it opens only to asuran blood: hold Sylvia's will, offer a Vritra horn, or carry Sylvia's feather. Its statue gives a piece of djinn relic armour, one for each of your first four visits. The far portal leads to the second ruin for anyone who has not been.

## Dark and Water

**The Lightless Vault**: perfect dark. Placed light crumbles and night vision fails; only drifting motes glow. Shadow sentinels sink into the floor and rise behind you. Find the three lamp plates in their alcoves and stand on each.

**The Flooding Cistern**: a tall round well whose water rises a level every 15 seconds. Each of its three valves is locked by a rune plate near it: step on the plate, turn the valve. When all three are open the water drains; the way out is at the top.
