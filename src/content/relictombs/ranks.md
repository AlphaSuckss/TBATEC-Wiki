## Ranks

Green ascenders are <span class="thing">wogarts</span>, as the slang has it. Ascender XP raises you from there:

**Wogart**: the badge<br>**Ascender**: 300 XP<br>**Seasoned**: 1,500 XP<br>**Veteran**: 5,000 XP<br>**Principal**: 12,000 XP and 10 ascents at depth 3 or deeper

## Ascender XP

**A zone cleared**: 20 x its depth<br>**An ascent finished**: 50 x its depth<br>**A guardian slain**: 40<br>**A djinn ruin, first time**: 200<br>**The assessment**: its total grade

XP counts before your badge too; the rank comes with it. Your rank, ascents per depth and accolades are on the **Ascender** tab of the character screen.

## What Ranks Do

Ranks open the deeper depths (see [Ways In and Out](/relictombs/ways)), and a Veteran's satchel holds 36 stacks.

A <span class="thing">Principal</span> is sent a <span class="item">Principal's Badge</span>: use it on a candidate to offer to lead them, then use it to set out with those who accepted. Each of them who comes out with a badge pays you a 20 G stipend.

## Accolades

Ascenders wear <span class="thing">accolades</span> for the beasts they have killed. The first time you share in the kill of a guardian you are given its accolade. Worn in a charm slot:

**Shadow Golem**: +5% damage to constructs<br>**Titan**: +1 armour toughness<br>**Millipede**: +10% aether regeneration<br>**Serpent**: poison hurts 5% less
