## Lore

In the fourth keystone Arthur saw Fate as threads of gold binding moments and lives. Once it was solved he could sway them, and only for moments: he searched the futures open to him and chose one, and he cut the threads that bound Cecilia to Agrona and the Legacy, which freed her and left Agrona senseless. He could not command Fate after. Cutting a lord's fate is how Agrona and Kezess at last fell.

## The Archon

After the fourth keystone the asura took up Lord Eccleiah's proposal and named Arthur <span class="thing">archon</span>, the voice of the lesser peoples among them.

Here: the first time you stand in Epheotus holding the Fate insight, you are named Archon, a title you may show (<span class="thing">/titles</span>); then the young asura of Featherwalk Aerie challenge you to their great hunt.

## A Thread Cut

**Sneak and press** <kbd title="Aetheric Intent (the reborn)">Y</kbd> looking at a creature in sight within 24-40 blocks: 300-200 aether (all of it or none), then Fate rests 5-3 minutes. Its strength is the mean Attunement of your woken godrunes.

**A boss**: its meld, raised aegis or phase's pause gives way; its minions come apart; its helpful effects go<br>**A summon**: a boss's minion or a conjured golem comes apart

**A player**: where PvP allows: helpful effects, casting and godrune arts let go of them; slowed 4 s<br>**Anything else**: its helpful effects go; slowed and weakened 8 s

Your friends, your pets and those you may not fight are never touched.

## The Lords

&#8203;<span class="thing">Kezess Indrath</span> and <span class="thing">Agrona Vritra</span>: once a fight, and only once they fight in earnest (their second phase).

**Kezess**: his folded space no longer turns blows aside and Aevum Mending no longer turns his wounds back, for the rest of the fight; whoever he holds in stopped time is let go; he staggers 4 s.

**Agrona**: his blood lets go of everyone and reaches for no one again; his spliced beasts come apart and he calls no more; his scales no longer turn blows aside; he staggers 5 s.

Neither loses any health to it, and Agrona's marks still hold: the fights stay long.

## A Future Chosen

On its own: a blow that would kill you is undone (not the void, not a command). You stand at a quarter of your health and nothing touches you for a breath and a half. It takes 100 aether (without it, no choice) and rests 10-5 minutes.
