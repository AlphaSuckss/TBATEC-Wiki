## The Key

What Arthur did with aether before his godrunes, on the arts key (<kbd title="Body Augmentation (tap) / Iron Skin (hold); with sneak: set their strength; the aether arts once reborn">B</kbd>) in place of the mana arts:

**Tap**: Aether Clad<br>**Hold, let go**: Aetheric Strike<br>**Sneak + tap**: Aether Burst Step<br>**Sneak + hold**: Aether Barrier<br>**Tap twice**: Aether Aura<br>**Strike just after a Burst Step**: Aether Burst Strike

## Aether Clad

**Aether Clad**: he poured aether through his whole body, a purplish-red sheen, to strike harder and reach what could not be touched.

Up: +14 attack, +35% speed, +16 armour, +8 toughness, +50% attack speed, higher jumps and softer falls, blows a quarter harder; 1.5 aether a second.

## Aetheric Strike

**Aetheric Strike**: he gathered aether in his fist, a black, smoky glove (with Regis's aether: Gauntlet Form) that slowed him as if through tar, and loosed it in a blast that warped the space it hit, about half his core a strike.

Hold to gather (full in 1.5 s, 1.1 s from the core's second layer; you walk slowly), let go: 40 to 320 aetheric damage to what you hit and 60% of it in a cone 6 blocks beyond; 20 to 80 aether.

## Burst Step

**Aether Burst Step**: Burst Step was his own flash step, built on the Thyestes clan's Mirage Walk: power fired through the legs' muscles for one near-instant step, which tore at his human body. The asuran body bears it on aether, and God Step grew out of it.

Here: 6-8 blocks along your look in an instant, untouchable while it lasts; 12 aether, every 1.5 s.

## Burst Strike

**Aether Burst Strike**: Burst Step's trick in the arm, aether fired through its muscles for a near-instant punch that even Lances and Retainers can hardly see. Arthur worked it out in the Relictombs and tried it on Chul; it still strained him.

Strike within half a second after an Aether Burst Step: 90 aetheric harm on top of the blow (112 in a fourth-layer core), 18 aether, every 2 s.

## Aether Barrier

**Aether Barrier**: aether hardened over the skin while held, the way he shielded himself (once from an Alacryan army's barrage): it soaks 70% of each blow (90% of shots) for half an aether a point, 2 aether a second, no knockback, a slower walk.

## Aether Aura

**Aether Aura**: he let aether out until the air itself grew heavy, pinning a pack of beasts and the millipede in place.

Within 8 blocks: lesser foes cannot move for 3 s and hit weakly; class S and up, bosses and players are only slowed, for half as long. 35 aether, every 20 s.

## Conduits

**Conduits**: at first, aether sent to one hand spread through his whole body; tempering himself in a lava river forged the passages until most of it arrived. Each art's strength runs from 70% (no conduits) to 100% (all open). You start at 40; every strike and step opens them a little, and <span class="thing">standing in lava with Aether Clad up</span> opens them half a point a second (4 aether a second; the lava burns at 15%).
