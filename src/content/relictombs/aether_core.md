## Lore

In the books Arthur's aether core was the only one there ever was, and it grew four layers. The first was the core he forged round the shards of his broken mana core. The second came on the Relictombs' second level, when he fed an obelisk's aether into his core, slowly and painfully, layering it as Sylvie's egg was layered: the core grew clearer and deep violet, and aether ran through his widened channels to wherever he sent it almost at once.

The third came in the aetheric realm while he tended Sylvie's egg: he drank in more aether than his cracked core could hold, then sealed the cracks with it in a new, hardened shell. The fourth came at the very end, in the Relictombs' aetheric river, long after his insight into Fate: the flood cracked his core, and the lightning bursting from him turned back and sealed it in moments. Each layer purged impurities and deepened his reserves and control.

## What a Layer Gives

Your broken core is the first layer. Each further one:

**Layer II**: +250 aether held, breathes 15% faster, godrunes to Attunement III, the arts at 108%, and a strike gathers in 1.1 s (aether answers sooner)<br>**Layer III**: +500 in all, 30% faster, godrunes to IV, arts 116%, a second Burst Step may follow the first at once, and your intent can freeze<br>**Layer IV**: +750 in all, 50% faster, godrunes to V (with Fate), arts 124%, and the Aether Aura pins even class S beasts (never bosses or players)

## Growing One

Three things, then a rite:

**Aether**: draw in 3,000 in all for layer II, 9,000 for III, 18,000 for IV: every point your core breathes in while it is not full counts, so spend it<br>**Insight**: 3, 4, then 5 godrunes woken, each won in its keystone's trial; the Fate insight counts as one, and layer IV needs Fate whatever the count<br>**The rite**: at a sanctuary pool in the Relictombs

## The Rite

When the core is ready you are told. **Meditate at a sanctuary pool** in the Relictombs with your core full: for 30 seconds the new layer drinks it, aether streaming into your chest. Rise, move or be struck out of the meditation, or leave, and the rite breaks off: the aether is lost and the core rests two minutes. When it forms, a quarter of the larger core is left in it.

## Godrunes and the Core

A godrune's Attunement rises no higher than one past the core's layer: II in the first, V only in the fourth (and only with the Fate insight). Uses past that still count, and the level comes the moment the next layer forms.

The character screen shows your layer, the bar toward the next and what it still wants; the HUD shows four small gems under your arts.

## Older Cores

A core reborn before 1.28.0 keeps every layer it had (a fourth without Fate too), and settles deep enough for every godrune it had already attuned.
