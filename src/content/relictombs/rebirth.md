## Lore

In the books, at the fall of Dicathen, Arthur pushed his mana, the aether in him and Sylvia's will past every limit, and his body began to tear itself apart. His bond Sylvie spent herself on an aether art to save him: she gave him part of her own asuran body and was left as an iridescent stone he carried. He woke in a djinn ruin, whole, with his mana core broken beyond repair.

## The Way In

**Who**: a <span class="thing">White core</span> with a living asura bond (a child asura befriended as an equal).

**When**: your own mana kills you: the damage of a backlash or of an overfill (pushing the dragon will too far can do it).

Instead of dying, you feel your bond reach for you, and must choose.

## The Choice

**Accept** (it cannot be undone): your core breaks in half; your bond pours itself into you and sleeps as an egg you carry (kept on death, never dropped); you wake whole where you fell, in its asuran body.

**Let go**: an ordinary death.

You have a minute. Nothing can harm or move you while you choose; leave the game and you are asked again when you return.

## The Only Way

This is the only way onto the aether path. A vessel woven round a White core in an earlier 1.27.0 build has gone quiet with its godrunes; it wakes if that player is reborn.

## One a World

Only one player in a world can be reborn (a server setting, on by default), and the first holds the world's aether path. After that the bond's reach slips for anyone else whose mana kills them: they die as anyone would, told whose path it is, and keystones stay dark for them. Those reborn before the rule keep their path.
