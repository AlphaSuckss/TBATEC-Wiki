## Destruction

Vivum, its destruction branch. Arthur won it on a black platform against a shadow golem: runes ran from the backs of his hands up his arms, down his back and legs and settled at the base of his spine, and a cold, calm violet flame consumed the golem.

**Tap** (30 aether): the flame wreathes your fists and weapon for 12 s; each blow adds 120-280 aetheric damage and <span class="thing">unmakes</span>: no healing for 8 s, and wards, barriers and summons come apart.

**Hold** (8-5 aether a second): a cone of flame 12-20 blocks long, 200-450 aetheric damage a second; it burns spell missiles, burns constructs half again as hard, and outside the Relictombs (when spells may change terrain) 96-256 blocks a second; inside, only the tomb wards.

**The Pull**: each use pushes toward madness (+3 a tap, +1 a second held, -1 every 8 s; the Regis gauntlet halves it). At 30 the power high (+25% damage, allies no longer spared); at 60 frenzy (the flame leaps to the nearest living thing); at 90 it tears away and leaves you hollow for 20 s.

## The Living Flame

The flame destroys anything it touches, and what it sets alight burns on, block to touching block, until it has eaten its <span class="thing">measure</span>: 384-1,536 blocks past what a cone burns, 12,000-48,000 for an Unleash. Stone, obsidian, water, lava, warded ground, chests (they spill): all of it.

Nothing else puts it out, not water, not another mage, not your death. **Sneak and press** Destruction to call it back. A creature in it burns, 50-112 aetheric every half second.

What it will not eat: air, the world's own bones (bedrock, barriers, the End's portals) and the gates and portals of Dicathen, Alacrya and the Relictombs, so no one is shut out of the world. A land claim from another mod still holds, and Aroa's Requiem can turn its work back. Inside the Relictombs it eats only the tomb wards. <span class="thing">Invented</span>: the measure, the creep and the call back.

It burns dark violet, amethyst at its heart, and cold: what it eats does not char or smoke, it comes apart in amethyst flecks.

## Unleash

Arthur once let Destruction loose on a war camp in Vechor and razed it with everyone in it, then lay senseless for a day and a half.

**Sneak and hold** the key two seconds, then let go (150 aether): the flame bursts from you 18-32 blocks round, 1600-4000 aetheric damage at its heart and half at its rim, unmaking all it touches and burning the ground away, then creeping on to its measure. Then you are spent for a minute (no godrunes, slow and weak; you can still call the flame back), +40 Pull, and it waits five minutes.

## God Step

Spatium: a refined Burst Step, the body made an aetheric vibration that slips through space, near instant, its rune like lightning.

**Hold**: violet paths to places in sight within 32-64 blocks, to your three marks within 128-512 (sneak and press to set one), and behind the creature under your crosshair. **Release** on one: you step there in purple lightning (10-5 aether), unharmed for that half second. Three to five steps chain within 2 s, then it rests 2-0.5 s.

**The step strike**: step behind a foe and you land facing its back; a blow within a second adds 160-480 aetheric damage (the dagger in the remnant's back). A step in the air holds you there a moment, so chains can cross the sky.

It never ends inside a block, over the void, past the world's edge or through bedrock; a step in sight or behind a foe needs an open line from your eyes, and a mark will not carry you through the walls of a guarded place you are not in. An unsafe spot gives way to the nearest safe one, or you are told why not.

## Aroa's Requiem

Aevum: it turns non-living things back to what they were, with a gold glow and purple motes. It cannot touch the living.

**Hold** within 8 blocks: a <span class="thing">Dead Relic</span> in your hand wakes into a relic (3 s, 60); your bond's <span class="thing">dormant egg</span> in your hand drinks the turning-back and wakes sooner (6 s, 80, 10-25% of what it still needs, every 5 minutes); a conjured golem or a barrier dome is turned back to before it was made (20).

A Relictombs mechanism mends (40-10); blocks changed in the last 5-30 minutes within 8-24 return (20, then 2 a second); every worn and held piece is mended at once (30). While it is held, hostile missiles that come within 4-8 are turned back along their own path, and are yours (3 each).

## Realmheart Godrune

Vivum: mana and the aether shaping it seen at once, violet marks glowing under the eyes.

**Toggle** (1-0.3 aether a second): living things show through walls within 48-128 blocks, the unseen too; the Relictombs' hidden mechanisms are traced in light; the world dims. Hostile spell missiles within 8-16 come apart (2-0 each); a tap unweaves the aimed field, ward, barrier or summon (15, every 4-1 s).

**Spells torn as they are cast**: a hostile mage within 16-32 blocks loses the spell they gather or channel, and a Scythe's, Lance's or Retainer's art comes apart as it is loosed (10 aether, every 2 s).

## King's Gambit

A gold crown of light above the head; thoughts that run side by side; the smallest movements read; coldly logical while it lasts.

**Toggle** (4-2 aether a second): every other godrune costs 85-70% and rests 60-40% as long; foes about to strike show red 1-2 s early, and their blows land at 70-40%; nausea, blindness and darkness do not hold; flight costs nothing (its insight gave Arthur back his flight); the people you talk to show whether they speak true.

It is hard to put down: its <span class="thing">Hunger</span> grows every 30 s it is up, and letting go leaves you slow for a while; past 6 it costs half again and holds on 10 s.

## Spatium

The last godrune Arthur won, of space, in Epheotus's great hunt rather than from a keystone (see The Great Hunt). With it he sensed the space round him, folded it round the edges of a tear, and pressed it into a jet-black blade and a wall.

**Tap** (20-12 aether): a blade of folded space cuts along your look to the first wall, 16-32 blocks: 420-800 aetheric harm to everything you may harm on the line.

**Hold** (6-4 aether a second): space folds round the point you aim at (16-32 away): hostile creatures within 5-8 are dragged in and bitten, 60-140 a second; let go and it snaps back, 120-300 more and a fling outward. Bosses barely move; the asura lords not at all.

**Sneak and tap** (40-25): space hardens into a pane 7-11 wide and 4-6 high, three blocks ahead, for 10-15 s: hostile missiles stop at it and hostile creatures cannot cross; you and your friends pass.

**Sneak and hold** (on, then off; 1.5-0.5 aether a second): the space within 16-32 blocks opens to you alone: motes in the hollows hidden behind stone (rooms, caves) and a gleam on every creature out of sight.

None of it changes a block. <span class="thing">Invented</span>: the four arts as the key works them, and every number.

## Attunement

Each godrune grows from I to V with use against a worthy foe within 16 blocks (a class B beast or more, a strong monster, a player you may fight): 20, 60, 150 and 300 uses. It stops at IV until the Fate insight.

Godrunes sit on your spell bar in gold, are paid in aether, never take a rune slot and never open in a Workshop. Their glyphs are gold on your back and shine through clothes while they work.

## Their Strength

They are the strongest power there is. At V an Unleash deals six times the heaviest asuran art's hit and more than a Scythe's whole life; the cone burns more each second than a White core's strongest spell strikes; a wreathed blow adds over four times the best Armoury weapon's. God Step outreaches the Third Phase's fold many times over. Godrune damage is aetheric (it gets past armour) and lands in full on players too (a server can lower it: config godrunePvp).

Beside your own aether arts: even at I an Unleash is five full Aetheric Strikes, the cone burns half again what the strike can deal a second, and a wreathed sword outpaces it; at V a step strike is half again a full strike with no gathering, and God Step reaches four Burst Steps by sight and sixteen by its marks.
