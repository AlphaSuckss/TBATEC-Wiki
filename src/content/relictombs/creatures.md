## Creatures

The Relictombs keep their own creatures, and they live nowhere else: the zones call them up. Most are djinn-made constructs that come apart into violet motes when they fall; none of them bears a beast will.

**Guardians**: the [Shadow Golem](/beasts/class_s#shadow_golem) (black platform), the [Aether Titan](/beasts/class_ss#aether_titan) (titan's field), the [Aether Millipede](/beasts/class_s#aether_millipede) (crystal den) and the [Three-headed Serpent](/beasts/class_s#three_headed_serpent). Each leaves an accolade the first time.

**The rest**: [chimeras](/beasts/class_c#chimera) (blades, bone bow, bone gun), [carallians](/beasts/class_c#carallian) and their four-armed brutes, [deadly shrews](/beasts/class_d#deadly_shrew), [serpentine ghouls](/beasts/class_b#serpentine_ghoul), [mirror phantoms](/beasts/class_a#mirror_phantom), [fly-trap lures](/beasts/class_c#flytrap_lure), [two-tailed monkeys](/beasts/class_e#two_tailed_monkey), [invisible centaurs](/beasts/class_a#invisible_centaur) and [shadow sentinels](/beasts/class_b#shadow_sentinel); the caldera's Shadow Claws, Four Fists, ghost bears and spear beaks.
