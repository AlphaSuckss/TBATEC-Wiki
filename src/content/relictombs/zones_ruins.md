## The Ruins

Three places in the tombs are still the djinn's own. Each is met once, and none is drawn at random.

**The First Ruin**: after the Titan's Field<br>**The Second Ruin**: after the Red-vein Maze<br>**The Sealed Ruin**: depth 5, with a Djinn Key

## The First Ruin

**The First Ruin**: a djinn's laboratory, where a violet crystal holds what is left of a djinn's mind. It tells of the refuge and of the Vritra who loot it, then sets a trial: five anchors stand round a pit, and you have 20 seconds to step between them all. It lends the step: sneak and look at an anchor. Pass and you are given <span class="thing">God Step's keystone</span> (its trial gives the godrune), with the Aevum keystone.

## The Second Ruin

**The Second Ruin**: a vast library that begins to fall in a minute after you arrive. Somewhere in it a statue holds the Realmheart keystone and a memory crystal; take them and get out before the roof comes down.

## The Sealed Ruin

**The Sealed Ruin**: a door that only a <span class="item">Djinn Key</span> opens. Its three shards glint in the pools of the deepest sanctuaries (depth 4 and 5); craft them together. Behind the door lies the Fate keystone.

Each ruin's first visit is worth 200 ascender XP.
