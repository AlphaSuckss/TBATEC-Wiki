## Depth

An <span class="thing">ascent</span> is a chain of zones drawn for your party when you step through the arch, at the depth you choose. Deeper is harder and richer:

**Depth 1**: 3 zones, a sanctuary after the 2nd<br>**Depth 2**: 3-4 zones, a sanctuary after the 2nd<br>**Depth 3**: 4-5 zones, sanctuaries after the 2nd and 4th<br>**Depth 4**: 5 zones, the same<br>**Depth 5**: 5-6 zones, the same

## The Draw

No two ascents are alike. Some zones lead on to others: the platform zone's top portal opens on a convergence, and the titan's field leads to the djinn's first ruin for anyone who has not yet been. An ascent meets one guardian at most (two at depth 4 and 5).

The zones <span class="thing">scale to the party</span>: every extra member gives the creatures 35% more health and the waves a fifth more creatures.

## Simulets

**Your party**: only a <span class="thing">simulet</span> keeps a party together; without one, everyone who steps in goes alone. Sneak and use your simulet on someone who carries one to offer a link; they accept by doing the same back within a minute. A set holds up to four. Sneak and use it in the air to leave the set.

At the arch, every member who steps in within 10 seconds of the first goes on the same ascent.

## Crossing

A zone's way on opens when its task is done: the doors, films and statue mouths of its exits come alive. Walk into one to cross.

Members cross one by one. When the first has gone, the others' simulets pull them after within 60 seconds, ready or not. A gold-white light takes you while the next zone forms; you can't be hurt in it.

## Sanctuaries

&#8203;<span class="thing">Sanctuaries</span> are round rest halls with white pillars and a pool, often stained with old blood. Nothing spawns there, nothing can be built there, and the pool gives back aether quickly.

Each has two doors: one on into the ascent, and a <span class="thing">descension door</span> that takes you out with what you carry. Each member chooses for themselves.

## The Satchel

Everything you pick up inside goes into your <span class="item">Ascent Satchel</span>, not your pack: 27 slots (36 for a Veteran), opened with <kbd title="Ascent Satchel">;</kbd> or the button on your inventory. It stays <span class="thing">locked</span> until you leave through an exit; only aether crystals, motes, aether fruit and food can be used from it (a gold corner marks them). Items you threw yourself you pick up as usual.

## Leaving

Leaving through an exit or a descension door brings you to the Descension Chamber with your satchel, where it is checked and emptied into your pack; what does not fit stays in the satchel, unlocked, until you take it.

**Abandon**: hold the button in the satchel for 5 seconds. You lose the satchel and are put out.

## Death and Logging Out

**Dying inside**: you wake at your usual spawn with your inventory and everything you wear, but the satchel and your experience are lost. When nobody of the party is left, the ascent collapses.

**Logging out**: the party goes on; log back in to rejoin it. If nobody is online the ascent waits 30 minutes, or a whole day when everyone logged out in a sanctuary; then it collapses, and anything in its satchels with it.
