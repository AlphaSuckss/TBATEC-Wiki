## Lore

After the fourth keystone the young asura challenged Arthur to their clan hunt, a climb without flight. Their quarry, the questing beast, brayed like a pack of ghostly hounds; slain, it gave birth to monstrosity after monstrosity, each a stronger blend of dragon, phoenix, basilisk and leviathan, the last wreathed in Destruction, which only ate their remains.

Arthur held it in a pocket of space and opened a passage into the aetheric realm along God Step's paths: the stillborn were drawn out of it, it died, and a godrune of space formed on his back.

## Who and Where

One of the reborn who holds the Fate insight and God Step, and not yet Spatium.

The questing beast lives in <span class="thing">Featherwalk Aerie</span>, the phoenixes' woods in Epheotus (<span class="thing">/locate biome tbatec:featherwalk\_aerie</span>). Wound one and the hunt begins; a bar shows the quarry to all near.

## Four Incarnations

Cornered, or killed outright, it births its monstrosity. Each monstrosity that falls births a stronger one: incarnations I to IV, with 1,400, 2,100, 3,150 and 4,200 health and blows up to 45% harder; the third and fourth burn with Destruction's flame. Friends may fight beside you; the godrune is yours alone.

## The Passage

The fourth will not die: its stillborn kin hold it between the worlds for 10 seconds. **God Step** to land within 6 blocks of it in that while and the passage opens: the stillborn are drawn out, it dies, and <span class="thing">Spatium</span> is yours. Miss the while and it comes back with a quarter of its health.

Go 128 blocks off, into another world, or away for a minute, and the hunt is lost; a hunt does not outlast a server's restart.
