## Lore

Arthur's new body sat between human and asura. His hair faded from sand to a grey wheat and grew past his shoulders, his eyes turned gold, his face sharpened and every scar was gone. It was weak at first; tempered, it punched through stone and regrew a lost leg and arm on aether.

## Gifts

Your body is your bond's race: its gifts at nine tenths of a grown asura's (health, strength, armour, footing, reach), plus 16 health, 4 attack, 4 armour and toughness, steadier footing and a little more speed. Your size does not change.

It <span class="thing">mends on aether</span>: 1 health a second (3 with Aether Clad up), two points of aether each, while your core is more than a fifth full.
