## The Dormant Gate

Deep under the Djinn Sanctuary, past the warded hall and the rune doors, a small carved frame holds a <span class="thing">dormant gate</span>: a dark, oily film that leads nowhere.

The <span class="item">Compass</span>'s halves open portals into and out of the Relictombs. Use it within 6 blocks of the gate and the film wakes for 30 seconds. The Compass rests for ten minutes afterwards. Only this gate wakes.

## The Fallen Ascent

The first time you walk through, the Relictombs take you the way they took Arthur: the <span class="thing">Fallen Ascent</span>. No badge is asked; a simulet set may come.

**Depth 2**: a sanctuary, the Statue Hall, the two-way jungle, the millipede's den, a sanctuary, the meadow and chasm<br>**Depth 3**: the platform zone, a convergence, a sanctuary, the titan's field, the first ruin

## Out at Maerin

It ends at Maerin, where Chief Mason wants a licence you do not have. Arthur showed his runes and the Highblood Denoir dagger he took from the millipede's lair, and was let through with bows.

Later walks through the woken gate are <span class="thing">unlicensed ascents</span> at the deepest depth your core and your record allow, out at Maerin as always. For those raised in Dicathen, it is a way to Alacrya.
