## The Ascension Chamber

The way in is the <span class="thing">Ascension Chamber</span> beside the Association in Aramoor: a hall of black stone, its great arch filled with golden-white light, a rail across the dais and a guard in the gap. Show your own badge and walk in. Candidates go up with their principal.

It sets you down on the <span class="thing">First Level</span>.

## The Levels and the Arch

The First Level's guarded glass portal wants your badge again and takes you up to the <span class="thing">Second Level</span>. At the far end of its avenue stands the <span class="thing">arch</span>: walk into it to choose a depth.

Ascenders meditate before the arch. Meditate within 8 blocks of it for 20 seconds and you go up <span class="thing">Centred</span>: +15% mana and +50% aether regeneration until the first zone is done.

## Depths

A depth opens when you finish an ascent of four or more steps at the depth above it. With the depth gates on (the default) you also need:

## The Descension Chamber

Every way out ends on the pad of the <span class="thing">Descension Chamber</span> in Maerin, a hall you cannot use to go in. <span class="thing">Chief Mason</span>, the town's chief, sees everyone who comes down:

## The Inquirer

His <span class="thing">obsidian inquirer</span>, like a pocket watch, senses every djinn relic on you: in the satchel, the pack, worn, or in a box you carry. For each: **give it up** to the Sovereigns (40-150 G and Maerin's patron blood's favour; on the Alacryan path, an audience with the Scythe: a deepening) or **keep it** for a levy of 30-120 G. Unpaid, it waits at the Association.

## What Comes Out

One thing slips past it: whatever you keep inside a <span class="item">Dimension Ring</span>.

The djinn trinkets in your satchel are sold onto your Runecard. Then the satchel empties into your pack; what will not fit is held for you by any Association clerk. The clerks also sell <span class="item">simulets</span> (8 G), reissue a lost badge (20 G) and send a <span class="item">Dead Relic</span> to the appraisers, who wake it into a relic of its tier for 80 G.

## The Team Board

The <span class="thing">team boards</span> in the Association and on the Second Level are where ascenders find a party. Post the role you want and a depth, with a short note, or ask to join someone's: they hear who asked, and take you into their set with a simulet.

## The Public Floors

The two floors are places to get ready in. **Second Level**: west of the avenue the <span class="thing">Association</span> (clerks, the appraiser, a board) and the <span class="thing">High Hall of Justice</span>; east the inn, the clock, the <span class="thing">market court</span> and the <span class="thing">Gardens</span>; at the avenue's end the arch, where those going up file on the east side past the badge desk.

**First Level**: the quarrymen's <span class="thing">canteen</span> north-west of the gate and the <span class="thing">hiring yard</span> north-east of it.
