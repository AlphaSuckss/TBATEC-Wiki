## Fights

Every zone has a task, and its way on stays shut until the task is done. Each also holds one to four <span class="thing">reliquary caches</span> (see [Rewards](/relictombs/rewards)).

**Statue Hall**: depth 1-3<br>**Convergence**: depth 2-5<br>**Millipede's Den**: depth 2-5, guardian<br>**Titan's Field**: depth 3-5, guardian

## The Statue Hall

**The Statue Hall**: a long marble hall lined with kneeling, armed statues. They crack open in waves (two, three from depth 3) as chimeras: blades, a bone bow loosing vertebrae, a bone gun firing aether. When the last falls, the tall metal door at the far end opens.

## Convergence

**Convergence**: a trampled crimson plain under a blood-red sky, the red moon held on a far pillar inside a ward. The sky reddens and carallians claw up out of the earth, wave after wave; from the third, four-armed brutes come with them. Each lull lasts about a minute.

After two waves plus the depth, the ward fails and the stair round the pillar opens. Breaking the moon is the only way out: every member gets a djinn relic for sure. Other parties at the same depth may share the plain.

## Guardians

**The Millipede's Den**: a glowing tunnel that winds down into a domed cavern of aether-crystal hills, and the giant millipede that lives in it.

**The Titan's Field**: an open plain of grey rock under the six-legged titan. Its door leads on to the djinn's first ruin for anyone who has not been there.

A guardian's first kill gives you its accolade.
