## A Tainted Will

Tainted by the Vritra like the Guardian's: four hours of meditation, its Strain fills 15% faster, and in its final struggle every third flare-up is a seizure of black smoke and red sparks.

**Acquire: Warlord's Command**<br>**Radius**: 16 / 20 / 24 / 28 / 32 blocks<br>**Your companions**: Strength and Speed I, I, I, II, II

## Warlord's Command

Every 6 s, the wild orcs and gnolls in the radius that you have not struck in the last 10 s stand down from you and turn on your foe (the last you struck, or the nearest after you) for 10 s; while the command holds they will not take you as a target.

## Mutant Form
