Only <span class="thing">S-class</span> beasts carry wills, and the <span class="thing">Shadow Panther</span>, the one below S (Virion's). Weak cores hold mana only. Not every beast of a kind carries one: from one in four to one in two of the S-class beasts, one in seventeen panthers, every Elder panther, and always the Elderwood Guardian.

A bearer shows it: a faint mote of its will's colour rises from its chest now and then, for anyone within 16 blocks.

## The Drop

Slay a bearer yourself (or with your companion, or a missile of yours) and its <span class="thing">will</span> drops in place of its core: the will lives in the core. Killed any other way, the will <span class="thing">disperses</span> in a burst of its colour and the ordinary core drops instead. A tamed bearer loses its will.

If you already hold a will, yours stirs when a bearer is within 24 blocks: a heartbeat, and a line over your hotbar.

## Taking It In

Hold use for two seconds with the will pressed to your chest to take it into your core. It binds <span class="thing">dormant</span>.

You can hold <span class="thing">one will</span> at a time. A wraith has no core to hold one, and an asura's blood will not take a lesser beast's will.

Sylvia's will is never dropped: she gives it, once, to someone she chooses.

## Release

&#8203;<span class="thing">Release</span> is in the mana core screen: confirm, then hold the button for three seconds. The will is destroyed with all its assimilation, Mastery and Insight, and it lashes out as it goes (a flare-up at its worst). Then your core is free for another.
