The novel names the phases: <span class="thing">Acquire</span> borrows a part of the beast's inherent ability; <span class="thing">Integrate</span> merges the body with the will, for more control of its abilities, and is not always the stronger. Every will in TBATEC has both; only a dragon's goes to a third.

&#8203;<kbd title="Beast Will (tap: Acquire, hold: Integrate)">N</kbd>: tap for Acquire, hold for Integrate (from Acquire the Strain carries over), sneak and hold for a third phase. Tap again to end it.

## Holding One

A phase needs the will awake and the phase open, no exhaustion or sealed mana, no meditation, its cooldown over and a second of upkeep in the pool. Phases are exclusive; a deeper one keeps the shallower one's signature.

It ends cleanly when you tap, when your mana can no longer pay (before it would backlash), on death, in another dimension, when you meditate or sleep, or when your mana is sealed. Each phase you entered then rests.

## Costs

Upkeep is paid from your pool each second (recovery keeps going, as for a held art).
