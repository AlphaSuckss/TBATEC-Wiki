Sylvia Indrath's will, a dragon's. In the novel she gave it to the infant Arthur as she lay dying: golden smoke with purple sparks poured from his core, his shirt bloodied with no wound beneath, her runes went out and her eyes dimmed to yellow.

Moments later time stopped for everyone but the two of them. Virion judged the will S or SS class. Given willingly, it made Arthur a <span class="thing">legacy tamer</span>; it never drops from anything, and asuras and wraiths cannot take it.

## Her Gift

She gives it once, to someone she chooses. You must have learned <span class="thing">Mana Rotation</span> from her, carry no beast's will, and have slain a work of the Vritra: a <span class="thing">Lion Mutant</span> or the <span class="thing">Elderwood Guardian</span>. Missing something, she only hints at it.

Then she calls you over and offers; speak to her again within a minute to accept. Hold still: golden smoke and violet sparks pour from her hand into your core, blood on your shirt and no wound, and time stops round you both. Her eyes stay dull for a day.

## Dormant

It binds dormant: six hours of meditation, half again as fast near her, and Insight comes half again as easily to a legacy tamer. Release it and it is gone for good: she cannot give it twice.

## Stopped Time

Time stops in a sphere that moves with you. Creatures, missiles, falling blocks, items, orbs, other casters' spells and every mote hang where they are, and nothing can hurt, push or take them. They go on exactly as they were when the sphere leaves them or lifts.

You move freely, but you cannot strike, cast, use or pick up anything, and the frozen mana gives you none back. Players freeze too if the server allows it (on by default); dragons, and anyone in their own Static Void, do not. The world goes grey and turns toward its negative.

## Mastery

Its Mastery comes from use: 15 points for each Static Void that froze a creature hunting you, and 5 more for each other one, up to 40 more.

## Control

An Indrath power whose gift is control, not raw strength. You see the mana of every living thing through walls, the invisible too; from Mastery III a hostile spell missile coming in comes apart (every 4 / 3 / 2 s, +4% Strain). +10% speed and Night Vision.

## Gravity

&#8203;<span class="thing">Gravity</span> answers you, and only here: once Realmheart is open to you, you can shape gravity spells in the workshop, and they go off only while it is up.

## The Look

Gold runes on your arms, back and under your eyes (one of four patterns, your own), long white hair, purple eyes, a swirl of the four elements and aether; the world grey but for its mana.

## Folding Space

The novel never names it: you hand your body to the will. It needs Integrate Mastery III and 100 Third Insight, costs 300 to 180 mana a second, and holds Realmheart. Swing at a foe out of reach and space folds: you stand beside it and the blow lands as 1 W of aether (+5% Strain).

Sneak and jump to step along your gaze through up to three blocks (rests 2 s). The aether share of each blow ignores armour, Protection and shields, and what it burns cannot heal for 3 s. Fear, weakness, slowness, nausea, blindness and darkness slide off you.

## It Can Kill You

It burns the body that carries it: health drains away (never the last point) and natural mending stops. For its first seconds the will does not let you end it.

&#8203;<span class="thing">The Third Phase can kill you.</span> If Strain reaches 100, the overload takes 45% of your health, and that can be your death.

Then come blindness, nausea, two minutes of mana backlash and a minute with the will locked away. From 75% Strain your heart pounds, sparks crack off you and the edge of your sight darkens red: let go in time.

Gold tinged with violet aether glows through you, and your sight turns lavender.
