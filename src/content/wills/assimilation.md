## The Final Struggle

From 90% comes the <span class="thing">final struggle</span>: the will fights you for your body. Flare-ups every 3-5 minutes, Weakness and Slowness II for 8 s, recovery x0.4 for 40 s, and your held spells collapse. A tainted will seizes you on every third; a dragon's freezes you for a moment on each.

## Training

&#8203;<span class="thing">Stabilization</span>: meditate (<kbd title="Meditate (sit / rise)">M</kbd>) with a dormant will and it takes the meditation: your core does not refine or grow meanwhile (in the novel a core does not develop during assimilation), but you keep the triple recovery. Spells you cast still refine it.

&#8203;<span class="thing">Strain training</span>: each second you sprint, fight or are hurt with mana circulating (at least a fifth of your pool, and 10) costs 2 mana and assimilates 2.5 times as fast as meditation, with a small chance of a flare-up. A heavy blow (a tenth of your health) counts as five seconds.

Meditation alone, start to finish:<br>**Shadow Panther**: 2 hours<br>**S-class wills**: 3 hours<br>**Tainted wills**: 4 hours (Guardian, Lion Mutant)<br>**A dragon's**: 6 hours
