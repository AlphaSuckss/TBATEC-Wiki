One <span class="thing">Strain</span> bar for every phase, beside your mana orb. It fills while a phase is up, faster in deeper phases and at low Mastery, and with every blow you take (half, three quarters or all of the share of your health lost). With no phase up it falls 4% a second after 3 s (10% while you meditate). The bar pulses from 75%; from 90% it flashes red with a heartbeat.

## Overload

At 100% the will <span class="thing">overloads</span>: the phase ends, you are exhausted (Slowness, Weakness, Mining Fatigue and Hunger II), and <span class="thing">Will Exhaustion</span> locks out spells and phases.<br>**Acquire**: no damage; 20 s exhausted; 10 s locked<br>**Integrate**: 25-15% of your health, never below 1; 40 s; 25 s<br>**Third**: 45% of your health: it CAN kill; blind, sick and backlashed; 90 s; 60 s<br>Strain stays full through the lockout; each phase that was up rests twice as long. End a phase before 100% and none of this happens.

## Lost Control

Can a will overpower its user? In the novel, yes: a dormant one can destroy the body, a final struggle fought Tessia for hers, Integrate pushes a sense of superiority and an urge to destroy, and in the third phase Arthur handed his body to the will and it burned him. Here that is lost control (the seizures, the third phase's hold, the overload), never possession: you always play yourself.
