## A Tainted Will

The Guardian of the Dire Tombs always carries it, and always leaves it. The Vritra's taint makes it heavy: four hours of meditation, its Strain fills 15% faster, and in its final struggle every third flare-up is a <span class="thing">seizure</span>: black thorns burst from you for 4 s, rooting you and tearing at everything within 2 blocks (you too, though it cannot kill you).

It was once a worn relic; its Emerald Vines and the Regeneration it lent are its Acquire now.
