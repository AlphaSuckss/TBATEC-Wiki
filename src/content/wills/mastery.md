Each phase has its own <span class="thing">Mastery</span>, I to V: higher Mastery costs less mana, strains slower, lasts longer, rests less and makes the will's abilities stronger.

Points come from controlled use: 1 a second the phase is up in combat (2 in the third phase) and 2 for a landed signature hit (at most every 2 s). Idling out of combat earns nothing. Points are banked only when the phase ends cleanly, +25% if Strain was at 70 or more (control); an overload banks a quarter.

## Points

**Acquire, Integrate**: II 300, III 1,200, IV 3,000, V 6,600<br>**Third**: II 150, III 600, IV 1,500, V 3,300

About two hours of fighting per phase to Mastery V.

&#8203;<span class="thing">W</span>, the will's blow, is your own attack damage or the will's floor at that Mastery, whichever is more: 8, 11, 14, 17, 20 (a dragon's 12 to 28). A sword-swinger hits with their own arm; a conjurer gets the floor. Will abilities never read your core's colour.
