## Fade

Any Shadow Panther may carry it: about one in seventeen, and every Elder in the Glades' heart. It is the one will below S class. A light will, easy to carry: two hours of meditation, its phases cheaper and slower to strain.

## The Look

Your skin goes matte black with a violet sheen, your eyes black with yellow irises; long black hair, small feline ears and a tail. Black smoke wisps off you, and a shadow streaks behind you when you sprint. Speed bonuses never take you past +100% together with Body Augmentation.
