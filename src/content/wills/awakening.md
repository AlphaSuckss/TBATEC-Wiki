At 100% the will is ready, and waits for a safe moment: 8 seconds calm on the ground, out of combat and out of water, or at once when you meditate.

Three heartbeats, quicker each time, while the will's colour gathers into your chest; then the mana bursts out of you. Everything within 6 blocks is thrown back, friends too, and missiles fly off; then you black out: blind for 5 to 12 seconds (longer for heavier wills), slow and weak, but hard to hurt.

## Awake

You wake a <span class="thing">beast tamer</span>: Acquire is yours at Mastery I (advancement Beast Tamer).

&#8203;<span class="thing">Cultivation surge</span>: for as many seconds of meditation as stabilizing took (at most two hours), meditation refines your core half again as fast. In the novel Arthur's cultivation sped up greatly once his will was assimilated.
