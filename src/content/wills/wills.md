A strong mana beast carries its <span class="thing">will</span> in a small part of its core. In the novels only a beast of A class or higher can pass it on, to one person; most beasts pass it to their young, most destroy it as they die, and a human, elf or dwarf rarely gets one, from the beast or from its core.

A will is not a [bond](/companions/contracts): a bond gives you a companion and no powers; a will makes you a <span class="thing">beast tamer</span>, with the beast's own abilities.

## Tamers

A <span class="thing">forged</span> tamer takes the will from a slain beast's core, as Virion did; a <span class="thing">legacy</span> tamer was given it willingly, as Arthur was by Sylvia. Insight comes harder to the forged.

A will awakens in <span class="thing">phases</span>: Acquire borrows a part of the beast's ability, Integrate merges the body with the will, and only the strongest go deeper. Its progress is its own: your core's colour sets only how much mana you have to hold a phase.

## The Road

A will is taken in dormant. It fights you while you [assimilate](/wills/assimilation) it, [awakens](/wills/awakening) when you have, and then its [phases](/wills/phases) grow with [Mastery](/wills/mastery) and open with [Insight](/wills/insight). Watch your [Strain](/wills/strain).

The mana core screen (the Spell Workshop's will button) and the character screen (<kbd title="Character Screen">I</kbd>) show it all.
